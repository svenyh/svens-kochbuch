Spaghetti carbonara

4 people · Italian inspired cuisine/pasta

Spaghetti Carbonara relies on just a few ingredients and a creamy, harmonious result. If the timing and heat are right, the result is a simple but very strong pasta dish with lots of character.

Ingredients

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 180 g pancetta or bacon cubes
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 1 whole egg
  • 90 g Parmesan or Pecorino, finely grated
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper, freshly ground
  • Salt

Instructions

  1. Cook spaghetti in plenty of salted water until al dente.
  2. Slowly fry the pancetta or bacon until crispy in a large pan without any additional fat.
  3. Mix the egg yolk, egg, grated cheese and black pepper thoroughly in a bowl.
  4. Reserve some pasta water. Drain the spaghetti and add it directly to the pan with the bacon.
  5. Remove pan from heat. Add the egg and cheese mixture and fold in quickly.
  6. Stir in a little hot pasta water as needed until a creamy sauce forms.
  7. Serve immediately and finish with some extra cheese and pepper.
