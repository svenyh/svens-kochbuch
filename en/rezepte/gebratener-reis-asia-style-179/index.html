Asian wok

Fried rice Asian style

4 people · Asian wok / wok dishes

Asian-style fried rice is an ideal way to use up leftovers and at the same time a very good wok dish in its own right. Cold rice, egg, vegetables and a spicy sauce combine to create a light, aromatic dish that is very suitable for everyday use.

Timing

  • Cook rice and cool: approx. 25 minutes plus cooling time
  • Preparation remaining ingredients: approx. 15 minutes
  • Wok preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Total active: approx. 25 minutes, excluding rice pre-cooking time

Ingredients

  • 300 g jasmine rice
  • 2 eggs
  • 200 g peas, frozen
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 red pepper
  • 4 spring onions
  • 150g cooked ham or cooked chicken, optional
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 piece of ginger, approx. 15 g
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • For the sauce: 4 tablespoons soy sauce
  • For the sauce: 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • For the sauce: 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • For the sauce: 1 teaspoon sugar
  • For the sauce: some white pepper

Instructions

  1. It's best to cook rice a few hours before or the day before and let it cool completely.
  2. Dice carrots and peppers small. Cut spring onions into rings. Finely chop the garlic and ginger.
  3. Mix the soy sauce, sesame oil, rice vinegar, sugar and pepper together.
  4. Heat the wok to high heat. Add 1 tablespoon of oil and beat in the eggs. Let it set briefly and then stir roughly. Take out.
  5. Add remaining oil to the wok. Fry garlic, ginger, carrots and peppers for 3 minutes.
  6. Add peas and optionally ham or chicken.
  7. Add rice to wok and stir-fry over high heat until fluffy and hot.
  8. Pour in the sauce, add the eggs and mix everything well.
  9. Fold in the spring onions last and serve immediately.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

Cold rice from the day before works best.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Add peas and optionally ham or chicken.
