Strawberry Basil Lemonade

approx. 4–6 glasses · Summer drinks, lemonades & mocktails

A fruity strawberry lemonade with fresh basil – refreshing, summery, and quick to make in the Thermomix.

Timing

  • Prep time: 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g strawberries
  • juice of 2 lemons
  • 700 ml sparkling mineral water
  • 2–3 tbsp honey or agave syrup
  • 8–10 basil leaves
  • ice cubes

Instructions

  1. Add strawberries, lemon juice, and honey to the mixing bowl and blend 20 Sek. / Stufe 8.
  2. Add the sparkling mineral water.
  3. Gently stir in the basil.
  4. Serve with ice.
