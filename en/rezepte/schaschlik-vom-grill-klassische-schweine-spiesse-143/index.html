Grilled shish kebab – classic pork skewers

4 people · Grill / BBQ / Eastern Europe

Classic grilled shish kebab not only depends on the meat, but also on the order on the skewer, the seasoning and a calm, even heat. This is exactly how you get that typically hearty, slightly Eastern European style that works best outside on the grill.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 25 minutes
  • Marinate: at least 2 hours
  • Grilling time: approx. 18-22 minutes over medium direct to indirect heat
  • Rest time: 3 minutes
  • Total without marinade: approx. 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork neck or shoulder
  • 3 peppers
  • 3 large onions
  • 4 tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp sweet paprika powder
  • 1 teaspoon rose-hot paprika powder, optional
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garlic granules or 1 fresh garlic clove
  • Wooden or metal skewers

Instructions

  1. Cut the pork into even, not too small cubes to keep it juicy.
  2. Cut the peppers into large pieces and the onions into thick segments.
  3. Mix a simple marinade with oil, paprika powder, salt, pepper and garlic.
  4. Mix the meat thoroughly and let it sit for at least 2 hours, preferably longer.
  5. Place the meat, onions and peppers alternately on skewers, making sure not to place them too tightly together.
  6. Cook on the grill over medium direct to indirect heat and turn regularly.
  7. Finally, grill more vigorously to create roasted aromas without burning the vegetables.
  8. Serve directly with bread, paprika sauce or a fresh salad.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Cut the pork into even, not too small cubes to keep it juicy.

Can I substitute ingredients?

It's best to work with a quiet, medium heat instead of too much full throttle.
