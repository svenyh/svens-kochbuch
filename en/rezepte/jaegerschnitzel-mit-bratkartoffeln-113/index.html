Jägerschnitzel with fried potatoes

4 people · Classics & home cooking

Jägerschnitzel is exactly one of those classics where you immediately know what you're getting – and that's the good thing about it. Juicy meat, strong mushroom sauce and crispy fried potatoes – you can hardly get more home-style cooking.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Fry schnitzels: approx. 8 minutes
  • Fried potatoes: approx. 12 to 15 minutes
  • Sauce: approx. 10 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 40 to 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 schnitzels
  • 250 g mushrooms
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 200 ml cream
  • 200 ml broth
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 1 kg pre-cooked potatoes
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Season schnitzels with salt and pepper. Heat oil and butter in a large pan and fry for 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden brown. Keep warm.
  2. Slice pre-cooked potatoes. Fry in a second pan with a little oil over medium heat for 12 to 15 minutes until crispy, turning occasionally.
  3. For the sauce, sauté onion and sliced mushrooms in butter for 4 to 5 minutes.
  4. Deglaze with broth and cream, stir in mustard and simmer for 5 to 8 minutes until the sauce is creamy.
  5. Season with salt, pepper and fresh parsley.
  6. Plate schnitzels with mushroom sauce and fried potatoes and serve immediately.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

Pre-cooked potatoes from the day before become especially crispy.

How long does it keep?
