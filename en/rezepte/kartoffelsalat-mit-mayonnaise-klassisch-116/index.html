Classic potato salad with mayonnaise

4 people · Salads / classics

This potato salad is creamy, flavorful and just the kind of classic that works at parties, barbecues or just because. With mayonnaise, gherkins and a little onion, it tastes the way we know it from many families.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Brewing time: approx. 60 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 80 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 kg waxy potatoes
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 3 pickles
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 2 tbsp cucumber water
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Boil the potatoes, peel them and cut them into slices.
  2. Hard-boil eggs and dice them. Finely chop the gherkins and onion.
  3. Mix mayonnaise with mustard and cucumber water.
  4. Mix everything carefully and season with salt and pepper.
  5. Let it steep for at least 1 hour.
