Goulash Grandma's style

4 people · Oven dishes / classics

A good goulash, Grandma's style, depends primarily on time, peace and a strong sauce. That's exactly what makes this dish so strong: it's deep in flavor, nice and hearty and always a piece of honest, comfort food.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Braising time: approx. 1.5-2 hours
  • In the oven: 150–160 °C top/bottom heat
  • Total time: approx. 2 hours 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef goulash
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tbsp clarified butter
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 500 ml beef stock
  • 200 ml red wine
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Fry the meat vigorously in clarified butter in batches.
  2. Add the onions, sauté and briefly roast the tomato paste.
  3. Deglaze with red wine and stock, season and bring everything to the boil.
  4. Close the pot or roaster and braise the goulash either on the stove at low heat or in the oven at 150-160 °C top/bottom heat.
  5. Cook for about 1.5-2 hours until the meat is tender.
  6. Season the sauce and let it simmer a little, uncovered, if desired.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with pasta, potatoes or dumplings.
