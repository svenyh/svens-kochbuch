Grilled shish kebab – chicken with peppers

4 people · Grill / BBQ / Eastern Europe

Chicken shish kebab is a slightly lighter version, but needs just as clear seasoning and precise timing. If the meat is allowed to marinate briefly beforehand and the peppers just roast instead of burning, it will be really strong.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Marinate: 1-2 hours
  • Grilling time: approx. 15-18 minutes over medium heat
  • Rest time: 3 minutes
  • Total without marinade: approx. 35-40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 kg boneless chicken breast or chicken thighs
  • 3 peppers
  • 2-3 onions
  • 4 tbsp oil
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder
  • 1 teaspoon curry or chicken seasoning, optional
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1 clove of garlic or 1 teaspoon of garlic granules
  • 1 tsp lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Cut the chicken into even pieces and marinate with oil, spices, garlic and a little lemon juice.
  2. Cut the peppers into large cubes and the onions into segments.
  3. Let the meat sit cold for at least 1 to 2 hours.
  4. Thread everything loosely and alternately onto skewers.
  5. Cook on the grill over medium heat, turning several times.
  6. Make sure the chicken is completely cooked through but not dry.
  7. Finally, briefly color over direct heat.
  8. Serve with yogurt dip, bread or rice salad.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with yogurt dip, bread or rice salad.
