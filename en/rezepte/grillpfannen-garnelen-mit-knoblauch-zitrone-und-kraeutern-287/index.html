Grill pan shrimp with garlic, lemon and herbs

4 people as a starter or 2 people as a main course · Fisch &amp; Meeresfrüchte / Grill &amp; BBQ

Briefly marinated shrimp with garlic, lemon and herbs from the grill pan or from the plancha. Quickly made and best served straight away.

Timing

  • Marinate: 15-20 minutes | Grilling time: 5-6 minutes | Total time: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2-3 cloves of garlic
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Zest of ½ organic lemon
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder
  • 1 pinch of chili flakes
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • fresh parsley
  • optional: baguette for serving

Instructions

  1. Pat shrimp dry.
  2. Chop garlic finely.
  3. Mix oil, garlic, lemon juice, lemon zest, paprika, chili, salt and pepper.
  4. Mix the shrimp with it and let it steep for about 15-20 minutes.
  5. Heat the grill pan or plancha to high heat.
  6. Grill shrimp for 2-3 minutes per side until pink and lightly browned.
  7. Sprinkle with fresh parsley.
  8. Serve directly with baguette.
