Zucchini casserole with minced meat

4 people · Oven dishes / quick cuisine

An uncomplicated casserole with zucchini, minced meat and tomato sauce. Everyday dish with good oven logic and little effort when serving.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 30-35 minutes at 190°C

Ingredients

  • 2 large zucchini
  • 500g minced meat
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 can chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 150g grated cheese
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Paprika powder
  • oregano

Instructions

  1. Slice the zucchini and finely chop the onion and garlic.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and fry the minced meat vigorously.
  3. Add the onion and garlic, briefly roast the tomato paste and add the chopped tomatoes and spices.
  4. Layer the zucchini and minced mixture in a baking dish.
  5. Sprinkle with cheese and bake in the oven until the zucchini is soft and the cheese is golden brown.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Goes well with baguette or rice.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Also very good with feta instead of normal cheese.
