Green asparagus from the air fryer

2-3 people · Airfryer / asparagus & seasonal cuisine

Green asparagus from the air fryer is ideal if you want something quick and still have something with a roasted aroma on your plate. With little effort, you can create a modern, practical side dish that fits particularly well into everyday life.

Timing

  • Preparation: 10 minutes
  • Airfryer: 8-10 minutes at 180°C
  • Total: approx. 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g green asparagus
  • 1-2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 1/2 lemon
  • optional: 20-30 g Parmesan
  • optional: 1 small clove of garlic

Instructions

  1. Break off or cut off the woody ends of the green asparagus. Grünen Spargel musst du nicht schälen.
  2. Mix the asparagus with olive oil, salt and pepper. Optionally add some finely grated garlic.
  3. Preheat the air fryer to 180 °C if your device supports this.
  4. Place the asparagus loosely in the basket - not too tight so that the hot air can circulate well.
  5. Cook for about 8-10 minutes. Shake or turn briefly once in a while.
  6. Finally, refine with lemon juice and serve with Parmesan if desired.
