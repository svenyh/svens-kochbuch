Warm grilled mushroom salad

4 people · Grill & BBQ / Salads & Side Dishes

A lukewarm salad with roasted mushrooms, onions and spicy dressing. Very good grill side dish with little effort.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Grilling/roasting time: approx. 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g mushrooms
  • 1 red onion
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • optionally some feta

Instructions

  1. Clean the mushrooms and cut them in half depending on their size.
  2. Cut the onion into fine strips.
  3. Mix olive oil, balsamic vinegar, mustard, garlic, salt and pepper to make a dressing.
  4. Roast the mushrooms on the grill or in the grill pan until they start to color.
  5. Place in a bowl with the onion, pour over the dressing and mix well.
  6. Sprinkle with parsley and optionally crumble some feta on top.
