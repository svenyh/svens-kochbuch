Herb curd dip

For 4 people · Basic recipes / dips & sauces

A fresh herb quark dip for baked potatoes, vegetables, bread or grilled dishes. Quick to make and very versatile.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Brewing time: approx. 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 250 g quark
  • 2 tbsp natural yogurt
  • 1 tbsp crème fraîche
  • 2 tbsp chopped herbs, e.g. E.g. parsley, chives, dill
  • 1 small clove of garlic
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • some lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Put the quark, yoghurt and crème fraîche in a bowl.
  2. Finely chop the herbs and add them.
  3. Finely chop or press garlic.
  4. Mix everything together.
  5. Season with salt, pepper and a little lemon juice.
  6. Let it steep briefly and serve.
