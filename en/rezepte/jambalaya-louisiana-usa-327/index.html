Jambalaya – Louisiana/USA

4 people · Culinary World Tour / World Tour

Spicy jambalaya from Louisiana with rice, chorizo, chicken and prawns – Sven's one-pot version for cast-iron pan or grill.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Total: approx. 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 300 g rice
  • 300 g chicken
  • 200 g chorizo or smoked sausage
  • 250 g prawns
  • bell pepper
  • onion
  • celery
  • garlic
  • tomatoes
  • stock
  • Cajun spice

Instructions

  1. Sear the sausage and chicken.
  2. Add the vegetables.
  3. Stir in the rice, tomatoes, stock and spices.
  4. Cook for 20 minutes.
  5. Add the prawns at the end.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

Vegetables and meat can be prepared ahead; add rice and prawns in the final minutes.

How long does it keep?

Leftovers keep in the fridge for 2 days – reheat with a little broth so the rice stays moist.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Andouille or smoked paprika sausage works instead of chorizo.
