Viennese Rice and Meat – Austria

4 people · Culinary World Tour / Europe

Hearty Viennese rice and meat with paprika – Sven's Austrian braise for the whole family. Simple, savoury and wonderfully filling.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Braising time: approx. 45 minutes
  • Total: approx. 60 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600 g pork shoulder or stewing meat
  • 250 g rice
  • 2 onions
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 800 ml stock
  • marjoram
  • salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Sear the meat.
  2. Sauté the onions and garlic.
  3. Stir in the paprika and tomato paste.
  4. Top up with stock and braise for 45 minutes.
  5. Add the rice and cook until tender.
