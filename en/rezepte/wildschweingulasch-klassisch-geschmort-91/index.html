Classically braised wild boar goulash

4 people · Soups & Stews

Wild boar goulash, classically braised, is a hearty braised dish with a deep, round sauce and lots of heat. Ideal for cooler days or a quiet weekend meal when something hearty with more character is required.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Braising time: approx. 2-2.5 hours
  • In the oven: 150–160 °C top/bottom heat
  • Total: approximately 2 hours 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 kg wild boar goulash
  • 2 large onions
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 300 ml dry red wine
  • 500 ml game or beef stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 6 juniper berries
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 2 tbsp oil

Instructions

  1. Pat the meat dry and fry it vigorously in portions in a roasting pan.
  2. Add onions and garlic and fry briefly.
  3. Stir in the tomato paste and fry lightly for 1-2 minutes.
  4. Deglaze with red wine and let it boil down a little.
  5. Add the stock and spices, bring to the boil and close the roaster.
  6. Either braise gently on the stove over a low heat or cook in the oven at 150-160 °C top/bottom heat.
  7. Simmer the goulash for 2 to 2.5 hours until the meat is soft, adding a little liquid occasionally if necessary.
  8. Finally, season with salt and pepper and, if necessary, let the sauce simmer a little more uncovered.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

It is important to use gentle heat instead of high boiling so that the meat remains tender.
