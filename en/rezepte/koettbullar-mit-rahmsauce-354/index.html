Köttbullar with cream sauce

4 people · Meat dishes / Pan dishes

Köttbullar are among Scandinavia's best-known dishes. The small meatballs served with a creamy sauce are true comfort food.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 25 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 25 minutes
  • Total: approx. 50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600 g mixed minced meat
  • 1 egg
  • 1 small onion
  • 50 g breadcrumbs
  • 100 ml milk
  • salt
  • pepper
  • butter
  • For the sauce: 30 g butter
  • For the sauce: 30 g flour
  • For the sauce: 300 ml beef stock
  • For the sauce: 200 ml cream

Instructions

  1. Soak the breadcrumbs in milk.
  2. Mix with the mince, egg and onion.
  3. Shape into small balls.
  4. Fry golden in butter.
  5. For the sauce, melt the butter.
  6. Stir in the flour.
  7. Add stock and cream.
  8. Let the meatballs simmer in the sauce.
