Herb butter for pizza and bread

For 1 small bowl · Basic recipes / dips & sauces

A simple herb butter for pizza crusts, garlic bread, baguettes or barbecues. Quick to make and versatile.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 8 minutes
  • Cooling time optional: approx. 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 125g soft butter
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 tsp chopped chives
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 1 tsp lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Put the butter in a bowl.
  2. Finely chop or press garlic.
  3. Add herbs, garlic and lemon juice to the butter.
  4. Mix everything well.
  5. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Cool briefly or serve immediately.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Stores well in rolls wrapped in cling film.
