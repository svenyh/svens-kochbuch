Fanta cake classic

For 1 tray · Baking & Sweets / Sheet Cakes

Airy sheet cake with cream and sour cream topping and cinnamon sugar. A real party cake classic.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 20-25 minutes at 180°C
  • Cooling time: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 300g flour
  • 200g sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 125 ml oil
  • 125ml Fanta
  • 1 packet of baking powder
  • 400 g whipped cream
  • 2 packets of cream stiffener
  • 200 g sour cream
  • some sugar
  • Cinnamon sugar for sprinkling

Instructions

  1. Beat eggs and sugar until fluffy.
  2. Stir in oil and Fanta.
  3. Fold in flour and baking powder.
  4. Place the dough on a tray and bake.
  5. Allow to cool.
  6. Beat the cream until stiff and mix with the sour cream.
  7. Spread on the cake.
  8. Sprinkle with cinnamon sugar and serve cold.
