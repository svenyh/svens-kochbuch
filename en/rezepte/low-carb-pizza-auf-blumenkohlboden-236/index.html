Pizza

Low-carb pizza on a cauliflower base

2 people · Pizza / Fast food

An alternative pizza variant with a cauliflower base. Not a replacement for classic pizza, but a good light alternative.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Pre-bake: approx. 15 minutes
  • Finish baking: approx. 10-12 minutes at 220°C

Ingredients

  • 1 small cauliflower
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g grated cheese
  • 2 tbsp parmesan
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 150 g pureed tomatoes
  • 125g mozzarella
  • Topping as desired, e.g. E.g. peppers, ham or mushrooms

Instructions

  1. Divide the cauliflower into small pieces and grate or mix finely.
  2. Cook or steam briefly and then squeeze well.
  3. Mix with eggs, cheese, parmesan, salt and pepper.
  4. Form the mixture flat into a base on baking paper and pre-bake.
  5. Spread with pureed tomatoes, add cheese and toppings and bake until everything is golden brown.
