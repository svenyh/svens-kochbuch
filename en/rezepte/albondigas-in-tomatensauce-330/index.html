Albóndigas in tomato sauce

4 people · Pan dishes / Meat dishes

Albóndigas are among Spain's most popular tapas. Juicy meatballs in an aromatic tomato sauce make an uncomplicated dish for family or guests.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 30 minutes
  • Total: approx. 50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600 g mixed minced meat
  • 1 egg
  • 50 g breadcrumbs
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • salt
  • pepper
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion (sauce)
  • 2 garlic cloves (sauce)
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 150 ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp sugar

Instructions

  1. Mix the minced meat with egg, breadcrumbs, small onion and spices.
  2. Shape into small balls.
  3. Brown all over in olive oil and set aside.
  4. For the sauce, sauté the onion and garlic.
  5. Add the tomato paste.
  6. Stir in tomatoes and stock, season with sugar.
  7. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  8. Return the meatballs.
  9. Braise for another 15 minutes.
