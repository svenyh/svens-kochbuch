Pulled pork pasta bake

4 people · Oven dishes

A hearty pasta casserole with pulled pork that makes great use of leftovers. Creamy sauce, spicy filling and gratinated cheese make it an uncomplicated family dish.

Ingredients

  • 300 g short pasta, e.g. B. Fusilli
  • 500 g cooked pulled pork
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 onion
  • 150g corn
  • 200 ml cream
  • 250 ml pureed tomatoes
  • 120 ml BBQ sauce
  • 150g grated Gouda
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta in plenty of salted water until just al dente, drain and set aside.
  2. Dice the onion, cut the pepper into small pieces. Heat olive oil in a pan and fry onion and peppers for 4 minutes.
  3. Add pulled pork and corn, heat briefly and season with paprika powder, salt and pepper.
  4. Mix the cream, pureed tomatoes and BBQ sauce, add to the pan and simmer gently for 3 minutes.
  5. Fold in the pasta and put everything in a greased baking dish.
  6. Sprinkle with grated Gouda and bake at 190 °C top/bottom heat for 20 to 25 minutes until the cheese is golden yellow.
