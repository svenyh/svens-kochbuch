Vegetable pan “leftover kitchen”

2-3 people · Fast cuisine

The “leftover kitchen” vegetable pan shows how simple supplies can be turned into a harmonious meal. Flexible, practical and still delicious – exactly the kind of recipe that really helps in everyday life.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Roasting time: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • Approx. 700 g mixed vegetables as available, e.g. E.g. peppers, zucchini, carrots, mushrooms, onions
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • Salt, pepper
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder or Italian herbs
  • optional: some feta, cheese or rice from the day before

Instructions

  1. Cut vegetables into similar sizes depending on the variety.
  2. Heat oil in a large pan and first fry the firmer varieties such as carrots, potatoes or broccoli.
  3. Add softer vegetables and garlic and continue frying everything together.
  4. Season with salt, pepper and suitable spices and cook until al dente.
  5. Top off with a little broth, ajvar or crème fraîche if desired.
  6. Add feta, cheese, egg, rice or bread if you want it to be more filling.
