Pasta salad with tuna and corn

4 people · Salads / classics

This pasta salad is particularly practical when you want something quick and still have something hearty on the table. Tuna, corn and a creamy dressing make it a typical all-rounder.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cook and cool pasta: about 10 minutes
  • Brewing time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 35-40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 400 g short noodles
  • 2 cans of tuna
  • 1 can of corn
  • 1 pepper
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 100g yogurt
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta in salted water, drain and let cool completely.
  2. Drain the tuna and corn well and dice the peppers.
  3. Mix mayonnaise, yogurt, mustard, salt and pepper into a creamy dressing.
  4. Mix all ingredients thoroughly and season to taste.
  5. Ideally let the salad sit for 20 minutes before serving.
  6. Add spring onions or parsley if desired.
