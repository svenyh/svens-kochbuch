Tagliatelle with mushroom cream sauce

4 people · Italian inspired cuisine/pasta

Tagliatelle with mushroom cream sauce is a creamy, feel-good dish with a calm, round taste. Especially on cooler days, this recipe is a nice classic between everyday life and a small moment of enjoyment.

Ingredients

  • 350g tagliatelle
  • 400 g mushrooms or brown mushrooms
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 200 ml cream
  • 100 ml vegetable broth
  • 40g Parmesan
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Clean and slice the mushrooms. Finely dice the onion and garlic.
  2. Cook tagliatelle in salted water until al dente.
  3. Heat butter in a large skillet. Fry the mushrooms vigorously until they color.
  4. Add onion and garlic and fry for 2 minutes.
  5. Deglaze with broth and cream. Let it boil down slightly.
  6. Stir in Parmesan and parsley and season with salt and pepper.
  7. Drain the tagliatelle and toss it directly in the sauce.
