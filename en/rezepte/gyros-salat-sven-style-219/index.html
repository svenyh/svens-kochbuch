Gyros salad Sven style

4 people · Salads & Side Dishes / Fast Food

A hearty, party-friendly salad with gyro meat, fresh vegetables and creamy tzatziki. Perfect for barbecues or guests.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Roasting time: approx. 10 minutes
  • Brewing time optional: approx. 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken or pork slices
  • 2 tbsp gyro seasoning
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 1 cucumber
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 can of corn
  • 150g grated cheese
  • 200 g tzatziki or garlic yogurt dressing

Instructions

  1. Marinate the meat with gyro seasoning and oil.
  2. Fry vigorously in a pan and then let cool.
  3. Cut iceberg lettuce into strips. Cut the cucumber, tomatoes and onion into small pieces. Drain the corn.
  4. Put everything in a large bowl or in layers in a mold.
  5. Spread the fried meat on top and sprinkle with cheese.
  6. Finally serve with tzatziki or garlic dressing.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Add the dressing just before serving to keep the salad fresh.
