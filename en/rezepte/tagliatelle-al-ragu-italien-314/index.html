Tagliatelle al Ragù – Italy

4 people · Culinary World Tour / Europe

Slow-simmered ragù with tagliatelle and Parmesan – Sven's Italian home cooking. The longer the sauce cooks, the deeper the flavour.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 90 minutes
  • Total: approx. 110 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g tagliatelle
  • 500 g mixed minced meat or beef
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 celery stalk
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 400 g tomatoes
  • 150 ml red wine
  • 200 ml stock
  • Parmesan
  • olive oil
  • salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Finely dice the vegetables and sauté.
  2. Sear the meat well.
  3. Roast the tomato paste and deglaze with red wine.
  4. Add the tomatoes and stock.
  5. Simmer for at least 90 minutes.
  6. Serve with tagliatelle and Parmesan.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

In Bologna ragù is classically served with tagliatelle, not spaghetti.
