Grilled halloumi with herbs and olive oil

2-3 people · Grill & BBQ / Side Dishes

Grilled halloumi is a strong side dish when you want something salty, warm and quick to come off the grill. With herbs and olive oil, it becomes more than just grilled cheese - a real small plate in itself.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 5 minutes
  • Grilling time: approx. 4-6 minutes over medium direct heat
  • Total: approx. 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 250g halloumi
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 small garlic clove, finely grated
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano or thyme
  • freshly ground pepper
  • optional: some honey for a mild sweet and salty version

Instructions

  1. Cut the halloumi into thicker slices and pat dry.
  2. Brush with a little olive oil, herbs and a little pepper; Salt is usually not necessary.
  3. Preheat grill to medium direct heat or heat a grill pan.
  4. Grill the halloumi for 2 to 3 minutes per side until clear grill marks appear.
  5. Turn only once so that the slices remain stable.
  6. Serve immediately with a little lemon juice or herb oil.
