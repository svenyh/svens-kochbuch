Carlsbad slices

4 people · Classic recipes / snacks & snacks

Carlsbad slices are one of the typical hearty snacks that are quick to make and taste like snacks, dinner and childhood memories. Baked, spicy and uncomplicated – that’s exactly where its charm lies.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 12-15 minutes at 200 °C top/bottom heat
  • Short standing time: approx. 2 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 8 slices of toast or mixed bread
  • 200 g hunting sausage or cooked ham in small cubes
  • 150g grated cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • 100 ml milk
  • 2 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder
  • some butter for the tray

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C top/bottom heat and lightly butter a tray or baking dish.
  2. Mix eggs with milk, ketchup, mustard, diced onions, paprika powder, salt and pepper.
  3. Fold in the hunting sausage or ham and the grated cheese until you get a spreadable, flavorful mixture.
  4. Place the bread slices on the tray and spread the mixture evenly over them.
  5. Bake in the middle of the oven for 12 to 15 minutes, until the slices are hot and golden on top.
  6. Let cool briefly for 2 minutes and serve warm.
