Grilled fruits with pineapple or peach

3-4 people · Grill & BBQ / Dessert

Grilled fruits are uncomplicated proof that a grilled dessert can also work really well. Pineapple or peach get sweetness, roasted aroma and exactly that summery direction that fits perfectly after a barbecue evening.

Timing

  • Preparation: 10 minutes
  • Grill time: 5-6 minutes over medium heat
  • Serve straight away: ideally warm
  • Total: approx. 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1/2 fresh pineapple or 4 peaches
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp butter or 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • optional: 1 scoop of vanilla ice cream or yogurt for serving

Instructions

  1. Cut pineapple into slices or pieces, halve and stone peaches.
  2. Melt butter and mix with brown sugar and lemon juice.
  3. Lightly brush the fruit with it and preheat the grill to medium heat.
  4. Grill the pineapple for 2 to 3 minutes per side until traces of roasting appear.
  5. Grill the peaches a little more slowly over medium heat so that they become soft but do not fall apart.
  6. Add a touch of cinnamon or vanilla if desired.
  7. Serve warm with vanilla ice cream, yoghurt or plain.
