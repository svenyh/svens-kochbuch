Brussels sprouts curry with chickpeas

4 people · Pan-fried dishes / quick cuisine

No Thai red curry from the wok, but a vegetarian stir-fry dish with Brussels sprouts and chickpeas: mildly spicy, creamy and suitable for everyday use. Coconut milk and curry powder round out the pot – naan, flatbread or rice go well with it.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 12 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Total: approx. 32 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g Brussels sprouts
  • 1 can of chickpeas (400 g), drained
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 200 ml pureed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: 1 teaspoon ginger, grated
  • optional: coriander greens
  • Naan, flatbread or rice for serving

Instructions

  1. Clean the Brussels sprouts, remove large leaves and halve the florets.
  2. Finely dice the onion and garlic.
  3. Heat oil in a large pan and sauté onion for 3-4 minutes.
  4. Briefly roast the garlic, ginger, curry powder and cumin.
  5. Add Brussels sprouts and fry for 5 minutes.
  6. Pour in the pureed tomatoes and coconut milk.
  7. Add the chickpeas and simmer for 12-15 minutes until the Brussels sprouts are tender and tender.
  8. Season with salt, pepper and coriander and serve.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

Vegetables and chickpeas can be prepared ahead; finish the curry when you are ready to eat.

How long does it keep?

Leftovers keep in the fridge for 2–3 days and often taste better reheated.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Cream works instead of coconut milk for a milder, creamier version.
