Risotto with chicken and vegetables

4 people · Italian inspired cuisine/rice dishes

A creamy risotto for every day that tastes really good without much effort. Chicken and vegetables make this a warm, balanced main dish with a pleasant feel-good factor.

Ingredients

  • 300 g risotto rice
  • 450 g chicken breast fillet
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 small zucchini
  • 100 g frozen peas
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 900 ml hot chicken stock
  • 80 ml white wine or 50 ml extra broth
  • 40g Parmesan
  • 20g butter
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut chicken into small cubes. Finely dice the onion, garlic, carrot and zucchini.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a pot or deep pan and fry the chicken until golden brown. Salt lightly and remove.
  3. Add remaining olive oil. Sauté the onion and garlic until translucent, then fry the carrot and zucchini for 2 minutes.
  4. Add risotto rice and roast for 1 minute.
  5. Deglaze with white wine or a little broth and allow to absorb almost completely.
  6. Gradually add the hot broth, one ladle at a time, stirring regularly.
  7. After about 15 minutes, add the peas and the browned chicken.
  8. Add more broth until the rice is creamy but still slightly al dente. This takes about 20 to 25 minutes in total.
  9. Stir in Parmesan and butter. Season with salt and pepper and serve immediately.
