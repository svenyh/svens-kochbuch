Potato minced gratin with a twist

4 people · Potato dishes

This minced potato gratin stays down-to-earth but gets a little modern twist through the seasoning. A nice family dish when you want something familiar with a twist on the table – creamy, hearty and from the oven.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Pre-cook potatoes: approx. 8 minutes
  • Minced meat pan: approx. 15 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 25 to 30 minutes
  • Total: approx. 50 to 55 minutes

Ingredients

  • 800g potatoes
  • 500 g minced beef
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 150 ml cream
  • 150 ml broth
  • 120g grated cheese
  • 1 teaspoon mild curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Peel the potatoes, cut into thin slices and pre-boil in salted water for 8 minutes. Drain and let drip briefly.
  2. Dice onion, carrot and pepper small. Heat oil in a pan and fry the minced meat until crumbly.
  3. Add vegetables and fry for 4 to 5 minutes. Stir in tomato paste, curry powder and paprika powder and toast for 1 minute.
  4. Pour in cream and broth, season with salt and pepper and simmer gently for 5 minutes.
  5. Layer potatoes and minced mixture alternately in a baking dish. Sprinkle with cheese.
  6. Bake at 190 °C top/bottom heat for 25 to 30 minutes until golden brown. Rest for 5 minutes and serve warm.
