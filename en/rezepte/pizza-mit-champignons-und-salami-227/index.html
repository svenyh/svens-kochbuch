Pizza

Pizza with mushrooms and salami

2 people · Pizza / classics & home cooking

A classic, safe pizza variant with salami, mushrooms and onions - uncomplicated and always popular.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 10-12 minutes at 250°C

Ingredients

  • 1 portion of classic pizza dough
  • 200 g pureed tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • oregano
  • 150 g grated mozzarella
  • 100g salami
  • 150 g mushrooms
  • 1 small onion

Instructions

  1. Prepare and roll out the pizza dough.
  2. Mix the pureed tomatoes with tomato paste, salt, pepper and oregano and spread over the dough.
  3. Sprinkle with cheese.
  4. Spread the salami, mushrooms and finely chopped onion on top.
  5. Bake in a hot oven until the edges and cheese are nicely browned.
