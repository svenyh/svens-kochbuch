Pea soup with sausages

4-6 people · Soups / classics

Pea soup is exactly the kind of pot dish that brings instant warmth and satiety. Thick, hearty and served with sausages, it is a real classic as we know it from the past.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 45-60 minutes
  • Sausages at the end: 5 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 75 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g split peas
  • 1 onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 piece of celery
  • 1 leek stalk
  • 1.5 l broth
  • 4 sausages
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • marjoram

Instructions

  1. Cut vegetables into small pieces and sauté briefly in a little oil or bacon fat.
  2. Add peas, add broth and bring to the boil.
  3. Simmer the soup gently for 45 to 60 minutes, until the peas are tender.
  4. If desired, puree some or leave it chunky.
  5. Cut the sausages into slices or pieces and let them sit hot for the last 5 minutes.
  6. Season with marjoram, salt and pepper and let rest briefly.
