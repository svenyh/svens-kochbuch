Smash cheeseburger with melted cheese

4 burgers · Grill &amp; BBQ

Smash cheeseburger with melted cheese, strong crust and classic burger sauce. Juicy, direct and without unnecessary frills.

Timing

  • Preparation: 15 minutes | Grilling: 10 minutes | Total time: 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600 g ground beef
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • some oil
  • 4 brioche buns
  • 4-8 slices of cheddar or processed cheese
  • 1 red onion, finely diced or in rings
  • Sliced ​​pickled cucumbers
  • Iceberg lettuce or romaine lettuce
  • Burger sauce
  • optional: jalapeños
  • 5 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder
  • 1 tsp cucumber water
  • 1 small gherkin, finely chopped
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Mix the sauce from mayonnaise, ketchup, mustard, paprika, cucumber water, cucumber and pepper.
  2. Divide the mince into 4 loose balls.
  3. Let the grill plate or pan get very hot.
  4. Roast the buns on the cut surface.
  5. Place the minced meat balls on the hot surface and flatten them vigorously.
  6. Salt and pepper.
  7. Turn after about 1.5-2 minutes.
  8. Immediately place the cheese on the hot side and let it melt briefly.
  9. If necessary, cover with a small hood or lid to help the cheese melt more quickly.
  10. Assemble burger with sauce, lettuce, patty, pickles and onions.
