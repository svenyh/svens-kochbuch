Bouillabaisse with Mussels & Prawns – Southern France

4 people · Culinary World Tour / Mediterranean & North Africa

Provençal seafood soup without fish fillets – Sven's version with mussels and prawns. Aromatic, Mediterranean and perfect with baguette.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 30 minutes
  • Total: approx. 50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 kg mussels
  • 300 g prawns
  • 1 fennel bulb
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 400 g tomatoes
  • 150 ml white wine
  • fish or vegetable stock
  • saffron
  • orange zest
  • parsley

Instructions

  1. Sauté the fennel, onion and garlic.
  2. Add the tomatoes, wine, stock and spices.
  3. Simmer for 25 minutes.
  4. Steam the mussels and prawns in the broth.
