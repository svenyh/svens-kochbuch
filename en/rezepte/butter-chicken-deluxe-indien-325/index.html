Butter Chicken Deluxe – India

4 people · Culinary World Tour / World Tour

Creamy butter chicken in classic Indian style – inspired by Sven's butter chicken wok, here as a silky pan version. Mild, aromatic and very popular.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes (incl. marinating)
  • Cooking time: approx. 30 minutes
  • Total: approx. 50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 700 g chicken
  • yoghurt
  • garam masala
  • paprika
  • cumin
  • 400 g tomatoes
  • 200 ml cream or coconut milk
  • butter
  • garlic
  • ginger

Instructions

  1. Marinate the chicken.
  2. Sear briefly.
  3. Cook the tomato and spice sauce.
  4. Gently cook the chicken in the sauce.
  5. Finish with butter and cream.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Marinated chicken keeps the meat especially juicy.
