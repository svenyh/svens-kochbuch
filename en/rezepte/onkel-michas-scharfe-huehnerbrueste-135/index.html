Uncle Micha's spicy chicken breasts

2 people · Oven dishes / chicken / everyday cooking

A simple, spicy oven dish with marinated chicken breasts, colorful pepper mix and a golden cheese crust. The soy sauce marinade provides lots of flavor, optionally with chili for extra spiciness.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Marinate: approx. 30 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 45 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 55 minutes + marinating time

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 100 ml soy sauce
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 3 peppers (colorfully mixed)
  • 150-200g grated cheese
  • 1 fresh chili or 1/2-1 tsp chili powder (optional)
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Place chicken breasts in a bowl.
  2. Mix soy sauce with finely chopped garlic and pour over the meat.
  3. Let the meat marinate for about 30 minutes.
  4. Cut the peppers into strips or coarse pieces.
  5. Preheat the oven to 180 °C fan oven or 200 °C top/bottom heat.
  6. Place chicken breasts in a baking dish.
  7. Add the remaining marinade to the mold.
  8. Distribute the peppers around the meat.
  9. Optionally add chili.
  10. Cook in the oven for 30 minutes.
  11. Sprinkle cheese on top.
  12. Bake for another 15 minutes until the cheese is golden brown.
