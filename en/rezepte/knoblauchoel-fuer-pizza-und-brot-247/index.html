Garlic oil for pizza and bread

For 1 small glass · Basic recipes / sauces & seasoning base

A quick garlic oil for pizza, focaccia, bruschetta or warm bread. Instantly adds more flavor and is made in just a few minutes.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 5 minutes
  • Brewing time optional: approx. 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 100 ml olive oil
  • 2-3 cloves of garlic
  • 1 small pinch of salt
  • optionally some oregano or chili

Instructions

  1. Chop or press the garlic cloves very finely.
  2. Mix with the olive oil and a small pinch of salt.
  3. Add some oregano or chili if desired.
  4. Let it steep briefly before using and pour sparingly over pizza, focaccia or bread.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Store in the refrigerator and use promptly.
