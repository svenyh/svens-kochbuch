Virgin Mojito

approx. 2 glasses · Summer drinks, lemonades & mocktails

A non-alcoholic virgin mojito – classically fresh with lime, mint, and sparkling mineral water.

Timing

  • Prep time: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • limes
  • mint
  • brown sugar
  • crushed ice
  • sparkling mineral water

Instructions

  1. Lightly muddle the limes.
  2. Add sugar.
  3. Add mint.
  4. Add crushed ice.
  5. Top up with sparkling mineral water.
