Creamy pasta bake with chicken

4 people · Oven dishes

A creamy pasta casserole with chicken that is mild, round and very suitable for everyday use. Just the thing if you want it to be filling without seeming heavy or complicated.

Ingredients

  • 300 g short pasta
  • 500 g chicken breast fillet
  • 1 small onion
  • 150 g peas, frozen
  • 200 ml cream
  • 150 ml milk
  • 100 ml chicken broth
  • 120g grated cheese
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp flour
  • 1 pinch of nutmeg
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta until just al dente. Cut the chicken into pieces and finely dice the onion.
  2. Fry the chicken in a pan, add the onion and let it become translucent.
  3. Melt butter in a small saucepan, stir in flour, then pour in milk, cream and stock. Cook until creamy, 3 to 4 minutes.
  4. Season the sauce with nutmeg, salt and pepper. Stir in peas.
  5. Place the pasta and chicken in a baking dish, spread the sauce over it and sprinkle with cheese.
  6. Bake at 190 °C top/bottom heat for 20 to 25 minutes until the surface is golden yellow.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

A small amount of broccoli or leek also goes well if you want to vary the dish.
