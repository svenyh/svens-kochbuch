Placki ziemniaczane with mushroom cream

4 people · Polish regional cuisine / potato dishes

When classic Placki ziemniaczane meets a creamy mushroom cream, a simple potato dish becomes a truly well-rounded meal. Crispy potato pancakes and creamy sauce complement each other perfectly here.

Timing

  • Prepare and fry Placki: approx. 50 minutes
  • Mushroom cream: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total: approximately 1 hour 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 basic recipe Placki ziemniaczane classic
  • 300 g mushrooms or brown mushrooms
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 150 ml cream
  • 100 ml vegetable or mushroom stock
  • 1 tsp flour
  • 1 tsp chopped parsley
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Prepare Placki ziemniaczane as in the basic recipe and bake in batches.
  2. For the mushroom cream, finely dice the onion and slice the mushrooms.
  3. Heat the butter in a pan, sauté the onions until translucent and then add the mushrooms.
  4. Fry the mushrooms for 5 to 7 minutes until they start to color and there is as little liquid as possible in the pan.
  5. Sprinkle flour over it, stir in briefly and deglaze with stock and cream.
  6. Let the sauce simmer gently for 3 to 5 minutes until it thickens creamy.
  7. Season with salt, pepper and parsley and serve the crispy placki directly with mushroom cream.
