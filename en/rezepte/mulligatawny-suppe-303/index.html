Mulligatawny soup

4 people · Soups & Stews

Mulligatawny is a spicy soup with curry, apple and vegetables - hearty, slightly creamy and surprisingly suitable for everyday use. With or without chicken, depending on what's in the fridge. Not a restaurant recipe, but an honest pot for cool evenings.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 25 minutes
  • Total: approx. 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 300 g chicken breast fillet, optional
  • 1 apple
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 stick of celery
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tbsp curry paste or 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 liter chicken or vegetable broth
  • 200 ml cream or coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp oil or butter
  • 1 tbsp flour
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: 1 teaspoon ginger, grated
  • optional: parsley or coriander

Instructions

  1. Peel and dice vegetables, core apples and cut into cubes.
  2. If using: Cut the chicken into small cubes, fry all over in oil and set aside - it will cook in the soup.
  3. Sauté the onion, carrot and celery in the pot for 4-5 minutes.
  4. Briefly roast the garlic, ginger and curry.
  5. Sprinkle in flour and sauté briefly.
  6. Slowly pour in the broth and let everything simmer for 10 minutes.
  7. Add the apple and optionally the browned chicken and simmer for another 10 minutes until the meat is cooked through.
  8. Stir in cream or coconut milk, season with salt and pepper and serve.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

The soup can be prepared the day before and often tastes even better when reheated.

How long does it keep?

It keeps well in the fridge for 2–3 days.

What side dish goes well?

Fresh bread or naan works well as a side.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Without chicken the Mulligatawny is vegetarian; cream can be replaced with coconut milk.
