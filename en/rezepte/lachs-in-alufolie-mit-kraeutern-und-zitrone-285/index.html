Salmon in aluminum foil with herbs and lemon

2-4 people · Fisch &amp; Meeresfrüchte / Grill &amp; BBQ

Salmon fillets in a foil package with herbs, lemon and a little garlic. Easy to prepare and particularly juicy when served straight from the grill.

Timing

  • Preparation: 10 minutes | Grilling time: 12-15 minutes | Total time: approx. 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 organic lemon
  • 1-2 cloves of garlic, finely chopped
  • fresh parsley or dill
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: some butter
  • optional: spring onions
  • Aluminum foil or grill paper

Instructions

  1. Pat salmon fillets dry.
  2. Prepare a piece of aluminum foil for each.
  3. Place salmon on the foil.
  4. Drizzle with olive oil.
  5. Season with salt, pepper, garlic and herbs.
  6. Place lemon slices on the salmon.
  7. Optionally add a small piece of butter.
  8. Loosely close the foil to create a small package.
  9. Place on the grill and cook over medium heat for about 12-15 minutes.
  10. Open carefully and serve immediately.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

For Simon you can also keep the recipe milder and add more seasoning at the table.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Optionally add a small piece of butter.
