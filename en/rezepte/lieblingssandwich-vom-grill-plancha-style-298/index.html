Grill & BBQ

Favorite grilled sandwich (plancha style)

2-4 people · Grill & BBQ

Not a long baguette with strips of steak, but a compact sandwich from the grill or plancha: pressed crispy, juicy on the inside and with exactly the filling you want on the go. Street food feeling at home.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Grill/pan time: approx. 10-12 minutes
  • Total: approx. 22 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 firm sandwich rolls or ciabatta slices
  • 300 g chicken breast or turkey breast
  • 4 slices of Cheddar or Gouda
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 tomato
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise or tartar sauce
  • 1 tbsp mustard
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Paprika powder
  • optional: lettuce leaves

Instructions

  1. Cut the meat into strips, season and sear it on the grill or pan.
  2. Cut the onion into rings and the tomato into slices.
  3. Halve the rolls and lightly toast the cut sides.
  4. Mix mayonnaise and mustard and spread on the bottom half.
  5. Top with meat, cheese, onion and tomato.
  6. Put the top half on, press lightly.
  7. Spread the sandwich with butter and press on both sides on the plancha or pan until crispy.
  8. Halve and serve hot.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

Instead of chicken, minced steaks or marinated vegetables also work.
