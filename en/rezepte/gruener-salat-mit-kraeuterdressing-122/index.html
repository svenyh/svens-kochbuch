Green salad with herb dressing

3-4 people · Salads/side dishes

A green salad is often exactly what makes a table complete. With iceberg lettuce, cucumber and a fresh herb dressing, it becomes a simple but very useful classic.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 10 minutes
  • No waiting necessary

Ingredients

  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 1/2 cucumber
  • 1 handful of herbs
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 1 tsp honey
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Clean the lettuce and cut it into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Slice cucumber, chop herbs.
  3. Mix oil, vinegar, mustard, honey, salt and pepper.
  4. Mix the herbs into the dressing.
  5. Mix everything together shortly before serving.
