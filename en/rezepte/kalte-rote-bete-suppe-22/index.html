Cold beetroot soup

4 people · Soups / cold soups

This cold soup is full of freshness, acidity and the typical color of beetroot. Just when it's well chilled, it seems light, summery and yet filling enough to be more than just a small appetizer.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cooling time: at least 1 hour
  • Total: approx. 1 hour 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 bunch of beets or cooked beets
  • 1 cucumber
  • 1 bunch of radishes
  • dill
  • chives
  • 1 liter of milk
  • 500 ml cream yoghurt
  • 100 ml beetroot juice
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: boiled eggs for serving

Instructions

  1. Cut the beetroot, cucumber and radishes into small pieces or grate them coarsely.
  2. Finely chop the dill and chives.
  3. Place vegetables and herbs in a large bowl.
  4. Add milk, cream yoghurt and beetroot juice and mix everything well.
  5. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Chill the soup for at least 1 hour so that it infuses well.
  7. Stir again before serving and serve with boiled egg if desired.
