Pizza

Pizza Bianca with crème fraîche and red onions

2 people · Pizza / oven dishes

A light pizza without tomato sauce with crème fraîche, mozzarella and red onions. A little more refined, but still uncomplicated.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 10-12 minutes at 250°C

Ingredients

  • 1 portion of classic pizza dough
  • 150 g crème fraîche
  • 125g mozzarella
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • some rosemary or thyme

Instructions

  1. Prepare and roll out the pizza dough.
  2. Mix the crème fraîche with finely chopped garlic, salt and pepper.
  3. Spread the cream over the dough and spread the mozzarella on top.
  4. Cut the red onion into fine rings and place on top.
  5. Sprinkle with rosemary or thyme and bake in a hot oven.
  6. After baking, refine with a little olive oil.
