Spicy grilled sausages (classic and simple version)

3-4 people · Grill & BBQ / meat dishes

Bratwursts are one of those recipes that often seem simple on the grill, but become much better with the right seasoning and cooking. That's exactly what it's about here: classic direction, but clean and with an honest grill character.

Timing

  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Grilling time: approx. 12-18 minutes over medium heat
  • Total: approx. 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 6 bratwursts
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder
  • 1/2 tsp mustard
  • optional for the simple version: 2 tablespoons BBQ sauce
  • optional: 1 small onion in rings
  • optional: baguette or rolls for serving

Instructions

  1. Remove the sausages from the refrigerator 15 minutes before grilling.
  2. Prepare grill for medium indirect to medium direct heat.
  3. Grill the sausages a little more slowly at first and turn them regularly so that they get an even color.
  4. Finally, brown briefly over direct heat if more roasted flavor is desired.
  5. Serve with mustard, bread or potato salad.
