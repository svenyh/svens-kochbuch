Babka ziemniaczana with smoked pork and onions

6 people · Polish regional cuisine / oven dishes

This version is a little heartier and smokier than the classic babka because smoked pork and onions add more flavor to the potato mixture. This is exactly what makes it a very hearty oven dish with a good crust and juicy center.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 30 minutes
  • Fry the onions and smoked pork: approx. 8 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 70-85 minutes at 190 °C top/bottom heat
  • Resting time: approx. 10 minutes
  • Total: approximately 1 hour 55 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg floury potatoes
  • 300 g Kassler in small cubes
  • 2 large onions
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 tbsp flour
  • 100 ml sour cream
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1/2 tsp marjoram
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • some butter or oil for the mold

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 190 °C top/bottom heat and grease a mold well.
  2. Finely dice the onions and sauté in a little oil for 5 minutes until translucent until slightly golden. Fry the smoked pork cubes briefly, then set aside.
  3. Peel and finely grate the potatoes. Squeeze the mixture lightly so that it doesn't contain too much liquid.
  4. Mix the grated potatoes with eggs, flour, sour cream and marjoram. Season with pepper and only add salt carefully, as the smoked pork already has some spice.
  5. Fold in the onion and smoked pork mixture and pour everything into the mold.
  6. Bake in the oven for 70 to 85 minutes, until the babka is well browned on top.
  7. Let rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serve warm.
