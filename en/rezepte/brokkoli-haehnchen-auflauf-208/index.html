Broccoli chicken casserole

4 people · Oven dishes

Broccoli chicken casserole combines some freshness with creamy oven cooking. This makes it a good everyday dish that is filling without being too heavy.

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast fillet
  • 1 large broccoli
  • 250 g short pasta or 600 g potatoes, pre-cooked
  • 1 onion
  • 200 ml cream
  • 150 ml broth
  • 120g grated cheese
  • 1 tbsp flour
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Divide the broccoli into small florets and blanch in salted water for 4 minutes. Pour off.
  2. If pasta is used, cook it until just al dente. Cut the chicken into pieces and finely dice the onion.
  3. Brown the chicken in a pan. Add onion and sauté until translucent.
  4. Add butter and flour, stir briefly, then pour in broth and cream. Stir in mustard and season with salt and pepper.
  5. Place broccoli and pasta or potatoes in a baking dish, spread chicken and sauce over it.
  6. Sprinkle with cheese and bake at 190 °C top/bottom heat for 20 to 25 minutes.
