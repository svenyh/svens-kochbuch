Potato dishes

Street food potatoes from the oven

4 people · Potato dishes

Street food potatoes from the oven bring together crispy potatoes, fresh topping and creamy dip. Modern in appearance, but clearly designed for good home cooking.

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg small potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 150g yogurt
  • 100 g sour cream
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 150 g diced tomatoes
  • 1 small red onion
  • 1 spring onion
  • 80g grated cheese
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Wash the potatoes thoroughly, cut them in half and mix them with olive oil, paprika powder, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
  2. Place on a tray and bake at 210°C top/bottom heat for 35 to 40 minutes until golden brown and crispy.
  3. For the dip, mix yoghurt, sour cream, finely grated garlic, lemon juice, salt and pepper.
  4. Finely dice tomatoes, red onion and spring onion.
  5. Place the hot baked potatoes on a plate, sprinkle with cheese and let them sit in the switched off oven for 2 minutes.
  6. Serve with dip and the tomato and onion topping.
