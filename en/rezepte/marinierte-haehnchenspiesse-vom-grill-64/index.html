Marinated grilled chicken skewers

3-4 people · Grill & BBQ / meat dishes

Well-marinated chicken skewers are a real grill classic if they remain juicy and have a nice color on the outside. That's exactly the appeal here: not very complicated, but reliably delicious and suitable for many occasions.

Timing

  • Preparation: 15 minutes
  • Marinate: at least 30 minutes
  • Grill time: 10-12 minutes over medium direct heat
  • Rest time: 2 minutes
  • Total active: approx. 30 minutes plus marinating time

Ingredients

  • 700 g chicken breast fillet
  • 2 peppers
  • 1 red onion
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp natural yogurt
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder or 1 small garlic clove
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Wooden or metal skewers

Instructions

  1. Cut chicken into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Mix oil, yogurt, paprika powder, garlic, lemon juice, salt and pepper to make a marinade.
  3. Let the chicken pieces soak in the marinade for at least 30 minutes.
  4. Cut the peppers and onions into coarse pieces.
  5. Alternately thread the chicken, peppers and onions onto skewers.
  6. Preheat the grill to medium direct heat and grill the skewers for 10 to 12 minutes, turning regularly, until the meat is cooked through and lightly browned.
  7. Serve immediately.
