Make zakvas for Żurek yourself

Approach for several servings of Żurek · Basics / Polish cuisine

A good Żurek depends on a clean, aromatic zakvas. The approach doesn't need much work, but it does take a bit of time - and that's exactly what creates the typical sour-spicy base that really rounds out the soup.

Timing

  • Setting up: approx. 10 minutes
  • Ripening time: 4-5 days
  • Then store refrigerated

Ingredients

  • 500 ml boiled, completely cooled water
  • 6 tbsp rye flour type 2000
  • 4-5 cloves of garlic
  • 4 bay leaves
  • some allspice seeds

Instructions

  1. Prepare a clean large jar or container.
  2. Mix the rye flour with the cooled water thoroughly until there are no lumps left.
  3. Lightly press the garlic cloves and add them together with the bay leaf and allspice.
  4. Cover the container with a cloth or loosely placed lid to allow air to circulate.
  5. Let stand at room temperature for 4 to 5 days.
  6. Stir once a day with a clean spoon.
  7. The Zakwas is ready when it smells pleasantly sour and appears clearly fermented.
  8. Strain through a sieve before use or use directly as desired.
  9. Store in the refrigerator for a few more days.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Just cover the jar, do not seal it airtight, so that the mixture can work.
