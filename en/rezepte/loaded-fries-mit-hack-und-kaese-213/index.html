Loaded fries with minced meat and cheese

4 people · Potato dishes

Loaded fries with minced meat and cheese deliver a lot of flavor and a very direct, hearty effect. Despite the modern look, the whole thing remains clearly anchored in the good, hearty home kitchen.

Ingredients

  • 900 g frozen fries or oven fries
  • 400 g minced beef
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 100 ml pureed tomatoes
  • 80 ml broth
  • 120g grated cheddar
  • 2 spring onions
  • 100 g sour cream
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Bake the fries in the oven according to package instructions until crispy.
  2. Dice the onion finely. Heat oil in a pan and fry the minced meat vigorously.
  3. Add the onion, sweat briefly, then stir in the tomato paste.
  4. Pour in the pureed tomatoes and broth. Season with paprika powder, salt and pepper and simmer gently for 6 to 8 minutes.
  5. Place the hot fries on an ovenproof plate or in a baking dish. Spread the mince over it and sprinkle with cheddar cheese.
  6. Melt in a hot oven for 2 to 3 minutes. Serve with sour cream and spring onions.

Frequently asked questions
