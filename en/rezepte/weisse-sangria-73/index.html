White Sangria

4 glasses · Cocktails / summer drinks

White sangria seems a little brighter and fresher than the red version, but remains just as uncomplicated. Especially with chilled wine and matching fruit, you can create a summer drink that seems light and still looks like something.

Timing

  • Preparation: 10 minutes
  • Cool: 30 minutes
  • Total: approx. 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 bottle of dry white wine (0.75 l)
  • 200 ml lemonade
  • 1 peach or 2 nectarines
  • 1/2 apple
  • 1/2 lime
  • 100 ml apple juice or white grape juice
  • 1 tbsp sugar, optional
  • Ice cubes

Instructions

  1. Cut the peach, apple and lime into pieces or slices.
  2. Put everything in a carafe and mix with apple juice.
  3. Pour in white wine and lemonade.
  4. Stir well and chill for at least 30 minutes.
  5. Serve with ice cream.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

Very refreshing with nectarine instead of peach.
