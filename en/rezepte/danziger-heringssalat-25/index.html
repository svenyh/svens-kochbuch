Danzig herring salad

4 people · Fish & seafood / salads

Danzig herring salad is a typical cold classic with a salty, sweet-sour and creamy flavor at the same time. Well chilled and a little steeped, it seems much more harmonious than immediately after mixing.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cooling time: at least 30 minutes
  • Total: approx. 50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 herring fillets or pickled herring fillets
  • 2 potatoes, cooked
  • 1 apple
  • 2 pickles
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp sour cream or yogurt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut herrings into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Peel the potatoes and cut them into small cubes.
  3. Also finely dice the apple, cucumber and onion.
  4. Put everything in a bowl.
  5. Mix mayonnaise with sour cream or yogurt and add as a dressing.
  6. Mix carefully and season with pepper.
  7. Chill for at least 30 minutes before serving so that the salad is well infused.
