Cucumber salad with cream and dill

3-4 people · Salads/side dishes

Cucumber salad with cream and dill is exactly the kind of side dish that immediately looks like Sunday fare. Cool, creamy and fresh, it is particularly good with hearty meat dishes.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Brewing time: approx. 10 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 large cucumber
  • 150 g sour cream or sour cream
  • 1 tsp vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • fresh dill

Instructions

  1. Finely slice the cucumber and season lightly with salt.
  2. Let it steep briefly and pour off any excess water.
  3. Mix the sour cream with vinegar, sugar, pepper and dill.
  4. Mix with the cucumber and season to taste.
