Chicken tinga tacos

4 people · Poultry dishes

Tender chicken in a spicy chipotle tomato sauce – popular Mexican street food, quick to make and full of flavour.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 35 minutes
  • Total: approx. 50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 700 g chicken breast
  • 400 g tomatoes
  • 2 onions
  • chipotle chilli
  • 8 tortillas

Instructions

  1. Cook the chicken.
  2. Shred with forks.
  3. Make the tomato sauce.
  4. Fold in the meat.
  5. Serve in tortillas.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with sour cream and avocado.
