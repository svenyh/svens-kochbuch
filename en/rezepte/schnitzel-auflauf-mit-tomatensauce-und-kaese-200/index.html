Schnitzel casserole with tomato sauce and cheese

4 people · Oven dishes

A schnitzel casserole that combines classic home cooking with a scalloped finish. Tomato sauce and cheese give the dish a juicy, oven-fresh twist.

Ingredients

  • 4 small pork schnitzels
  • 2 eggs
  • 60g flour
  • 100 g breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp oil for frying
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 500 g pureed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 150 g mozzarella or Gouda, grated
  • 30g Parmesan
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Lightly flatten the schnitzels and season with salt and pepper. Dredge in flour, beaten egg and breadcrumbs.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan and fry the schnitzels for 2 minutes per side until golden. You don't have to be completely done yet.
  3. Finely dice the onion and garlic. Sweat in the same pan, add tomato paste and fry briefly.
  4. Pour in the pureed tomatoes and simmer with oregano, sugar, salt and pepper for 10 to 12 minutes.
  5. Place the schnitzels in a baking dish, cover with tomato sauce and sprinkle with mozzarella and Parmesan.
  6. Bake at 190 °C top/bottom heat for 15 to 18 minutes until the cheese is melted and lightly browned.
