Pulpo a la Gallega

4 people · Fish & seafood

Galician octopus on potato slices with olive oil, paprika and sea salt – a classic festive dish from north-west Spain. Simple to plate, outstanding in flavour.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 70 minutes
  • Total: approx. 90 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 kg octopus
  • 800 g potatoes
  • olive oil
  • sweet paprika
  • sea salt

Instructions

  1. Cook the octopus until tender.
  2. Cook the potatoes separately.
  3. Slice the potatoes.
  4. Cut the octopus.
  5. Arrange on the potatoes.
  6. Drizzle with olive oil.
  7. Sprinkle with paprika and sea salt.
