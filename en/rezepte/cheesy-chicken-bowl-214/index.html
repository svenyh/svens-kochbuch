Cheesy Chicken Bowl

4 people · Fusion cuisine

The Cheesy Chicken Bowl is creamy, spicy and very suitable for everyday use. Rice, chicken and cheese combine to create a modern main dish with familiar flavors.

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast fillet
  • 250g rice
  • 1 small pepper
  • 150g corn
  • 1 avocado, optional
  • 150 ml cream
  • 80g cream cheese
  • 80g grated cheddar
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 spring onion
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook rice according to package instructions.
  2. Cut the chicken into cubes and chop the peppers into small pieces. Heat oil in a pan and fry the chicken.
  3. Add the peppers and corn and fry for 3 to 4 minutes. Season with paprika powder, salt and pepper.
  4. Stir in the cream and cream cheese and let it become creamy for 2 minutes. Add the cheddar and let it melt.
  5. Divide rice into bowls. Add the chicken and cheese mixture on top.
  6. If desired, add avocado, lemon juice and spring onion and serve immediately.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

Avocado is optional, but tastes very good with the warm chicken and cheese base.
