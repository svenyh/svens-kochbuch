Cheese pasta from the oven

4 people · Oven dishes

Cheese pasta from the oven thrives on creamy sauce and a beautifully browned surface. A simple but very rewarding recipe for days when comfort food is required.

Ingredients

  • 350g rigatoni
  • 40g butter
  • 40g flour
  • 500 ml milk
  • 100 ml cream
  • 120 g Gouda, grated
  • 100 g cheddar cheese, grated
  • 40g Parmesan
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 2 tbsp breadcrumbs
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook rigatoni in salted water until just al dente and drain.
  2. Melt butter in the pot, stir in flour and sauté for 1 minute. Pour in milk and cream while stirring.
  3. Cook the sauce gently for 5 minutes, then stir in the Gouda, Cheddar, mustard and half the Parmesan.
  4. Season with salt and pepper and mix in the rigatoni.
  5. Pour everything into a baking dish. Mix the remaining Parmesan with breadcrumbs and sprinkle on top.
  6. Bake at 200 °C top/bottom heat for 15 to 20 minutes until golden brown.
