Classic salami pizza

For 2 people · Pizza & Bread / Oven & Casseroles

A classic pizza night favorite with tomato sauce, cheese and salami. Simple, reliable and always popular.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 10-12 minutes at 250°C

Ingredients

  • 1 portion of classic pizza dough
  • 200 g pizza sauce
  • 150 g grated mozzarella
  • 80-100g salami
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • some olive oil

Instructions

  1. Roll out the pizza dough into a round shape on a floured surface.
  2. Place on baking paper or a well-heated tray.
  3. Spread the pizza sauce thinly on top.
  4. Sprinkle with mozzarella.
  5. Distribute the salami slices evenly on top.
  6. Season with a little oregano.
  7. Bake in a very hot oven until the edges and cheese are golden brown.
  8. After baking, serve with a few drops of olive oil.
