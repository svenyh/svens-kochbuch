Quiche Lorraine

4 people · Oven dishes

Creamy bacon quiche from Lorraine – hearty, straightforward and perfect for guests or Sunday brunch. Sven's version with crisp shortcrust pastry.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 35 minutes
  • Total: approx. 55 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 shortcrust pastry
  • 200 g bacon lardons
  • 3 eggs
  • 200 ml cream
  • 100 g cheese
  • salt
  • pepper
  • nutmeg

Instructions

  1. Line a tin with the pastry.
  2. Fry the bacon.
  3. Whisk eggs, cream and cheese, season.
  4. Spread bacon on the pastry and pour over the custard.
  5. Bake for 35 minutes at 180 °C.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with green salad.
