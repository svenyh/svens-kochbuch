Pizza Margherita classic

For 2 people · Pizza & Bread / Basic Recipes

A real classic with tomato, mozzarella and basil. Few ingredients, but that's exactly why it's particularly good.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 10-12 minutes at 250°C

Ingredients

  • 1 portion of classic pizza dough
  • 1 portion of pizza sauce basic recipe
  • 2 balls of mozzarella
  • some fresh basil leaves
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Instructions

  1. Prepare the pizza dough and roll it out thinly.
  2. Spread the pizza sauce evenly on top.
  3. Tear the mozzarella into pieces or cut into slices and spread on top.
  4. Bake the pizza in a very hot oven until the edges and cheese are nicely browned.
  5. After baking, garnish with basil and a little olive oil.
