Flammkuchen Alsatian style

4 people · Pizza & basics

Thin crisp dough with crème fraîche, bacon and onions – the Alsatian oven classic, quick to make and perfect for sharing.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 10–12 minutes
  • Total: approx. 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 250 g flour
  • 125 ml water
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • For the topping: 200 g crème fraîche
  • For the topping: 150 g bacon lardons
  • For the topping: 2 onions
  • For the topping: pepper

Instructions

  1. Make a thin dough.
  2. Spread with crème fraîche.
  3. Scatter bacon and onions.
  4. Bake for about 10–12 minutes at 250 °C.
