Jollof rice

4 people · Pan dishes

Red, spiced rice from West Africa – a celebration classic in Nigeria and Ghana that appears at every feast.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 25 minutes
  • Total: approx. 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 300 g long-grain rice
  • 1 onion
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 400 g tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 750 ml stock
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp curry
  • salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Fry the onion and pepper.
  2. Add the tomato paste.
  3. Stir in the tomatoes.
  4. Add the rice.
  5. Pour in the stock.
  6. Cook for about 25 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with chicken or grilled meat.
