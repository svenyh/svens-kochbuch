Meatballs Grandma's style

4 people · Classics & home cooking

Meatballs are one of those recipes that feel immediately familiar in every kitchen. Nicely browned on the outside, juicy and well seasoned on the inside – that's exactly how they taste best Grandma's style.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Resting the mixture: 10 minutes
  • Frying time: approx. 12 to 15 minutes over medium heat
  • Total time: approx. 35 to 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600g mixed minced meat
  • 1 bun
  • 1 onion
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Paprika powder
  • oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Soak the bun in a little water or milk, squeeze out well and tear into small pieces.
  2. Thoroughly mix the minced meat with the bun, finely diced onion, egg, mustard, salt, pepper and paprika powder.
  3. Let the mixture rest for 10 minutes so it binds.
  4. Shape even patties with wet hands – don't press too firmly.
  5. Fry in a pan with a little oil over medium heat for 12 to 15 minutes until golden brown on all sides, turning occasionally.
  6. Rest for 2 minutes and serve warm with potatoes or cold on bread.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Don't fry too hot – medium heat keeps the patties juicy inside.

What side dish goes well?

A little marjoram goes well with it.
