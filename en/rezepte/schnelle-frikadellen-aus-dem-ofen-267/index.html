Quick meatballs from the oven

For 4 people · Quick kitchen / oven dishes

Simple meatballs from the oven, golden brown on the outside and juicy on the inside. Good for everyday life, snacks or dinner.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 20-25 minutes at 200°C

Ingredients

  • 500g minced meat
  • 1 roll or 3 tablespoons breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Paprika powder
  • some oil

Instructions

  1. Soak and squeeze the bun or provide breadcrumbs.
  2. Dice the onion finely.
  3. Knead everything with minced meat, egg, mustard and spices.
  4. Form meatballs.
  5. Place on a baking tray lined with baking paper.
  6. Brush lightly with oil.
  7. Bake in the oven until golden brown and cooked through.
  8. Serve hot.
