Grilled Stuffed Portobello Mushrooms

2-3 people · Grill & BBQ / Side Dishes

Stuffed portobellos from the grill have real substance and quickly feel much more special than classic side dishes. With a creamy cheese filling they become juicy and aromatic – almost the main star on the grill, or a light main meal with baguette.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Grilling time: approx. 10 to 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 25 to 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 large portobello mushrooms
  • 150 g cream cheese
  • 2 small tomatoes
  • 1 small clove of garlic
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley or herbs
  • 30g grated cheese or parmesan optional
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Carefully clean the mushrooms, remove the stems and lightly clean the underside. Brush caps inside and out lightly with olive oil.
  2. Dice tomatoes small and mix with cream cheese, finely chopped garlic, parsley, salt, pepper and a little olive oil into a creamy filling.
  3. Fill the portobellos generously. If you like, sprinkle some grated cheese on top.
  4. Grill over indirect to medium heat for 10 to 15 minutes until tender and the filling is hot and lightly browned. Close the lid if needed.
  5. Serve immediately – as a side with grilled meat or with baguette as a light main meal.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

Alternatively bake in the oven at 190 °C for about 18 to 22 minutes until the filling is lightly browned.
