Grilled Stuffed Portobello Mushrooms

2-3 people · Grill & BBQ / Side Dishes

Filled portobellos have real substance when grilled and quickly seem much more high-quality than classic side dishes. Especially with a good filling, they become juicy, aromatic and almost the main star on the grill.

Timing

  • Preparation: 15 minutes
  • Grilling time: 10-15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 25-30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 large portobello mushrooms
  • 150 g cream cheese
  • 2 small tomatoes
  • 1 small clove of garlic
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley or herbs
  • 30g grated cheese or parmesan optional
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Carefully clean the mushrooms, remove the stems and lightly clean the underside.
  2. Dice tomatoes small and mix with cream cheese, garlic, herbs, salt, pepper and a little olive oil.
  3. Fill the portobellos generously with the filling. If you like, sprinkle some grated cheese on top.
  4. Grill the mushrooms over indirect to medium heat for 10-15 minutes, until tender and the filling is hot and lightly browned.
  5. Serve straight away – as a side dish or with baguette as a light main meal.
