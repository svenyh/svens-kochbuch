Dead grandma with sauerkraut and mashed potatoes

4 people · Classic recipes / classics & home cooking

Dead Grandma is one of those hearty Eastern dishes that polarize, but that's exactly why it has so much character. With fried black pudding, mild sauerkraut and creamy mashed potatoes, this is a very down-to-earth dish.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cook potatoes: approx. 20-25 minutes
  • Sauerkraut: approx. 20-25 minutes
  • Fry the black pudding: approx. 8-12 minutes
  • Total: approx. 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600 g blood sausage
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tbsp clarified butter
  • 800 g floury potatoes
  • 40g butter
  • 150 ml milk, warm
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 1 pinch of nutmeg
  • 500 g sauerkraut
  • 1 tbsp lard or butter
  • 100 ml broth or water
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 4 juniper berries

Instructions

  1. Peel the potatoes, cut them into pieces and boil them in salted water until soft.
  2. For the sauerkraut, heat the lard or butter in a pot, add the sauerkraut, bay leaves, juniper and stock and cook gently for 20 to 25 minutes.
  3. Skin the blood sausage and cut it into coarse pieces. Dice onions finely.
  4. Heat the clarified butter in a large pan and sauté the onions until golden.
  5. Add black pudding and fry over medium heat. Roughly break it up with a spatula until you get a rustic, juicy mass. This takes about 8 to 12 minutes.
  6. Drain the cooked potatoes, let them evaporate briefly and mash them into puree with butter and warm milk. Season with salt, pepper and nutmeg.
  7. Finally season the sauerkraut and serve everything together while it is hot.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Leftovers keep in the fridge for 1–2 days; reheat the mash with a little milk.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Clarified butter can be replaced with neutral oil when frying the black pudding.
