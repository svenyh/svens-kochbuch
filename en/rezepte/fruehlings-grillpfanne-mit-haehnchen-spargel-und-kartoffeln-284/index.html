Spring grill pan with chicken, asparagus and potatoes

4 people · Grill &amp; BBQ / Pfannengerichte

Spring grill pan with chicken, green asparagus, potatoes and vegetables. Hearty, fresh and good for plancha or grill pan.

Timing

  • Preparation: 20 minutes | Grilling time: 20-25 minutes | Total time: approx. 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600 g boneless chicken breast or chicken thighs
  • 800 g small potatoes
  • 500 g green asparagus
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 zucchini
  • 2 red onions
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder or 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp Herbs de Provence
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • optional: some feta or parmesan to serve

Instructions

  1. Halve the potatoes and pre-cook them for about 10 minutes.
  2. Cut chicken into bite-sized pieces.
  3. Trim the asparagus at the bottom and cut into pieces.
  4. Roughly chop the peppers, zucchini and onions.
  5. Season chicken with oil, paprika, garlic, herbs, salt and pepper.
  6. Preheat grill pan or plancha.
  7. First fry the potatoes and chicken.
  8. After about 8-10 minutes, add vegetables and asparagus.
  9. Grill everything, turning occasionally, until the chicken is cooked through and the vegetables are lightly roasted.
  10. Finally season with lemon juice.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with herb quark or tzatziki.
