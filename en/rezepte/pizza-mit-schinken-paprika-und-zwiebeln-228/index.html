Pizza

Pizza with ham, peppers and onions

2 people · Pizza / classics & home cooking

A mild, family-friendly pizza with ham, peppers and red onions. Also very suitable as a sheet pizza.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 10-12 minutes at 250°C

Ingredients

  • 1 portion of classic pizza dough
  • 200 g pureed tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 150g grated cheese
  • 100 g cooked ham
  • 1 pepper
  • 1 small red onion
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • oregano

Instructions

  1. Roll out the pizza dough.
  2. Mix the pureed tomatoes with tomato paste and spices and spread over the dough.
  3. Pour the cheese on top.
  4. Cut the ham into pieces, cut the pepper into strips and the onion into fine rings.
  5. Spread everything evenly on the pizza and bake in the preheated oven.
