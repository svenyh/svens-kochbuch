White bean stew with mushrooms

4 people · Soups/Stews

A down-to-earth stew with white beans, mushrooms, onions and garlic - hearty, warming and just as good as a vegetarian version as with a little bacon. Herbs and tomatoes give the pot depth without making it heavy.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 20-25 minutes
  • Total: approx. 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cans of white beans (400 g each), drained
  • 300 g mushrooms or chanterelles
  • 2 onions
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 400 g pureed tomatoes
  • 500 ml vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 teaspoon rosemary, dried
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: 100 g smoked bacon or pancetta
  • optional: parsley

Instructions

  1. Finely dice the onions and garlic, clean and quarter the mushrooms.
  2. Heat oil in a large pot and optionally fry bacon briefly.
  3. Sauté onions and garlic for 4-5 minutes.
  4. Add the mushrooms and fry vigorously until they take on color.
  5. Briefly roast the tomato paste, pour in the tomatoes and broth.
  6. Add thyme, rosemary, beans, salt and pepper.
  7. Let simmer for 15-20 minutes until everything is well cooked through.
  8. Sprinkle with parsley and serve hot.
