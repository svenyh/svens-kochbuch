Classic marble cake

For 1 loaf tin or bundt cake tin · Baking & Sweets / Cakes

A classic marble cake with light and dark dough. Juicy, uncomplicated and always suitable for coffee.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 50-60 minutes at 175°C

Ingredients

  • 250 g soft butter
  • 200g sugar
  • 1 packet of vanilla sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 300g flour
  • 1 packet of baking powder
  • 100 ml milk
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 1 tbsp milk additionally

Instructions

  1. Mix butter, sugar and vanilla sugar until creamy.
  2. Stir in the eggs one at a time.
  3. Mix the flour and baking powder and stir in the milk.
  4. Pour about two thirds of the dough into a greased mold.
  5. Mix the remaining dough with cocoa and 1 tablespoon of milk.
  6. Place the dark dough on top of the light one.
  7. Fold through in a spiral with a fork.
  8. Bake in the oven, let cool and dust with powdered sugar if desired.
