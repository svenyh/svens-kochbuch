Quick pumpkin soup

4 people · Soups/home cooking

Creamy pumpkin soup from Hokkaido in less than 30 minutes - just right for autumn and winter. The shell can stay on, which saves time. With coconut milk it becomes mildly exotic, with cream it becomes classic creamy.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 Hokkaido pumpkin (approx. 1 kg)
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 800 ml vegetable broth
  • 200 ml coconut milk or cream
  • 2 tbsp oil or butter
  • 1 teaspoon curry or nutmeg
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: 1 teaspoon ginger, grated
  • optional: pumpkin seeds for garnish

Instructions

  1. Wash the pumpkin, halve it, remove the seeds and cut it into cubes - leave the peel on in Hokkaido.
  2. Peel and dice the onion and garlic.
  3. Heat oil or butter in a pot and sauté onion for 3-4 minutes.
  4. Add garlic, ginger and pumpkin and sauté briefly.
  5. Deglaze with broth and simmer for 15-20 minutes until the pumpkin is soft.
  6. Puree the soup finely and stir in coconut milk or cream.
  7. Season with curry or nutmeg, salt and pepper.
  8. Serve with pumpkin seeds.
