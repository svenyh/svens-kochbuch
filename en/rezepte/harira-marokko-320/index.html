Harira – Morocco

4 people · Culinary World Tour / Mediterranean & North Africa

Spicy Moroccan harira with lentils and chickpeas – Sven's version that also works without meat. Hearty, aromatic and ideal as a main course.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 45–60 minutes
  • Total: approx. 60–75 minutes

Ingredients

  • 300 g lamb or beef optional
  • 1 can chickpeas
  • 100 g lentils
  • 1 onion
  • celery
  • 400 g tomatoes
  • stock
  • ginger
  • turmeric
  • cumin
  • coriander
  • parsley
  • lemon

Instructions

  1. Optionally sear the meat.
  2. Add the vegetables, pulses, tomatoes and stock.
  3. Simmer for 45–60 minutes.
  4. Season with herbs and lemon.
