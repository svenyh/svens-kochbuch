Peach Iced Tea

approx. 6–8 glasses · Summer drinks, lemonades & mocktails

A sweet, fruity peach iced tea based on black tea – ideal for relaxed summer afternoons.

Timing

  • Prep time: 15 minutes
  • Chill time: 1 hour

Ingredients

  • 1 litre black tea
  • 4 ripe peaches
  • juice of 1 lemon
  • honey
  • ice cubes

Instructions

  1. Brew the black tea and let it cool completely.
  2. Purée the peaches.
  3. Mix with the tea.
  4. Add lemon juice and honey.
  5. Serve cold.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

Tea and peach syrup can chill the day before; add ice when you are ready to serve.

Can I freeze this?

Tea frozen into ice cubes prevents watering down when topping up.

How long does it keep?

Iced tea keeps in the fridge for 1–2 days in an airtight container.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Mango or passion fruit syrup works instead of peach syrup.
