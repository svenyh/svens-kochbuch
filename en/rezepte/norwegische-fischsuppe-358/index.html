Norwegian fish soup

4 people · Soups

Creamy soup with fish, prawns and vegetables – a Norwegian classic that brings the richness of the coast to the plate.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 25 minutes
  • Total: approx. 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g fish fillet
  • 200 g prawns
  • 1 litre fish stock
  • 200 ml cream
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 leek
  • salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Sweat the vegetables.
  2. Add the stock.
  3. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Add the fish and prawns.
  5. Stir in the cream.
  6. Rest briefly.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

Fish and vegetables can be prepared ahead; simmer the soup gently when you are ready to serve.

How long does it keep?

Leftovers keep in the fridge for 1–2 days – do not boil hard or the fish turns tough.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Crème fraîche works instead of cream for a slightly tangier note.
