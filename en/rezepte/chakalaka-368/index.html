Chakalaka

4 people · Side dishes

Spicy vegetables from South African township cooking – colourful, punchy and the perfect partner for braai and grilled meat.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 onions
  • 2 bell peppers
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 can beans
  • 400 g tomatoes
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • chilli to taste

Instructions

  1. Dice the vegetables.
  2. Fry the onions.
  3. Add the remaining vegetables.
  4. Stir in the spices.
  5. Add the tomatoes.
  6. Simmer for 20 minutes.
  7. Fold in the beans.
