Garlic and potato pan with chili and egg

1 person · Fast cuisine

The potato pan is down-to-earth, spicy and perfect when you want something hearty on the table without much effort. Chili, garlic and egg make this a simple stir-fry dish that doesn't seem boring.

Timing

  • Total time: 25-30 minutes
  • Preparation: 10 minutes
  • Pan time: 15-20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500g potatoes
  • 3-4 cloves of garlic
  • Chili or sambal oelek to taste
  • 2 eggs
  • Oil or clarified butter
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Pre-boil the potatoes in salted water, let them cool and cut into slices or cubes.
  2. Heat oil or clarified butter in a pan and fry the potatoes until golden brown.
  3. Finely chop the garlic and add it together with the chili or sambal oelek.
  4. Continue frying everything briefly until the garlic smells fragrant.
  5. Pour the eggs over the potatoes and let them set over medium heat.
  6. Season with salt and pepper and serve immediately.
