Tyrolean Gröstl

4 people · Pan dishes

Tyrolean leftover deluxe: crisp potato slices, roast pork and a fried egg on top – hearty, quick and classic Alpine home cooking.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Total: approx. 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 800 g boiled potatoes
  • 400 g leftover roast pork
  • 2 onions
  • 4 eggs
  • clarified butter
  • salt
  • pepper
  • marjoram

Instructions

  1. Slice the potatoes.
  2. Fry the onions.
  3. Add the meat.
  4. Fry the potatoes until crisp.
  5. Season with marjoram.
  6. Serve with a fried egg.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

Potatoes boiled the day before work best.

What side dish goes well?

Serve with a fried egg.
