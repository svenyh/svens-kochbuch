Grilled asparagus wrapped in bacon with honey mustard glaze

4 people as a side dish · Salate &amp; Beilagen / Grill &amp; BBQ

Green asparagus wrapped in bacon with a thin honey mustard glaze. Very suitable as a side dish from the grill or from the grill pan.

Timing

  • Preparation: 15 minutes | Grilling time: 10-12 minutes | Total time: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g green asparagus
  • 150-200g bacon or bacon
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp mustard
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • pepper
  • optional: some parmesan

Instructions

  1. Wash the asparagus and cut off the woody ends.
  2. Wrap 2-3 asparagus spears with bacon.
  3. Mix honey, mustard, oil, lemon juice and pepper.
  4. Spread the asparagus packets thinly with the glaze.
  5. Grill on the grill or in a grill pan over medium heat.
  6. After about 8-12 minutes, the bacon should be crispy and the asparagus should be al dente.
  7. Optionally sprinkle with Parmesan and serve.
