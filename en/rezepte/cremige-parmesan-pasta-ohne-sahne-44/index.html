Creamy Parmesan pasta without cream

1 person · Pasta dishes

This pasta shows how creamy a pasta dish can be even without cream when parmesan and pasta water work together properly. The result is simple, quick and just right if you want to cook something warm with few ingredients.

Timing

  • Total time: approx. 15 minutes
  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Cooking time: 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 120-150g pasta
  • 30-40g Parmesan
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • some pasta water
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta in plenty of salted water until al dente.
  2. Reserve some pasta water.
  3. Melt butter in a pan and add 2-3 tablespoons of pasta water.
  4. Put the pasta in the pan and toss briefly.
  5. Gradually stir in the finely grated Parmesan until a creamy sauce is formed.
  6. Season with pepper and serve immediately.
