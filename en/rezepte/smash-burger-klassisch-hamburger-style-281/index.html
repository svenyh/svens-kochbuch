Smash Burger classic – Hamburger style

4 burgers · Grill &amp; BBQ

Classic smash burger with loose beef patties, strong crust and typical hamburger style. Served straight from the hot plate.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes | Grilling/roasting: approx. 10 minutes | Total time: approx. 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600 g ground beef, preferably with approx. 20% fat content
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • some oil for the grill or pan
  • 4 brioche burger buns
  • 4-8 slices of pickled cucumber
  • 1 red onion, cut into thin rings
  • 4 large lettuce leaves
  • 1-2 tomatoes, sliced
  • ketchup
  • Mustard
  • optional: burger sauce
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 1 tsp cucumber water
  • 1 small gherkin, finely chopped
  • some pepper

Instructions

  1. Loosely divide the ground beef into 4 portions of approx. 150 g each. Do not knead tightly.
  2. Form each portion into a loose ball.
  3. Preheat griddle, plancha or heavy pan to very hot.
  4. Halve the buns and briefly toast them on the cut side. Set aside.
  5. Place the meatballs on the hot surface and flatten vigorously with a sturdy spatula or burger smasher.
  6. Salt and pepper immediately.
  7. Fry for about 1.5-2 minutes until a strong crust forms.
  8. Turn the patties over and fry again for about 1 minute.
  9. Cover the bottom half of the bun with sauce, lettuce, tomato and patty.
  10. Add onions, cucumbers, ketchup and mustard.
  11. Put the lid on and serve immediately.
