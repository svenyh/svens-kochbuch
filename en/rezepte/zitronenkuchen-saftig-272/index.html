Juicy lemon cake

For 1 loaf pan · Baking & Sweets / Cakes

Juicy lemon cake with a fresh citrus note and simple icing. Uncomplicated and very popular.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 50-55 minutes at 175°C

Ingredients

  • 250g butter
  • 200g sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 300g flour
  • 1 packet of baking powder
  • 2 organic lemons
  • 100 ml milk
  • 150 g powdered sugar for the icing

Instructions

  1. Mix butter and sugar until creamy.
  2. Stir in eggs one at a time.
  3. Grate the lemon peel finely and add it.
  4. Mix the flour and baking powder and stir in the milk.
  5. Place the dough in a greased form.
  6. Bake in the oven.
  7. Let the cake cool slightly.
  8. Mix the powdered sugar with lemon juice to form a icing and pour it onto the cake.
