Kashubian-style herring

4 people · Fish & Seafood / Polish cuisine

Kashubian-style herring thrives on its sweet and sour sauce and the fact that everything is given enough time to soak through. Especially the next day the aroma is rounder, deeper and much more harmonious.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Brewing time: at least 4 hours
  • Total: approximately 4 hours 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 herring fillets
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 allspice seeds
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 100 ml water
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp vinegar
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Water herring fillets as needed and cut into pieces.
  2. Cut onions into fine strips.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and slowly fry the onions until soft.
  4. Stir in the tomato paste and roast briefly.
  5. Add water, bay leaf, allspice, sugar and vinegar and let the sauce simmer for a few minutes.
  6. Allow the tomato and onion mixture to cool.
  7. Layer herring and sauce alternately in a dish or glass.
  8. Let it sit in the fridge for at least a few hours, preferably overnight.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Let it sit in the fridge for at least a few hours, preferably overnight.

What side dish goes well?

Serve with fresh bread.
