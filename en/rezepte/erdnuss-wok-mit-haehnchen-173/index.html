Peanut wok with chicken

4 people · Asian wok / wok dishes

Peanut wok with chicken is a creamy, spicy wok dish with clear peanut notes and fresh lime. The coconut milk and peanut butter make the sauce very round without seeming heavy.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Wok preparation: approx. 12 minutes
  • Total: approx. 32 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast fillet
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 yellow pepper
  • 2 carrots
  • 150g broccoli
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 piece of ginger, approx. 20 g
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • For the sauce: 3 tablespoons peanut butter
  • For the sauce: 250 ml coconut milk
  • For the sauce: 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • For the sauce: 1 tbsp lime juice
  • For the sauce: 1 tbsp honey
  • For the sauce: 1 teaspoon red curry paste or chili
  • For the sauce: 2 tablespoons water, if necessary

Instructions

  1. Cut chicken into strips. Cut pepper and onion into strips. Cut carrots into fine sticks. Divide broccoli into small florets.
  2. For the sauce, mix the peanut butter, coconut milk, soy sauce, lime juice, honey and curry paste until smooth.
  3. Heat the wok to high heat. Add 2 tablespoons of oil and fry the chicken for 4 minutes. Take out.
  4. Add remaining oil to the wok. Saute onion, garlic, ginger, carrots and broccoli for 3 minutes.
  5. Add the peppers and continue frying briefly.
  6. Return chicken to wok. Pour in the sauce and simmer over medium heat for 4 to 5 minutes.
  7. If the sauce becomes too thick, add 1 to 2 tablespoons of water.
  8. Serve hot.
