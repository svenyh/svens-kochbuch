Croquetas de Jamón

4 people · Tapas

Crispy Spanish croquettes with a creamy ham filling – a tapas favourite worth the prep. Fried golden and wonderfully moist inside.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 30 minutes
  • Chilling time: at least 1 hour
  • Frying: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 45 minutes + chilling

Ingredients

  • 100 g Serrano ham
  • 50 g butter
  • 50 g flour
  • 500 ml milk
  • 2 eggs
  • breadcrumbs
  • oil for frying
  • salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter.
  2. Stir in the flour.
  3. Gradually add the milk and whisk until smooth.
  4. Fold in the ham and season.
  5. Chill the mixture.
  6. Shape into croquettes.
  7. Coat in breadcrumbs.
  8. Fry until golden.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

Prepare the mixture the day before.
