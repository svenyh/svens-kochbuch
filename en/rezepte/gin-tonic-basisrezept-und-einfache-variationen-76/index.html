Gin Tonic (basic recipe and simple variations)

1 glass · Cocktails / summer drinks

Gin Tonic is very simple at its core, but that's precisely why it's worth having a clean base. A good gin, matching tonic and small variations are enough to turn a standard drink into something much more harmonious.

Timing

  • Preparation: 3 minutes
  • Mix: 1 minute
  • Serve immediately with well-chilled tonic

Ingredients

  • 50 ml gin
  • 150 ml tonic water
  • Ice cubes
  • 1 slice of lemon or lime
  • optional variant 1: 3 slices of cucumber
  • optional variant 2: 1 small sprig of rosemary
  • optional variant 3: 1 slice of orange

Instructions

  1. Fill a large glass with ice cubes.
  2. Pour in gin.
  3. Slowly pour tonic water over it.
  4. Stir briefly.
  5. Garnish with lemon or lime.
  6. For variations, add additional cucumber, rosemary or orange to the glass.
