Layered salad with chicken and garlic sauce

4-6 people · Salads & Side Dishes / Fast Food

A fresh layered salad with fried chicken and creamy garlic sauce – visually beautiful and ideal for guests. Each layer stays clearly visible, and after a short chill it tastes round and fresh.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Chicken frying time: approx. 8 to 10 minutes
  • Chilling time: approx. 15 to 30 minutes
  • Total: approx. 45 to 60 minutes

Ingredients

  • 400g chicken breast
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Paprika powder
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 1 cucumber
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 can of corn
  • 150g grated cheese
  • 200 g natural yogurt
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • some parsley

Instructions

  1. Season chicken breast with salt, pepper and paprika powder, fry in oil for 8 to 10 minutes until cooked through. Cool and cut into small pieces.
  2. Wash iceberg lettuce and cut into strips, slice cucumber, dice tomatoes, drain corn.
  3. For the sauce, mix yogurt, mayonnaise, finely chopped garlic, salt and pepper until smooth – the sauce should be creamy, not too runny.
  4. Layer in a tall bowl or dish: lettuce, vegetables, corn, chicken, sauce and cheese on top.
  5. Sprinkle with parsley, chill for 15 to 30 minutes and serve in portions.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

Also works very well with turkey breast instead of chicken.
