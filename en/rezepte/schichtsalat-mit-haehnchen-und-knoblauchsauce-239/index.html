Layered salad with chicken and garlic sauce

4-6 people · Salads & Side Dishes / Fast Food

A fresh layered salad with fried chicken and creamy garlic sauce. Very good for guests and visually beautiful.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Roasting time: approx. 10 minutes
  • Brewing time: approx. 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 400g chicken breast
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Paprika powder
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 1 cucumber
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 can of corn
  • 150g grated cheese
  • 200 g natural yogurt
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • some parsley

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken breast, fry it in the pan, let it cool and cut into small pieces.
  2. Prepare iceberg lettuce, cucumber and tomatoes.
  3. For the sauce, mix yogurt, mayonnaise, garlic, salt and pepper.
  4. Put the salad in a mold in layers: lettuce, vegetables, corn, chicken, sauce, cheese.
  5. Sprinkle with parsley and let it steep briefly.
