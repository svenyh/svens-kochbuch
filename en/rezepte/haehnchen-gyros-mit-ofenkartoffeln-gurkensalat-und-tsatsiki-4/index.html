Chicken gyros with baked potatoes, cucumber salad and tzatziki

3-4 people · Stir-fry/oven dishes

Chicken gyros with baked potatoes, cucumber salad and tzatziki is a well-rounded feel-good dish that is very suitable for everyday use. Spicy meat, fresh side dishes and crispy potatoes combine to create a real Sunday classic.

Timing

  • Preparation: 20 minutes
  • Baked potatoes: 35-40 minutes
  • Gyros: 10-12 minutes
  • Total: approx. 50-60 minutes

Ingredients

  • 700 g shredded chicken or chicken fillet in strips
  • 2 tbsp gyro seasoning
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 large onion
  • 1.2kg potatoes
  • 1 cucumber
  • 250g Greek yogurt
  • 1/2-1 garlic clove
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • some oil for the potatoes
  • optional: paprika powder for the potatoes

Instructions

  1. Marinate the chicken with oil, gyro seasoning and onion strips.
  2. Cut the potatoes into wedges, season and mix with a little oil.
  3. Place the potatoes on a tray and bake at 200°C for about 35-40 minutes.
  4. Slice or grate the cucumber for the salad.
  5. For tzatziki, finely grate the cucumber, squeeze it out and mix it with the yogurt, garlic, salt, pepper and lemon juice.
  6. Sear the chicken in a large pan.
  7. Make fresh cucumber salad.
  8. Serve everything together.
