Kasnocken

4 people · Pan dishes

Alpine cheese spätzle with fried onions – creamy, hearty and one of Austria's most popular mountain-hut classics.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 300 g spätzle
  • 250 g mountain cheese
  • 2 onions
  • butter

Instructions

  1. Fry the onions until golden.
  2. Heat the spätzle.
  3. Fold in the cheese.
  4. Serve with the fried onions.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with the fried onions.
