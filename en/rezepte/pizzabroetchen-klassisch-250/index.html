Classic pizza rolls

For 4 people · Basic recipes / pizza & bread

Crispy, golden-brown pizza rolls with a hearty filling. Perfect as a snack, for guests or as an accompaniment to soup, salad and pizza evening.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 15-18 minutes at 200°C

Ingredients

  • 1 roll of pizza dough or approx. 300 g of homemade pizza dough
  • 150g grated cheese
  • 100g salami
  • 1 small red pepper
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 tablespoons crème fraîche or cream cheese
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Roll out the pizza dough and cut into small squares or rectangles.
  2. Dice the salami, peppers and onion very finely.
  3. Mix everything with cheese, crème fraîche, oregano, salt and pepper.
  4. Place a little filling on each dough, fold it up or roll it up and seal it tightly.
  5. Place the pizza rolls on a baking tray lined with baking paper.
  6. Bake in the preheated oven until golden brown and serve warm.
