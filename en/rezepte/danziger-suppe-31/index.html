Danzig soup

4 people · Soups / Polish cuisine

Danziger soup is a mild, traditional soup with a gentle seasoning and a pleasant, slightly creamy nature. It does not thrive on power, but on the fact that the individual components are brought together cleanly.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 45 minutes
  • Total: approximately 1 hour 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1.5 liters of water
  • 300g chicken
  • 300 g soup greens
  • 250 ml milk
  • a handful of noodles
  • a small amount of peas
  • a small amount of beans
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: dill

Instructions

  1. Put the chicken and water in a pot and bring to the boil slowly.
  2. Add the soup greens and cook everything to a strong broth.
  3. Remove the chicken, remove the meat from the bones and cut into small pieces.
  4. Strain the broth through a sieve if you want it to be finer.
  5. Cook the pasta, peas and beans in the broth until done.
  6. Add milk and warm gently.
  7. Put the chicken back in.
  8. Season with salt, pepper and a little dill if desired and serve hot.
