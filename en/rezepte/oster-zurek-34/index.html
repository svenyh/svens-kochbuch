Easter Żurek

approx. 2 liters of soup · Soups / Polish cuisine

Easter Żurek is a hearty, traditional soup that thrives on zakwas, sausage, egg and a well-balanced acidity. Especially at Easter, it is one of the dishes that seems simple but has a lot of character in terms of taste.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 25 minutes
  • Total: approx. 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 liter vegetable broth
  • 500 ml of zakwas for Żurek
  • 100 g smoked, air-dried sausage
  • 200 g white sausage, raw or pre-cooked
  • 200 g raw, smoked bacon
  • 3 tablespoons cream or whipped cream
  • 1 tsp marjoram or more
  • 1 allspice grain
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1/4 teaspoon each garlic and salt
  • To serve: 4-6 eggs, rye bread, parsley or oregano

Instructions

  1. Heat broth in a large pot.
  2. Cut smoked sausage, white sausage and bacon into suitable pieces and add to the broth.
  3. Let it simmer gently for a few minutes so that the broth takes on flavor.
  4. Add allspice, bay leaf, garlic, salt and marjoram.
  5. Add the zakwas while stirring.
  6. Bring to the boil briefly until the Żurek thickens slightly.
  7. Finally stir in the cream.
  8. Serve with halved eggs, rye bread and some parsley or oregano.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?
