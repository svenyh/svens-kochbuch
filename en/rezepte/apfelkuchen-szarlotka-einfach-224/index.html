Apple pie / szarlotka simple

1 springform pan or small baking dish · Baking & sweets / classics & home cooking

A simple apple pie with a short dough and juicy filling. Classic, uncomplicated and very good for everyday life.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cooling time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 40-45 minutes at 180°C

Ingredients

  • 300g flour
  • 150 g cold butter
  • 100g sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 800g apples
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Knead the flour, butter, sugar, egg and baking powder into a shortcrust pastry.
  2. Chill the dough for 20 minutes.
  3. Peel and core the apples and cut them into small pieces or grate them coarsely.
  4. Mix with sugar, cinnamon and lemon juice.
  5. Press two thirds of the dough into the mold, spread the apple filling on top and pluck or roll out the remaining dough over it.
  6. Bake in the oven until the cake is golden brown.
