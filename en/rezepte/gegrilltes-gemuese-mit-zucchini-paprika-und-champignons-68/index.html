Grill & BBQ

Grilled vegetables with zucchini, peppers and mushrooms

3-4 people · Grill & BBQ / Side Dishes

Grilled vegetables are really good when they get roasted flavors and still remain juicy. Zucchini, peppers and mushrooms are a strong combination because together they bring color, sweetness and spice to the plate.

Timing

  • Preparation: 15 minutes
  • Marinate: 10 minutes
  • Grill time: 8-10 minutes over medium to medium-high heat
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 zucchini
  • 2 peppers
  • 250 g mushrooms
  • 1 red onion
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small clove of garlic
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp Italian herbs
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut zucchini into slices, peppers into pieces, mushrooms halved and onion into wedges.
  2. Mix olive oil with finely chopped garlic, lemon juice, herbs, salt and pepper.
  3. Mix the vegetables with the marinade and let it steep for 10 minutes.
  4. Grill in a grill pan, grill tray or on a plancha over medium to medium-high heat.
  5. Grill for 8-10 minutes, turning several times, until the vegetables are soft and lightly browned.
  6. Serve hot.
