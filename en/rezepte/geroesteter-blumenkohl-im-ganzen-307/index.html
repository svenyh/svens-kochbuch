Whole roasted cauliflower

2-4 people · Oven dishes/side dishes

A whole cauliflower from the oven looks impressive and is actually really simple: heavily seasoned, nicely roasted on the outside and soft on the inside. Suitable as a side dish, vegetarian main dish or with yogurt dip as a snack in between.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 50-65 minutes
  • Total: approx. 65-75 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 whole cauliflower (approx. 1-1.2 kg)
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp paprika powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: 1 teaspoon curry
  • optional: yogurt dip made from 150 g yogurt, lemon juice and herbs
  • optional: parsley or coriander

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C top/bottom heat.
  2. Wash the cauliflower, remove the green leaves and cut a cross at the end of the stalk - this way the inside will cook better.
  3. Mix olive oil with paprika, cumin, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
  4. Coat the cauliflower generously on all sides.
  5. Place on a baking sheet and bake for 50-65 minutes, until the outside is golden brown and the stalk is soft.
  6. Check with a knife whether the stalk is soft.
  7. Optionally prepare yogurt dip and serve the cauliflower with it.
  8. Sprinkle with fresh herbs.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

Optionally prepare yogurt dip and serve the cauliflower with it.
