Sven's meatball casserole with Ajvar & potatoes - the oven bomb

3-4 people · Oven dishes / meat dishes

If you want something hearty, creamy and really satisfying, this casserole is right up there. Juicy meatballs, spicy Ajvar cream sauce and a nice oven crust - there's not much more to say about it.

Timing

  • Preparation: 25 minutes
  • Oven time: 30-35 minutes
  • Total: approx. 60 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600 g minced meat
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp breadcrumbs
  • 1 small onion
  • 3 tbsp ajvar
  • 200 ml cream
  • 100g grated cheese
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Paprika powder
  • Oil or butter for the mold

Instructions

  1. Pre-cook potatoes and cut into slices.
  2. Mix the minced meat with egg, breadcrumbs, onion and spices.
  3. Form small meatballs.
  4. Fry the meatballs briefly or use them raw if you want them to cook through in the oven.
  5. Mix ajvar with cream.
  6. Layer potatoes and meatballs in a baking dish.
  7. Pour Ajvar cream sauce on top.
  8. Sprinkle with cheese.
  9. Bake at 190°C for about 30-35 minutes until golden brown.
