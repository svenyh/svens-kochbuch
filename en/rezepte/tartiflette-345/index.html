Tartiflette

4 people · Oven dishes

From the French Alps: potatoes, bacon and Reblochon in creamy cream – hearty, warming and perfect for cold days.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 25 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 30 minutes
  • Total: approx. 55 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 kg potatoes
  • 200 g bacon lardons
  • 2 onions
  • 250 ml cream
  • 250 g Reblochon or Camembert

Instructions

  1. Parboil the potatoes.
  2. Fry the onions and bacon.
  3. Slice the potatoes.
  4. Layer everything in a dish.
  5. Pour over the cream.
  6. Spread the cheese on top.
  7. Bake for 30 minutes at 180 °C.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with a green salad.
