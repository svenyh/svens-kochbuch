Watermelon Lime Mint Agua Fresca Rosé Spritz

1 glass · Summer horchatas & aguas frescas

Light summer aperitif: Watermelon Lime Mint Agua Fresca Rosé Spritz. Dry rosé and sparkling mineral water turn the base drink into a light terrace aperitif.

Timing

  • Prep time: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 120 ml Watermelon Lime Mint Agua Fresca
  • 120 ml well-chilled dry rosé
  • 30–40 ml mineral water
  • Ice cubes
  • watermelon cubes, lime slice and mint

Instructions

  1. Chill the base drink (Watermelon Lime Mint Agua Fresca) and alcohol well.
  2. Fill a large wine glass with ice cubes.
  3. Pour 120 ml Watermelon Lime Mint Agua Fresca first.
  4. Add 120 ml rosé and 30–40 ml mineral water.
  5. Stir gently, garnish with matching fruit and serve immediately.
