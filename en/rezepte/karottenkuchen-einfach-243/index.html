Easy carrot cake

1 springform pan · Baking & sweets / classics & home cooking

A moist carrot cake without much effort. Very suitable for everyday use and optionally with a simple cream cheese topping.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 40-45 minutes at 180°C

Ingredients

  • 300g carrots
  • 250g flour
  • 180g sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 150 ml oil
  • 1 packet of baking powder
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 100 g ground almonds
  • 1 packet of vanilla sugar
  • optional: 150 g cream cheese
  • optional: 50 g powdered sugar
  • optional: some lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Finely grate carrots.
  2. Beat eggs with sugar and vanilla sugar until fluffy.
  3. Stir in oil.
  4. Mix and fold in the flour, baking powder, cinnamon and almonds.
  5. Fold in the carrots and pour the dough into a greased form.
  6. Bake in the oven and, once cooled, optionally spread with cream cheese topping.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Keeps well for 2-3 days.
