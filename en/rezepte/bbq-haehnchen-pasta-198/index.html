BBQ chicken pasta

4 people · Pasta dishes

This BBQ chicken pasta is quick to make and still full of flavor. The creamy sauce combines chicken, peppers and pasta to create an everyday comfort food.

Ingredients

  • 350g penne
  • 500 g chicken breast fillet
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 200 ml cream
  • 150 ml BBQ sauce
  • 100 ml chicken broth
  • 80g grated cheddar
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 2 spring onions

Instructions

  1. Cook the penne in plenty of salted water until al dente and drain.
  2. Cut chicken into bite-sized pieces. Dice peppers and onions.
  3. Heat olive oil in a large pan and brown the chicken all over. Season with salt, pepper and paprika powder.
  4. Add onion and pepper and fry for 4 minutes.
  5. Mix the cream, BBQ sauce and broth, pour in and let it simmer slightly for 4 to 5 minutes.
  6. Stir in cheddar until sauce becomes creamy. Fold in the penne and mix everything well.
  7. Serve with finely chopped spring onions.
