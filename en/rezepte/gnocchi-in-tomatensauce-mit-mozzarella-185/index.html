Gnocchi in tomato sauce with mozzarella

4 people · Italian inspired cuisine/pasta

Gnocchi in tomato sauce with mozzarella combines soft gnocchi with a fruity sauce and melted cheese. The result is uncomplicated, filling and very suitable for quick family cooking.

Ingredients

  • 800 g gnocchi from the refrigerated shelf
  • 700 g pureed tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp Italian herbs
  • 2 balls of mozzarella
  • 40g Parmesan
  • 1 handful of basil
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Finely dice the onion and garlic. Sauté in olive oil until translucent.
  2. Add pureed tomatoes, sugar and herbs. Season with salt and pepper and simmer gently for 15 minutes.
  3. Cook the gnocchi briefly according to the instructions on the package or fry them directly in a little butter in the pan, depending on the desired texture.
  4. Add the gnocchi to the tomato sauce and mix well.
  5. Tear the mozzarella into pieces and pour over it along with the Parmesan.
  6. Cover or let it melt briefly in the oven until the mozzarella becomes soft.
  7. Serve with fresh basil.
