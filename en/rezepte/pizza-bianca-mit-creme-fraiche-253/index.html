Pizza Bianca with crème fraîche

For 2 people · Pizza & Bread / Oven & Casseroles

White pizza without tomato sauce with crème fraîche, mozzarella and red onions. Fine, spicy and something different.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 10-12 minutes at 250°C

Ingredients

  • 1 portion of classic pizza dough
  • 150 g crème fraîche
  • 125g mozzarella
  • 1 small red onion
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • 2 tbsp grated Parmesan
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • some thyme or rosemary

Instructions

  1. Roll out the pizza dough.
  2. Mix the crème fraîche with finely chopped garlic, salt and pepper.
  3. Spread thinly over the dough.
  4. Tear the mozzarella into pieces and distribute it.
  5. Cut the red onion into fine rings and place on top.
  6. Add parmesan and some thyme or rosemary on top.
  7. Bake in a hot oven.
  8. Let it rest briefly and serve.
