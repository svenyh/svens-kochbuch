Pizza & Bread

House pizza from the tin

For 4 people · Pizza & Bread / Oven & Casseroles

An uncomplicated sheet pizza for family, guests or a cozy evening at home. Easy to prepare and wonderfully suitable for everyday use.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Walking time: approx. 45-60 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 15-20 minutes at 230°C

Ingredients

  • 1 portion of classic pizza dough
  • 1 portion of pizza sauce basic recipe
  • 200 g grated mozzarella
  • 100g grated Gouda
  • 100g salami
  • 1 pepper
  • 1 small red onion
  • 150 g mushrooms

Instructions

  1. Prepare the pizza dough and put it on a baking sheet.
  2. Spread the pizza sauce evenly on top.
  3. Sprinkle with mozzarella and Gouda cheese.
  4. Spread the salami, peppers, onions and mushrooms on top.
  5. Bake in a well-preheated oven until the edge is golden brown and the cheese is nicely melted.
  6. Let it rest briefly and cut into pieces.
