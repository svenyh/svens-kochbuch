Potato casserole with sour cream, cheese & bacon

3 people · Oven dishes/side dishes

This is the classic casserole you want: softly cooked potatoes, a creamy sour cream mixture, spicy bacon and a golden cheese crust. Simple, hearty and just right when it comes out of the oven.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Oven time: approx. 40 minutes
  • Total: approx. 55 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1.1-1.2kg potatoes
  • 2 onions
  • 150 g bacon cubes
  • 200 g sour cream
  • 100 ml cream or milk
  • 150–200 g grated cheese (Gouda or Emmental)
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • nutmeg
  • Butter for the mold

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C and brush the tin with butter.
  2. Peel the potatoes and cut them into thin slices.
  3. Cut onions into fine rings.
  4. Mix the sour cream with cream or milk, salt, pepper and a little nutmeg.
  5. Layer half of the potatoes in the dish, then top with the onions and bacon.
  6. Place the remaining potatoes on top and pour the sour cream mixture evenly over it.
  7. Sprinkle with grated cheese.
  8. Bake in the oven until golden brown, about 35-40 minutes, until the potatoes are soft and the surface is nicely browned.
