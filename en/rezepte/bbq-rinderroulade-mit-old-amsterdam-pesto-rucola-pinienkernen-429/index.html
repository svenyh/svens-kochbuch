BBQ beef roulade with Old Amsterdam, pesto, arugula & pine nuts

4 people · Grill & BBQ / Meat dishes

Juicy beef roulade from the top round, filled with Old Amsterdam, green pesto, arugula and pine nuts. Seared all over, slowly roasted in the oven and beautifully pink inside – a BBQ dish with bold flavour and clear technique.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Searing: approx. 8–10 minutes
  • Oven time: approx. 45–60 minutes at 120 °C
  • Resting: 10 minutes
  • Total: approx. 85–100 minutes

Ingredients

  • approx. 900 g beef roulades from the top round (3 large pieces)
  • 150 g Old Amsterdam
  • 3 tbsp green pesto
  • 2 handfuls arugula
  • 2 tbsp pine nuts
  • salt
  • black pepper
  • a little vegetable oil

Instructions

  1. Lay out three beef roulades slightly overlapping.
  2. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Spread pesto thinly.
  4. Add Old Amsterdam.
  5. Distribute arugula and pine nuts.
  6. Roll up tightly.
  7. Tie with kitchen twine.
  8. Sear vigorously all over.
  9. Then roast at 120 °C conventional or fan until core temperature reaches 54–55 °C.
  10. Rest for 10 minutes.
  11. Remove the twine.
  12. Slice thickly and serve.
