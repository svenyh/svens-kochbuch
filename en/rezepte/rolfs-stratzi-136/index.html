Rolf's Stratzi

4 people · Oven dishes / braised dishes / home cooking

A classic, hearty braised dish with roulade meat, bacon and onions. The long cooking time and boiling the next day creates a particularly intense taste - real home cooking.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Slow cooking: approx. 3 hours on a low flame or at 150 °C top/bottom heat
  • Rest time: overnight
  • Complete the following day: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total: 2 days

Ingredients

  • Roulade meat
  • Onions
  • Bacon, diced
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • some flour
  • some fat for frying
  • 1 can of mushrooms
  • 500 ml cream

Instructions

  1. Cut the roulade meat into small strips.
  2. Fry the bacon and onions in a pan until they color.
  3. Place the meat and onion-bacon mixture in layers in an ovenproof roasting pan or heavy pot.
  4. Season each layer with salt and pepper and dust lightly with flour.
  5. Cover and cook slowly either on the lowest heat setting or in a preheated oven at 150 °C top/bottom heat for about 3 hours.
  6. The next day, bring to the boil again while stirring.
  7. Add the mushrooms and cream and heat gently for 10 minutes.
  8. Serve with potatoes, pasta or bread.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with potatoes, pasta or bread.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Fresh mushrooms can be used instead of canned mushrooms.
