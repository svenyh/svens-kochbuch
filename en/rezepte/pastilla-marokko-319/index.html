Pastilla – Morocco

4 people · Culinary World Tour / Mediterranean & North Africa

Moroccan pastilla with chicken, almonds and filo pastry – sweet and savoury and festive. Sven's version for special guest evenings.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 30 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 35 minutes
  • Total: approx. 90 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken
  • filo pastry
  • 2 onions
  • almonds
  • eggs
  • cinnamon
  • turmeric
  • ginger
  • parsley
  • coriander
  • butter
  • icing sugar optional

Instructions

  1. Braise the chicken with onions and spices, then shred.
  2. Bind the sauce with egg.
  3. Toast the almonds.
  4. Layer everything in filo pastry and bake until crisp.
