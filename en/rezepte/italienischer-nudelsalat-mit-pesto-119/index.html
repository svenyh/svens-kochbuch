Italian pasta salad with pesto

4 people · Salads / summer dishes

This pasta salad is a little fresher and more Mediterranean than the classic mayo version. Pesto, tomatoes and mozzarella ensure that it immediately tastes like summer and a barbecue evening.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 25 minutes
  • Brewing time optional: approx. 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 400 g short noodles
  • 150g mini mozzarella
  • 250 g cherry tomatoes
  • 3 tbsp green pesto
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Arugula
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta and let it cool.
  2. Halve the tomatoes and drain the mozzarella.
  3. Mix pesto with olive oil.
  4. Mix everything with the noodles and season with salt and pepper.
  5. Finally, fold in some arugula.
