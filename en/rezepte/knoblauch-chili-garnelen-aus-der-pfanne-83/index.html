Garlic chili prawns from the pan

2 people · Fish & Seafood

This is exactly the kind of shrimp dish that comes together quickly and still tastes like something special. Garlic, chili and pan heat are sufficient here if the base is right.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Roasting time: approx. 6 minutes
  • Total: approx. 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 400g prawns, raw and peeled
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 1 small red chili or chili flakes to taste
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 lemon
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: some baguette to serve

Instructions

  1. Finely chop the garlic and cut the chilli into small rings.
  2. Heat olive oil and butter in a large skillet.
  3. Sauté the garlic and chili briefly without letting them darken.
  4. Add shrimp and fry over high heat for 2 to 3 minutes per side.
  5. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Finally, fold in the lemon juice and parsley.
  7. Serve straight away hot, preferably with bread for dipping.
