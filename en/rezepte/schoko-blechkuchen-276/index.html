Chocolate sheet cake

For 1 tray · Baking & Sweets / Sheet Cakes

Juicy chocolate cake on a tray with icing. Perfect for guests, birthdays and larger gatherings.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 20-25 minutes at 180°C

Ingredients

  • 300g flour
  • 250g sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g butter
  • 3 tbsp cocoa
  • 1 packet of baking powder
  • 150 ml milk
  • 100 g dark chocolate
  • 150 g powdered sugar for the icing

Instructions

  1. Mix butter and sugar until creamy.
  2. Stir in eggs one at a time.
  3. Mix the flour, cocoa and baking powder and stir in the milk.
  4. Place the dough on a greased or lined tray.
  5. Bake in the oven.
  6. Make a icing from chocolate and powdered sugar.
  7. Frost the cake after it has cooled.
  8. Cut into pieces and serve.
