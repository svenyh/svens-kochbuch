Salmon with mustard-dill sauce

4 people · Fish & seafood

Gently baked salmon with a creamy mustard-dill sauce – a Swedish classic on the table in minutes and elegantly simple.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 800 g salmon fillet
  • 2 tbsp mustard
  • 200 ml cream
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Season the salmon.
  2. Bake for about 20 minutes at 180 °C.
  3. Whisk cream, mustard and dill.
  4. Warm briefly.
  5. Pour over the salmon.
