Blueberry Lemon Agua Fresca White Wine Spritz

1 glass · Summer horchatas & aguas frescas

Light summer aperitif: Blueberry Lemon Agua Fresca White Wine Spritz. Well-chilled dry white wine and mineral water make a fresh summer spritz.

Timing

  • Prep time: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 120 ml Blueberry Lemon Agua Fresca
  • 120 ml well-chilled dry white wine
  • 30–40 ml mineral water
  • Ice cubes
  • lemon slice and fresh blueberries

Instructions

  1. Chill the base drink (Blueberry Lemon Agua Fresca) and alcohol well.
  2. Fill a large wine glass with ice cubes.
  3. Pour 120 ml Blueberry Lemon Agua Fresca first.
  4. Add 120 ml white wine and 30–40 ml mineral water.
  5. Stir gently, garnish with matching fruit and serve immediately.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

The Blueberry-Lemon Agua Fresca can chill the day before; add dry white wine and mineral water when you are ready to serve.

How long does it keep?

Mixed spritz tastes best fresh and keeps in the fridge only briefly.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Another dry sparkling wine works instead of dry white wine in the same ratio.
