Gratin Dauphinois

4 people · Side dishes / Oven dishes

Potato gratin from the Dauphiné – thinly sliced, covered with cream and baked slowly. Creamy, hearty and the perfect side for roasts or braises.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 60 minutes
  • Total: approx. 80 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg potatoes
  • 400 ml cream
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • nutmeg
  • salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Peel and slice the potatoes thinly.
  2. Season with garlic, salt, pepper and nutmeg.
  3. Layer in a baking dish.
  4. Pour over the cream.
  5. Bake for 60 minutes at 180 °C.
