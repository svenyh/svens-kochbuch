Polish beefsteak

4 people · Stir-fries/meat dishes

Polish beefsteak is down-to-earth, spicy and quick to make. What is important is a well-seasoned meat mixture and a shape that cooks evenly in the pan so that the beef steaks retain color on the outside and juiciness on the inside.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Roast: approx. 10 minutes
  • Total: approx. 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g minced beef
  • 1 onion
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp mustard
  • 1 tbsp breadcrumbs
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Oil or butter for frying

Instructions

  1. Dice the onion finely.
  2. Mix ground beef with onion, egg, mustard, breadcrumbs, salt and pepper.
  3. Form flat beef steaks.
  4. Heat some oil or butter in a pan.
  5. Fry the beef steaks over medium to high heat until brown on both sides until cooked through.
  6. Let it rest briefly and serve.
