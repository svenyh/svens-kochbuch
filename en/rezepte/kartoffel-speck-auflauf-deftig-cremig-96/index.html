Potato and bacon casserole (hearty & creamy)

3-4 people · Oven dishes / home cooking

If you want something really hearty, you've come to the right place. The potatoes become soft, the bacon adds flavor and a nice, golden crust forms on top. This is not an easy dish - but that's exactly why it's so good.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 45-55 minutes
  • Total: approx. 70 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1.2kg potatoes
  • 150 g bacon cubes
  • 2 onions
  • 200 g sour cream
  • 150 ml cream
  • 150-200g grated cheese
  • Salt, pepper, nutmeg
  • Butter for the mold

Instructions

  1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into thin slices.
  2. Dice onions and fry briefly with the bacon.
  3. Mix the sour cream and cream and season with salt, pepper and nutmeg.
  4. Layer potatoes, bacon and onions in a buttered baking dish.
  5. Pour the sour cream mixture over it and sprinkle with cheese.
  6. Bake in the oven at 180°C for about 45-55 minutes until the potatoes are soft and the surface is golden brown.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Goes well with green salad.
