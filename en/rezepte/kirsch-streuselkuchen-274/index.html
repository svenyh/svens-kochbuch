Cherry crumble cake

For 1 springform pan or small casserole dish · Baking & Sweets / Cakes

Fruity cake with cherries and crunchy sprinkles. Simple, juicy and very good for afternoon coffee.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 40-45 minutes at 180°C

Ingredients

  • 1 glass of sour cherries
  • 250g flour
  • 125g butter
  • 100g sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 packet of vanilla sugar
  • 150 g flour for the crumbles
  • 80 g sugar for the sprinkles
  • 100 g butter for the crumbles

Instructions

  1. Drain the sour cherries well.
  2. Knead a dough from flour, butter, sugar, egg, baking powder and vanilla sugar.
  3. Press into the greased form.
  4. Spread the cherries on top.
  5. Make streusel from flour, sugar and butter.
  6. Pour over the cherries.
  7. Bake in the oven until the streusel is golden brown.
  8. Let cool and serve.
