Sven's BBQ Rub (basic seasoning)

stock · Basics / Grill & BBQ

You just love having a spice base like this in the house. Quickly mixed, versatile and just right if you want meat or vegetables to taste like they've been grilled without much effort.

Timing

  • Mix: 5 minutes
  • Filling: 2 minutes
  • Allow to soak in before cooking: ideally 20-60 minutes
  • Store dry and airtight

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp sweet paprika powder
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tbsp pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper, optional

Instructions

  1. Place all dry ingredients in a bowl and mix thoroughly until the color and texture are uniform.
  2. Strain the mixture once through a fine sieve or crush any lumps with your fingers.
  3. Pour into a dry screw-top jar or spice jar and label.
  4. Before using, lightly oil meat or vegetables and massage the rub in evenly.
  5. For the oven or grill, ideally apply the rub 20 to 60 minutes before cooking.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Stored in a dry, dark place, the mixture will last for several weeks.

Can I substitute ingredients?

For a milder version, omit the cayenne pepper.
