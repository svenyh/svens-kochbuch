Grilled baguette with steak strips, onions and cheese

4 people · Grill &amp; BBQ

Filled grilled baguette with steak strips, onions, peppers and cheese. Easy to prepare and ideal for a barbecue or party.

Timing

  • Preparation: 20 minutes | Grilling time/baking: 15-20 minutes | Total time: approx. 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 large baguettes or 4 small baguette rolls
  • 500 g beef steak, minute steaks or sirloin steak
  • 2 large onions
  • 1 pepper
  • 150-200g grated cheese
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1 teaspoon steak seasoning or salt and pepper
  • 2 tbsp crème fraîche or sour cream
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • optional: jalapeños
  • optional: spring onions

Instructions

  1. Cut steak into thin strips.
  2. Cut onions and peppers into strips.
  3. Season meat with oil, paprika, steak seasoning, salt and pepper.
  4. Sear on the griddle or in the grill pan.
  5. Add onions and peppers and fry.
  6. Cut the baguettes lengthways.
  7. Mix the sour cream or crème fraîche with mustard and spread on the baguettes.
  8. Spread the meat and vegetable mixture on top.
  9. Sprinkle with cheese.
  10. Bake on the grill over indirect heat or in the oven until the cheese is melted.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

Instead of beef, you can also use chicken or pork neck.
