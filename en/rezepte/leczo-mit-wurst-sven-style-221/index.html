Leczo with sausage Sven style

4 people · Pan-fried dishes / quick cuisine

A rustic leczo with peppers, zucchini and spicy sausage. Hearty, aromatic and ideal for everyday life.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 3 peppers of different colors
  • 2 onions
  • 2 zucchini
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 300 g Cabanossi or Krakauer
  • 1 can chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • some chili to taste

Instructions

  1. Cut the onions, peppers and zucchini into bite-sized pieces and slice the sausage.
  2. Heat oil in a large pot and fry the sausage briefly, then remove.
  3. Sauté onions and garlic, add peppers and zucchini and fry.
  4. Roast the tomato paste briefly, then add the chopped tomatoes and spices.
  5. Put the sausage back in and let everything stew gently for 20-25 minutes.
  6. Season to taste and serve hot.
