Airfryer hot dogs with cheddar, cucumber and fried onions

2 people · Airfryer / Quick kitchen

These air fryer hot dogs are quick to make, beautifully cheesy and perfect for an uncomplicated evening. The rolls are first toasted with ketchup and cheddar, then the sausage with extra cheese is added. Finally, the hot dogs are freshly topped with cucumbers, fried onions, mustard and ketchup.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Airfryer first round: 2-3 minutes
  • Airfryer second round: 3-4 minutes
  • Total: approx. 15-18 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 hot dog buns
  • 4 hot dog sausages
  • 4-6 tbsp ketchup
  • 4 slices cheddar or grated cheddar
  • 2-3 gherkins
  • 4 tbsp fried onions
  • Mustard to taste
  • optional extra ketchup for serving
  • optionally some grated cheese for gratinating

Instructions

  1. Cut the hot dog bun lengthways, but don't cut it all the way through.
  2. Spread a thin layer of ketchup on the inside of the buns on the left and right.
  3. Place cheddar in the buns or add grated cheddar.
  4. Place the prepared rolls in the hot air fryer at 180°C for about 2-3 minutes until the cheese melts slightly.
  5. Place the hot dog sausages in the buns.
  6. If desired, add a little more cheddar cheese over the sausages.
  7. Put the hot dogs back in the air fryer at 180°C for about 3-4 minutes, until the sausages are hot and the cheese has melted.
  8. Remove the hot dogs and top with cucumber slices, fried onions, mustard and a little ketchup if desired.
  9. Serve immediately warm.
