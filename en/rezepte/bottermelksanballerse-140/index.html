Bottermelksanballerse

4 people · Wölpinghausen specialties / sweet dishes

Bottermelksanballerse are exactly the kind of regional dessert that is far too rarely seen today. They appear simple, but have a pleasant freshness and fit perfectly into a Wölpinghausen block with real village and family recipes.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 15-20 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes
  • Best served fresh and still slightly warm

Ingredients

  • 500 ml buttermilk
  • 150g flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 40g sugar
  • 1 packet of vanilla sugar
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • Butter or oil for baking
  • optional: cinnamon sugar for sprinkling

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, eggs, sugar, vanilla sugar and salt into a smooth dough.
  2. Gradually incorporate the buttermilk until you have a creamy, slightly flowing dough.
  3. Heat a pan with a little butter or oil.
  4. Pour in the dough in portions and bake small balls or thicker cakes.
  5. Cook until golden on both sides and drain briefly on kitchen paper.
  6. Serve warm and sprinkle with cinnamon sugar if desired.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

It's better to keep the heat medium so that the Ballerse don't get too dark on the outside and raw on the inside.
