Farmer's omelette with tomato and mozzarella salad

2-3 people · Stir-fries/egg dishes

Farmer's omelette with tomato and mozzarella salad is quick to make and yet pleasantly complete. Warm eggs, hearty potatoes and a fresh salad together ensure a well-rounded meal without unnecessary effort.

Timing

  • Preparation: 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 6 eggs
  • 500 g potatoes, cooked
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tbsp oil or butter
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 2 balls of mozzarella
  • 1 small onion or red onion
  • 2 spring onions
  • some parsley
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp light balsamic vinegar or lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Cut potatoes into slices.
  2. Dice onion and fry in the pan.
  3. Add potatoes and fry.
  4. Whisk eggs, season and pour over.
  5. Let it set and cook carefully until finished.
  6. Cut tomatoes and mozzarella.
  7. Mix with onions, spring onions and parsley.
  8. Dress with oil and a little acid.
  9. Do not use feta.
