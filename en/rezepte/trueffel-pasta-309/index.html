Truffle pasta

2 people · Pasta dishes / Quick & easy

Not a simple Parmesan pasta, but creamy pasta with truffle oil or truffle butter as a clear specialty. Parmesan, pepper and pasta water are enough for the sauce - quick to make, but with this typical truffle aroma it is much more special than a classic cheese dish.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 5 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 12 minutes
  • Total: approx. 17 minutes

Ingredients

  • 200 g pasta, e.g. E.g. tagliatelle or spaghetti
  • 40 g Parmesan, finely grated
  • 1-2 tbsp truffle oil or 1 tbsp truffle butter
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Salt
  • black pepper
  • some pasta water
  • optional: 1 tbsp parsley
  • optional: some grated truffles

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta in plenty of salted water until al dente.
  2. Reserve some pasta water.
  3. Melt butter in a large skillet over gentle heat.
  4. Stir in truffle oil or truffle butter.
  5. Drain the pasta and add it directly to the pan.
  6. Mix with 2-3 tablespoons of pasta water and Parmesan to make a creamy sauce.
  7. Season with freshly ground pepper.
  8. Serve immediately, optionally with parsley and a little extra truffle oil.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?
