Rhubarb crumble with vanilla ice cream

4-6 people · Baking & Sweets / Cakes

Tart rhubarb under crispy butter crumbles - this dessert is just right in spring and summer when the stalks are fresh. Served warm from the oven with cold vanilla ice cream, it becomes an uncomplicated classic for coffee and guests.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 35-40 minutes
  • Total: approx. 55 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600 g rhubarb
  • 80g sugar
  • 1 tsp cornstarch
  • 150g flour
  • 100g sugar
  • 100 g cold butter
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • optional: vanilla ice cream for serving
  • optional: some lemon zest

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C top/bottom heat.
  2. Clean the rhubarb, cut it into approx. 2 cm pieces and mix with 80 g sugar and cornstarch.
  3. Pour the rhubarb mixture into a greased baking dish.
  4. Mix flour, 100 g sugar, cinnamon and salt.
  5. Add butter in flakes and rub with your fingers to form coarse crumbles.
  6. Sprinkle the streusel evenly over the rhubarb.
  7. Bake for 35-40 minutes until the rhubarb is soft and the streusel is golden brown.
  8. Serve warm with vanilla ice cream.
