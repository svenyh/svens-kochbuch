Danzig porcelain

4 people · Pastries & Sweets / Polish cuisine

Danziger Porzeln are a traditional pastry that tastes best when freshly baked. Soft on the inside, slightly golden brown on the outside and with a little sugar if desired - it's exactly this simple style that makes them so pleasant and typical.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Walking time: approx. 60 minutes
  • Bake: approx. 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 30g yeast
  • 500g flour
  • 100g butter
  • 100g sugar
  • 250 ml milk
  • 100g raisins
  • Oil or fat for frying

Instructions

  1. Warm the milk slightly and dissolve the yeast in it.
  2. Place flour, sugar and softened butter in a bowl.
  3. Add the yeast milk and knead everything into a smooth yeast dough.
  4. Let the dough rise, covered, in a warm place for about 1 hour.
  5. Fold the raisins into the risen dough.
  6. Use two spoons to scoop out small portions and shape them carefully.
  7. Fry in portions until golden brown in hot fat.
  8. Drain on kitchen paper and best serve warm.
