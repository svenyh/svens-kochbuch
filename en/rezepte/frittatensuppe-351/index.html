Frittatensuppe

4 people · Soups

Clear beef broth with fine pancake strips – a light, elegant soup from Viennese cuisine, perfect as a starter or light lunch.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 5 minutes
  • Total: approx. 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 litre beef broth
  • 2 pancakes
  • chives

Instructions

  1. Cut the pancakes into strips.
  2. Heat the broth.
  3. Add the strips.
  4. Serve with chives.
