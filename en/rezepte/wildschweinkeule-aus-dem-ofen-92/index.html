Wild boar leg from the oven

4-6 people · Oven dishes/holiday meals

For me, a wild boar leg is exactly the kind of roast that remains special without being overly complicated. When it cooks slowly, the meat becomes beautifully tender and the sauce has that rich, festive depth.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 25 minutes
  • Oven time: approx. 2.5-3 hours
  • Total: approximately 3 hours 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg wild boar leg
  • 2 onions
  • 2 carrots
  • 150 g celeriac
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 400 ml red wine
  • 400 ml stock
  • 2 sprigs of rosemary
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Salt and pepper the wild boar leg and fry it all over.
  2. Roughly chop the vegetables and roast them briefly.
  3. Deglaze with red wine and stock.
  4. Add rosemary and bay leaf.
  5. Cook in a preheated oven at 160°C for about 2.5 to 3 hours.
  6. Pour the pan juices over them occasionally.
  7. Let it rest briefly before cutting.
