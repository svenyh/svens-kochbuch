Smashed burger with baked potatoes & sour cream dip

2-3 people · Grill & BBQ

A good smashed burger depends on crust, juiciness and clear components that work together immediately. With baked potatoes and sour cream, it becomes a well-rounded burger meal that looks like a weekend, a grill plate and a real appetite.

Timing

  • Preparation: 15 minutes
  • Oven: 30-35 minutes
  • Pan: 10 minutes
  • Total: approx. 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef (approx. 20% fat)
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 slices of cheddar
  • 1 large tomato
  • 4-6 leaves of lettuce
  • 4-6 slices of pickled cucumbers
  • 1 red onion
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 700g potatoes
  • 2-3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 200 g sour cream or sour cream
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • optional: fresh herbs

Instructions

  1. Wash the potatoes, cut into wedges and mix with oil and spices.
  2. Place on a baking tray and bake at 200 °C for approx. 30-35 minutes.
  3. Form the minced meat into 4 balls and do not season beforehand.
  4. Heat the pan or griddle to high heat.
  5. Place the minced meat balls in the pan and press them flat (smash).
  6. Season with salt and pepper and fry vigorously for 2-3 minutes.
  7. Turn over, place cheese on top and let it melt briefly.
  8. Halve the burger buns and fry briefly.
  9. Top the burger with lettuce, tomato, onion and cucumber.
  10. Mix the sour cream with lemon juice and spices to make a dip and serve with the baked potatoes.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Mix the sour cream with lemon juice and spices to make a dip and serve with the baked potatoes.
