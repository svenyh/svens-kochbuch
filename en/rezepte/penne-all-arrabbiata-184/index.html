Penne all'Arrabbiata

4 people · Italian inspired cuisine/pasta

Penne all'Arrabbiata is spicy, clear and pleasantly direct in taste. A good choice for days when you need a quick pasta dish with a bit of heat.

Ingredients

  • 400g penne
  • 800 g diced tomatoes
  • 4 cloves of garlic
  • 1 small onion
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 to 2 red chilies
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 handful of parsley
  • 40g Parmesan, optional
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Finely chop the onion, garlic and chilli.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large pan. Sauté the onion and garlic until translucent, briefly fry the chili.
  3. Add tomatoes and season with sugar, salt and pepper.
  4. Let the sauce simmer uncovered for 20 minutes.
  5. Meanwhile, cook the penne in salted water until al dente.
  6. Drain the pasta and add it directly to the sauce. Mix well.
  7. Serve with chopped parsley and, if desired, Parmesan cheese.
