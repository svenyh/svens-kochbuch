Shish kebab skewers with paprika sauce and lecho

4 people · Grill / BBQ / Eastern Europe

This variant combines the classic shish kebab skewer with paprika sauce and lecho much more completely. This is exactly why the dish not only looks colorful, but also technically more rounded: the skewers add a roasted aroma, paprika sauce ensures binding and the Letscho provides the appropriate sweet and spicy vegetable base.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Marinate: approx. 2-3 hours
  • Grilling time: approx. 18-22 minutes over medium heat
  • Sauce in parallel: approx. 10 minutes
  • Rest time: 3 minutes
  • Total without marinade: approx. 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1kg pork
  • 2 peppers
  • 2 onions
  • 4 tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp paprika powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 250 g lecho
  • 200 ml paprika sauce
  • optional: 1 clove of garlic

Instructions

  1. Cut the pork into cubes and marinate with oil, paprika powder, salt, pepper and optionally garlic.
  2. Cut the peppers and onions into large pieces and put them on skewers along with the meat.
  3. Cook the skewers on the grill or in a grill pan over medium heat, turning regularly.
  4. At the same time, heat the paprika sauce and lecho together in a small pot and let it simmer gently for a few minutes.
  5. Season the sauce and, if necessary, bring it to the desired consistency with a little water or broth.
  6. Either nap the finished skewers directly with the sauce or serve the paprika and lecho sauce separately.
  7. Serve with bread, rice or baked potatoes.
