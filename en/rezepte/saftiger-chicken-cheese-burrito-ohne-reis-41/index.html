Juicy chicken cheese burrito without rice

4 people · Fast cuisine

This burrito is strong, juicy and very useful in everyday life because it seems full and rounded even without rice. Spicy chicken, melted cheese and a good filling make this a quick after-work meal that still tastes like real food.

Timing

  • Total time: 25 minutes
  • Preparation: 10 minutes
  • Pan time: 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600-700 g shredded chicken
  • 2 peppers
  • 2 onions
  • 1 large zucchini
  • 2 cans of beans
  • 200-250 g grated cheese
  • 2-3 tbsp yogurt or sour cream
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 1-2 tbsp oil
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • ½ tsp chili
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Peel onions and cut into strips. Cut peppers and zucchini into small pieces. Rinse and drain beans.
  2. Heat the oil in a large pan and fry the chicken strips vigorously.
  3. Add onions, peppers and zucchini and fry for 3-4 minutes.
  4. Stir in beans, paprika, cumin, chili, salt and pepper.
  5. Reduce the heat and stir the grated cheese into the filling along with the yogurt or sour cream until everything is creamy and juicy.
  6. Warm the tortillas briefly so that they become soft and pliable.
  7. Place some filling in the middle of each tortilla as an elongated strip.
  8. Fold in the sides on the left and right, then roll them up tightly from bottom to top.
  9. Optionally, toast the burritos in a dry pan for 2-3 minutes per side.
