Wrap evening (DIY style)

4 people · Fast cuisine

A DIY-style wrap evening brings movement and conviviality to the table without getting complicated. Everyone puts together their own food, and that's exactly what makes this format so everyday and relaxed.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Warm filling: approx. 15 minutes
  • Heat wraps: 1-2 minutes
  • Total: approx. 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 8 large wraps
  • 500 g chicken or minced meat
  • 1 iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 cucumber
  • 1 red onion
  • Corn as desired
  • 2-3 sauces, e.g. B. Tzatziki, aioli, salsa
  • oil
  • Spices to taste

Instructions

  1. Prepare meat or a vegetarian filling, fry it and season appropriately.
  2. Pour vegetables, salad, cheese and sauces into small bowls and place on the table.
  3. Heat the wraps briefly in the pan, microwave or oven so that they are soft and rollable.
  4. Everyone covers their wrap first with sauce, then with warm contents and finally with fresh ingredients.
  5. Fold in the sides and roll or fold the wrap tightly.
  6. Serve straight away or briefly fry the finished wraps in the pan.
