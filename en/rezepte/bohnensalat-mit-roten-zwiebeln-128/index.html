Bean salad with red onions

3-4 people · Salads/side dishes

Bean salad is one of those underrated classics that goes perfectly with grilled meat or bratwurst. With red onions and a clear dressing, it remains simple but very effective.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Brewing time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cans of green beans
  • 1 red onion
  • 3 tbsp vinegar
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Savory or parsley

Instructions

  1. Drain beans.
  2. Cut the onion into fine half rings.
  3. Mix vinegar, oil, sugar, salt and pepper.
  4. Mix with beans and onion.
  5. Finish with herbs and let steep.
