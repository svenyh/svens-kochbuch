Old Polish sauce

For multiple servings · Sauces / Polish cuisine

This old Polish sauce is not an afterthought, but rather a base that really brings other dishes together. If onions and flour are processed properly and the liquid is stirred in smoothly, it becomes round, flavorful and versatile.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 10 minutes
  • Total: approx. 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 onion
  • 1 tablespoon butter or fat
  • 1 tbsp flour
  • 250 ml broth
  • 100 ml cream
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Marjoram or other spices to taste

Instructions

  1. Dice the onion very finely.
  2. Heat butter or fat in a small pot and slowly sauté the onion.
  3. Add flour and sauté briefly without letting it get too dark.
  4. Pour in the broth while stirring so that there are no lumps.
  5. Add the cream and let the sauce simmer gently for a few minutes.
  6. Season with salt, pepper and spices to taste.
  7. If necessary, strain through a sieve or leave to rusticate.
