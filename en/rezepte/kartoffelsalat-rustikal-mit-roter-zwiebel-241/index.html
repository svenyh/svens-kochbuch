Rustic potato salad with red onion

4 people · Salads & side dishes / classics & home cooking

A down-to-earth potato salad with broth, mustard and pickles. Tastes very good lukewarm and cold.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Brewing time: approx. 20-30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 kg waxy potatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 4 pickles
  • 150 ml vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp mustard
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp cucumber water
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • some parsley or chives

Instructions

  1. Boil the potatoes in their skins, let them cool and peel.
  2. Slice.
  3. Finely dice the onion and chop the cucumber into small pieces.
  4. Mix the broth with mustard, oil, cucumber water, salt and pepper.
  5. Mix with potatoes, onions and cucumbers.
  6. Let it steep briefly and sprinkle with herbs before serving.
