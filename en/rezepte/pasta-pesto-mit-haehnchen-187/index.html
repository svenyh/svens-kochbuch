Pasta pesto with chicken

4 people · Italian inspired cuisine/pasta

Pasta pesto with chicken cooks quickly and is still aromatic enough not to seem random. Pesto, chicken and pasta together make an uncomplicated main dish that is very suitable for everyday use.

Ingredients

  • 350 g pasta of your choice
  • 500 g chicken breast fillet
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 80g green pesto
  • 150 ml cream
  • 40g Parmesan
  • 1 handful of cherry tomatoes
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta in salted water until al dente.
  2. Cut the chicken into strips and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan and fry the chicken until golden brown.
  4. Halve the cherry tomatoes and briefly add them to the pan.
  5. Stir in the pesto and cream and heat briefly, do not boil heavily.
  6. Add parmesan and season the sauce to taste.
  7. Drain the pasta, mix with the sauce and serve immediately.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Only heat the pesto gently to keep the aroma fresh.
