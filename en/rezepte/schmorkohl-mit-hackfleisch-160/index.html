Braised cabbage with minced meat

4 people · Classic recipes / classics & home cooking

Braised cabbage is a real classic everyday dish: simple, cheap, filling and still really tasty when cabbage, onions and minced meat slowly braise together. It is precisely this simple kitchen logic that makes the dish so strong.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Fry and start: approx. 15 minutes
  • Braising time: approx. 30-40 minutes
  • Total: approx. 1 hour

Ingredients

  • 1 small white cabbage, approx. 1 kg
  • 500g mixed minced meat
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tbsp oil or lard
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 200 ml vegetable or meat broth
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder
  • 1 pinch of caraway seeds, optional
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 1 small pinch of sugar
  • To serve: boiled potatoes or mashed potatoes

Instructions

  1. Quarter the white cabbage, remove the stalk and cut the cabbage into fine strips. Dice onions.
  2. Fry the minced meat in hot oil in a large pot or roaster until crumbly and vigorous.
  3. Add onions and fry until translucent. Stir in tomato paste and roast briefly.
  4. Add the cabbage little by little and let it collapse again and again. This takes about 8 to 10 minutes.
  5. Pour in the broth, add paprika powder and a little caraway if desired.
  6. Cover and simmer everything over a low to medium heat for 30 to 40 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  7. Finally, season with salt, pepper and a small pinch of sugar and serve with potatoes.
