Chicken quesadillas

4 people · Quick weeknight cooking

Tortillas filled with chicken and cheese, fried golden in a pan – quick, hearty and perfect for weeknights.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 8 tortillas
  • 400 g chicken
  • 250 g cheese
  • bell pepper

Instructions

  1. Fill the tortillas.
  2. Fold in half.
  3. Fry golden in a pan.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with salsa and guacamole.
