Mezze Evening – Lebanon

4 people · Culinary World Tour / Mediterranean & North Africa

Lebanese mezze evening with hummus, baba ganoush, tabbouleh and flatbread – Sven's platter cooking for summer evenings and guests. Variety instead of a single dish.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 45 minutes
  • Total: approx. 45–60 minutes

Ingredients

  • hummus
  • baba ganoush
  • tabbouleh
  • flatbread
  • olives
  • yoghurt dip
  • grilled vegetables
  • köfte optional

Instructions

  1. Blend hummus from chickpeas, tahini, lemon and garlic.
  2. Roast the aubergine and turn it into baba ganoush.
  3. Mix tabbouleh with parsley, bulgur, tomato and lemon.
  4. Arrange everything on platters.
