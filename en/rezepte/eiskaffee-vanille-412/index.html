Vanilla Iced Coffee

approx. 2 glasses · Summer drinks, lemonades & mocktails

A creamy vanilla iced coffee – mild, cooling, and perfect for coffee lovers on warm days.

Timing

  • Prep time: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 300 ml cold coffee
  • 250 ml milk
  • vanilla extract
  • ice cubes
  • optional: vanilla ice cream

Instructions

  1. Stir together the coffee and milk.
  2. Add vanilla.
  3. Add ice cubes.
  4. Optionally add vanilla ice cream.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?
