Vegetable tofu wok with sesame

4 people · Asian wok / wok dishes

Vegetable tofu wok with sesame is a fresh, vegetable wok dish with crispy tofu and a slightly sweet and spicy soy-sesame sauce. The broccoli, peppers and snow peas give the dish a lot of color and bite.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Fry tofu: about 8 minutes
  • Vegetables and sauce: approx. 10 minutes
  • Total: approx. 38 minutes

Ingredients

  • 400 g firm natural tofu
  • 1 broccoli
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 yellow pepper
  • 2 carrots
  • 150 g sugar snap peas
  • 4 spring onions
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 piece of ginger, approx. 20 g
  • 4 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • For the sauce: 4 tablespoons soy sauce
  • For the sauce: 2 tablespoons maple syrup or honey
  • For the sauce: 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • For the sauce: 1 teaspoon sesame oil
  • For the sauce: 1 teaspoon chili or sambal oelek
  • For the sauce: 80 ml water

Instructions

  1. Squeeze out the tofu well and cut it into cubes. Dust with starch.
  2. Divide broccoli into small florets. Cut the peppers and carrots into fine pieces. Cut spring onions into rings.
  3. Mix the sauce from soy sauce, maple syrup, rice vinegar, sesame oil, chili and water.
  4. Heat wok. Add 2 tablespoons of oil and fry the tofu until crispy all over. Take out.
  5. Add remaining oil to the wok. Briefly fry the garlic and ginger.
  6. Fry broccoli, carrots and peppers over high heat for 4 minutes.
  7. Add snow peas and spring onions.
  8. Put the tofu back in the wok, pour on the sauce and let it simmer briefly until glazed.
  9. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and serve.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

Using cashews instead of sesame gives the dish a different twist.
