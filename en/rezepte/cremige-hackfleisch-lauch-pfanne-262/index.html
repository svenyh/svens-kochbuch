Creamy minced meat and leek pan

For 3 people · Quick cuisine / stir-fry dishes

Quick pan with minced meat, leeks and cream cheese. Hearty, creamy and very suitable for everyday use.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 12 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 18 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500g minced meat
  • 2 stalks of leeks
  • 1 onion
  • 200 g cream cheese
  • 150 ml broth
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • nutmeg

Instructions

  1. Dice the onion and cut the leek into rings.
  2. Heat oil in a pan.
  3. Fry the minced meat vigorously.
  4. Add onion and fry briefly.
  5. Add leek and sauté until soft.
  6. Pour in the broth.
  7. Stir in cream cheese and let it melt.
  8. Season with salt, pepper and a little nutmeg.
  9. Let it simmer briefly and serve.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with pasta, rice or potatoes.
