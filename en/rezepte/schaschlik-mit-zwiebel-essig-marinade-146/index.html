Shish kebab with onion and vinegar marinade

4 people · Grill / Eastern Europe / Classic

The onion and vinegar marinade brings exactly that classic, slightly bitter note that many people like in shish kebab from Eastern Europe. The recipe seems simple, but works very well if you give the meat enough time in the marinade.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Marinate: at least 4 hours, ideally overnight
  • Grilling or roasting time: approx. 18-22 minutes over medium heat
  • Rest time: 3 minutes
  • Total without marinade: approx. 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1kg pork
  • 4 large onions
  • 3 tablespoons neutral vinegar or white wine vinegar
  • 4 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • optional: 1 bay leaf, finely crushed

Instructions

  1. Cut pork into even cubes.
  2. Cut onions into rings or half rings and mix with vinegar, oil, salt, pepper and paprika powder.
  3. Press the onions lightly so that they draw juice and release aroma.
  4. Mix the meat thoroughly with the marinade and let it steep for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight.
  5. Place the meat and some of the onions on skewers or use as a grill pan variant.
  6. Grill or fry over medium heat until the meat is cooked through and nicely toasted on the outside.
  7. Serve with bread, paprika sauce or mustard if desired.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

The onion-vinegar marinade should rest at least 4 hours, ideally overnight in the fridge.

How long does it keep?

Raw marinated meat keeps 1 day in the fridge; grilled leftovers about 2 days.

What side dish goes well?

Pairs well with pepper salad, flatbread and sharp mustard or ajvar sauce.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Lamb or chicken works instead of pork – marinate a little shorter then.
