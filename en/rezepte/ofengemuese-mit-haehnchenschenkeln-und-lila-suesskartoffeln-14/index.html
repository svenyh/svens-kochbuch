Oven vegetables with chicken thighs and purple sweet potatoes

3-4 people · Oven dishes / chicken dishes

This sheet pan dish depends on everything working in the oven at the same time. The vegetables become soft and aromatic, the chicken legs add spice to the tray and the purple sweet potatoes make the whole thing particularly harmonious in terms of appearance and taste.

Timing

  • Preparation: 15 minutes
  • Oven time: 45-50 minutes
  • Total: approx. 60-65 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken legs
  • 2 purple sweet potatoes
  • 2 peppers
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 onion
  • 1 zucchini
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Paprika powder
  • optional: garlic and rosemary

Instructions

  1. Cut vegetables and sweet potatoes into rough pieces.
  2. Season chicken thighs.
  3. Put everything on a tray or in a large oven dish.
  4. Mix with oil and spices.
  5. Cook at 190-200°C for approx. 45-50 minutes.
  6. Check the chicken at the end until the skin is nicely browned.
  7. Serve immediately hot.
