Schnitzel Viennese style

4 people · Classics & home cooking

A good Viennese-style schnitzel is crispy on the outside, juicy on the inside and that's exactly why it's a real enduring classic. With the loose breading and a little lemon, that typical Sunday feeling immediately comes to the plate.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Roasting time: approx. 4-6 minutes over medium to medium-high heat
  • Rest time: 1 minute
  • Total time: approx. 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 pork schnitzels
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 2 eggs
  • 4 tbsp flour
  • 8 tbsp breadcrumbs
  • Butter or oil for baking
  • 1 lemon

Instructions

  1. Pat the schnitzel thinly between foil and season with salt and pepper.
  2. Place the flour, beaten eggs and breadcrumbs on each plate.
  3. Dip the schnitzel first in flour, then egg and finally in breadcrumbs, without pressing the breading firmly.
  4. Bring the clarified butter to medium to medium-high heat in a large skillet.
  5. Fry the schnitzels for 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden brown and swirl the pan slightly.
  6. Drain on kitchen paper and let rest for 1 minute.
  7. Serve with lemon wedges and a suitable side dish.
