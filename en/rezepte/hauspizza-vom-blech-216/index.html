Pizza

House pizza from the tin

4 people · Pizza / oven dishes

An uncomplicated sheet pizza for family or guests. It has a generous filling, is quickly portioned and is perfect for a relaxed pizza evening.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Walking time: approx. 45-60 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 15-20 minutes at 230°C

Ingredients

  • 500 g wheat flour type 550
  • 1 cube of fresh yeast
  • 325 ml lukewarm water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 can chopped tomatoes (400 g)
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 200 g grated mozzarella
  • 100g grated Gouda
  • 100g salami
  • 1 pepper
  • 1 small red onion
  • 150 g mushrooms

Instructions

  1. Make the yeast dough as with the basic recipe and let it rise.
  2. For the sauce, mix tomatoes, tomato paste, garlic, oregano, salt, pepper and sugar.
  3. Pull the dough onto a lined tray and spread the sauce evenly on top.
  4. Top with cheese, salami, peppers, onions and mushrooms.
  5. Bake in a well preheated oven until golden brown.
  6. Let it rest briefly and cut into pieces.
