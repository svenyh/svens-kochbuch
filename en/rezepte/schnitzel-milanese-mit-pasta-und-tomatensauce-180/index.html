Schnitzel Milanese with pasta and tomato sauce

4 people · Italian inspired cuisine / pasta & meat dishes

Schnitzel Milanese with pasta and tomato sauce combines crispy breaded meat with an uncomplicated Italian-inspired pasta plate. A hearty main course that seems familiar but still feels a little more special on the table.

Ingredients

  • 4 pork schnitzels or veal schnitzels, approx. 160 g each
  • 250g spaghetti
  • 800 g pureed tomatoes
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 small onion
  • 80 g Parmesan, finely grated
  • 100 g breadcrumbs
  • 60g flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 150 ml sunflower oil for baking
  • 1 ball of mozzarella, optional
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 handful of basil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 1 tbsp butter

Instructions

  1. Finely dice the onion and garlic. Heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil in a pot, sauté the onion and garlic until translucent, then add the pureed tomatoes.
  2. Stir in sugar, oregano, salt and pepper and simmer the sauce gently over low heat for 20 to 25 minutes. Finally, finely chop some of the basil and stir it in.
  3. Cook the spaghetti in plenty of salted water until al dente, drain and mix loosely with 1 tablespoon of butter and 1 tablespoon of olive oil.
  4. Carefully flatten the schnitzels between cling film. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Place flour on a plate. Whisk eggs in a deep plate. Mix breadcrumbs with 40 g of Parmesan.
  6. First coat the schnitzels in the flour, then pull them through the egg and then coat them evenly in the Parmesan breading. Only press the breading lightly.
  7. Heat the sunflower oil in a large pan. Fry the schnitzels over medium to medium-high heat for 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden brown.
  8. Place the schnitzels briefly on kitchen paper. If you want the gratinated version, spread some tomato sauce and some mozzarella on the schnitzels and gratinate them for 5 minutes at 220 °C top/bottom heat.
  9. Serve the spaghetti with tomato sauce, serve the schnitzel next to it or lightly on top and finish with the remaining Parmesan and fresh basil.
