Grilled corn cobs with butter

3-4 people · Side dishes / grill & BBQ

Grilled corn on the cob is one of those side dishes that is almost always a hit because it's both simple and very satisfying. With butter it becomes juicy, slightly sweet and just right for everything else that comes off the grill.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 5 minutes
  • Grilling time: approx. 12-18 minutes over medium indirect heat
  • Finish straight away: 1-2 minutes optional
  • Total: approx. 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 pre-cooked ears of corn
  • 50g butter
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 1 pinch of pepper
  • optional: 1/2 teaspoon paprika powder
  • optional: some chopped parsley

Instructions

  1. Clean the corn cobs and rub lightly with butter or oil.
  2. Preheat the grill to medium indirect heat.
  3. Grill the cobs for 12 to 18 minutes, turning regularly, until they get color all over.
  4. Finally, grill briefly over direct heat if more roasted flavor is desired.
  5. Serve with butter, salt and, if desired, paprika powder or herbs.
