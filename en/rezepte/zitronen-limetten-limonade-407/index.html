Lemon Lime Lemonade

approx. 6 glasses · Summer drinks, lemonades & mocktails

A classic homemade lemon and lime lemonade with pleasant acidity and plenty of freshness – perfect for hot summer days.

Timing

  • Prep time: 10 minutes
  • Chill time: 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 lemons
  • 3 limes
  • 800 ml sparkling mineral water
  • 3–4 tbsp honey or agave syrup
  • ice cubes
  • lemon and lime slices
  • mint

Instructions

  1. Juice the lemons and limes.
  2. Stir the juice with honey or agave syrup.
  3. Add the sparkling mineral water.
  4. Fill with ice cubes.
  5. Serve with lemon and lime slices and mint.
