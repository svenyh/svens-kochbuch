Crispy tortilla minced meat pockets

3-4 people · Fast cuisine

Not a softly rolled burrito, but folded tortilla pockets that get crispy in the pan or air fryer. Spicy minced meat, cheese and vegetables inside – golden and crunchy on the outside.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Filling: approx. 12 minutes
  • Pan/air fryer time: approx. 6 to 8 minutes
  • Total: approx. 25 to 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500g minced meat
  • 1 onion
  • 1 pepper
  • 1 can of corn
  • 150g grated cheese
  • 8 small flour tortillas
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: 2 tbsp salsa

Instructions

  1. Finely dice onion and pepper, drain corn.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and fry minced meat until crumbly.
  3. Add vegetables, corn, tomato paste, paprika, cumin, salt and pepper and fry for 5 minutes.
  4. Reduce heat, stir in cheese until creamy. Let cool slightly.
  5. Warm tortillas briefly in a dry pan or microwave.
  6. Put filling on one half of each tortilla, fold into a pocket and press edges firmly.
  7. Fry in oil for 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden – or bake in air fryer at 190 °C for 6 to 8 minutes, turning once.
  8. Serve with salsa – best eaten immediately while crispy.
