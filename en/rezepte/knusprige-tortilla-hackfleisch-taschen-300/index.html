Crispy tortilla minced meat pockets

3-4 people · Fast cuisine

Not a softly rolled burrito, but folded tortilla pockets that get nice and crispy in the pan or air fryer. Spicy minced meat, cheese and vegetables are inside - the outside is golden brown and really crunchy.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Pan/Air Fryer Time: Approx. 12-15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500g minced meat
  • 1 onion
  • 1 pepper
  • 1 can of corn
  • 150g grated cheese
  • 8 small flour tortillas
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: 2 tbsp salsa

Instructions

  1. Finely dice the onion and peppers.
  2. Fry the minced meat vigorously in a pan.
  3. Add vegetables, corn, tomato paste, paprika, cumin, salt and pepper and fry for 5 minutes.
  4. Stir in cheese until filling is creamy.
  5. Warm the tortillas slightly.
  6. Put the filling on one half of each tortilla, fold it into a pocket and press the edges firmly.
  7. Fry the pockets in oil on both sides until golden brown and crispy - or bake in the air fryer at 190 °C for 6-8 minutes.
  8. Serve with salsa.
