Rigatoni with BBQ mince

4 people · Pasta dishes

Rigatoni with BBQ mince is spicy, strong and yet uncomplicated. The combination of pasta, mince and BBQ touch brings a modern but accessible home cooking style to the plate.

Ingredients

  • 350g rigatoni
  • 500 g minced beef
  • 1 onion
  • 1 red pepper
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 250 ml pureed tomatoes
  • 120 ml BBQ sauce
  • 100 ml broth
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 80g grated cheese
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook rigatoni in salted water until al dente and drain.
  2. Dice onion and peppers. Heat oil in a large pan and fry the minced meat vigorously.
  3. Add onion and pepper and fry for 4 minutes. Stir in tomato paste and fry briefly.
  4. Pour in the pureed tomatoes, BBQ sauce and broth. Season with paprika powder, salt and pepper and simmer gently for 8 to 10 minutes.
  5. Fold in the rigatoni and put everything in a baking dish. Sprinkle with cheese.
  6. Bake at 190 °C top/bottom heat for 12 to 15 minutes.
