Crispy fried potatoes with onions

2-3 people · Salads & side dishes

Fried potatoes can be really good – if you give them time. Nice and crispy fried, refined with onions and exactly how you want them. Simple, but with a big difference.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Roasting time: approx. 20-25 minutes
  • Total: approx. 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 800 g waxy potatoes, preferably cooked the day before
  • 2 large onions
  • 2-3 tablespoons oil or clarified butter
  • Salt, pepper
  • optional: some bacon

Instructions

  1. Cut potatoes into slices.
  2. Cut onions into half rings.
  3. Heat oil or clarified butter in a large pan and fry the potatoes until golden brown without constantly turning.
  4. Add onions in the last few minutes and fry.
  5. Season with salt and pepper.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

They are best with potatoes from the day before.
