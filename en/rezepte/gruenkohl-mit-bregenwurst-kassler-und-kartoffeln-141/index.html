Kale with Bregenwurst, smoked pork and potatoes

4 people · Wölpinghausen specialties / winter cuisine

This is what a real kale plate should look like: strong, hearty and no frills. Bregenwurst, Kassler and potatoes bring exactly the mix of spice, satiety and winter feeling that has made this dish so popular for years.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Braising time: approx. 45-60 minutes
  • Potatoes in parallel: approx. 25 minutes
  • Total: approx. 70-80 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg of kale
  • 2 tbsp lard
  • 150 g bacon cubes
  • 4 Bregenwursts
  • 500 g smoked pork
  • 800g potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Mustard to taste

Instructions

  1. Sort, wash and roughly chop the kale if you are using fresh cabbage. Provide frozen kale directly.
  2. Sauté onion cubes in a little lard or oil and add the kale.
  3. Pour in a little broth, season and slowly braise the cabbage over a low heat until soft.
  4. Cut the smoked pork into pieces and after a while add it to the kale so that it soaks through.
  5. Allow the Bregenwurst to steep separately in hot, not boiling water or cook directly in the kale at the end if there is enough liquid.
  6. Peel the potatoes, boil them and serve them as boiled potatoes or lightly mashed, depending on your preference.
  7. Season the kale with salt, pepper and a little mustard or sugar and serve everything together.
