Boeuf Bourguignon – France

4 people · Culinary World Tour / Europe

Sven's hearty take on the French classic: beef shoulder braised in a dark red wine sauce with vegetables and mushrooms. Rustic, rich and perfect in a Dutch oven or with a light smoke on the Pit Boss.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 30 minutes
  • Braising time: approx. 2.5–3 hours
  • Total: approx. 3–3.5 hours

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef shoulder
  • 200 g bacon
  • 2 onions
  • 2 carrots
  • 250 g mushrooms
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 500 ml red wine
  • 500 ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp flour
  • bay leaf
  • thyme
  • salt
  • pepper
  • clarified butter

Instructions

  1. Dice the meat and sear it well.
  2. Sauté the bacon, onions, carrots and garlic.
  3. Roast the tomato paste, dust with flour and deglaze with red wine and stock.
  4. Return the meat and braise for 2.5–3 hours.
  5. Add the mushrooms towards the end.
  6. Season to taste and serve.
