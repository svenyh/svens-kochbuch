Focaccia with rosemary and olive oil

1 tray or 1 large mold · Baking & Sweets / Basics & Sauces

A fluffy focaccia with olive oil, rosemary and coarse salt. Ideal as a side dish, for grilling or for antipasti.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Walking time: approx. 60-75 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 20-25 minutes at 220°C

Ingredients

  • 500g flour
  • 1 cube of fresh yeast
  • 325 ml lukewarm water
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2-3 sprigs of rosemary
  • coarse salt
  • a little more olive oil for drizzling

Instructions

  1. Dissolve yeast and sugar in lukewarm water.
  2. Add flour, salt and olive oil and knead into a soft dough.
  3. Cover and let the dough rise for 45-60 minutes.
  4. Place in an oiled mold and make hollows with your fingers.
  5. Drizzle with olive oil, add rosemary and coarse salt and let rest briefly again.
  6. Bake in the preheated oven until golden brown.
