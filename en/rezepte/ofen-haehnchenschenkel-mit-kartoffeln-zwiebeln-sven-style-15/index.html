Oven chicken thighs with potatoes & onions – Sven style

2-3 people · Oven dishes / chicken dishes

Sometimes you don't need anything more: good chicken legs, potatoes, onions and a nice seasoning. In the oven, it turns into a down-to-earth dinner that requires little work and still looks really good on the table.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Oven time: approx. 45 minutes
  • Total: approx. 55 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken legs
  • 800 g potatoes (with skin, in wedges)
  • 2 large onions
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Sweet paprika powder
  • Marjoram or thyme

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 200 °C fan oven.
  2. Pat the chicken thighs dry and season with salt, pepper, paprika powder and marjoram or thyme.
  3. Cut potatoes into wedges and onions into coarse pieces.
  4. Place potatoes and onions in a large baking dish, mix with oil and a little salt.
  5. Place the chicken thighs on top so that the juices will later soak into the vegetables.
  6. Cook in the oven for a total of about 45 minutes.
  7. After about 30 minutes, turn the potatoes once and pour some of the pan juices over the meat.
  8. At the end, check whether the potatoes are soft and the skin is nicely browned, then serve straight away hot.
