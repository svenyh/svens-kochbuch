Fried Asian noodles with chicken

4 people · Asian wok / wok dishes

Fried Asian noodles with chicken are an uncomplicated everyday classic from the wok. With Mie noodles, lots of vegetables and a spicy soy-sesame sauce, you create a quick, filling dish with a clear wok character.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cook pasta: about 5 minutes
  • Wok preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 300g Mie noodles
  • 500 g chicken breast fillet
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 yellow pepper
  • 200 g white cabbage
  • 1 large carrot
  • 4 spring onions
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 piece of ginger, approx. 20 g
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp sesame
  • For the sauce: 5 tbsp soy sauce
  • For the sauce: 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
  • For the sauce: 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • For the sauce: 1 tbsp honey
  • For the sauce: 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • For the sauce: 1 teaspoon sambal oelek or chili paste

Instructions

  1. Cook the Mie noodles according to the package instructions, drain and rinse with cold water.
  2. Cut chicken into thin strips. Cut the pepper into fine strips, the white cabbage into fine strips and the carrot into julienne pieces. Cut spring onions into rings.
  3. Mix the sauce from soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, honey, rice vinegar and sambal oelek.
  4. Heat the wok to high heat. Add 2 tablespoons of oil and fry the chicken for 4 minutes. Take out.
  5. Add remaining oil to the wok. Stir-fry the garlic, ginger, carrot, peppers and white cabbage for 3 to 4 minutes.
  6. Return chicken and noodles to wok. Pour on the sauce and mix everything well.
  7. Fold in the spring onions and continue to fry for 1 minute.
  8. Sprinkle with sesame seeds before serving.
