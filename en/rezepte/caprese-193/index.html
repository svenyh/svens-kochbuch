Caprese

4 people · Italian-inspired cuisine / salads & starters

Caprese thrives on good ingredients and a clear, fresh combination. Tomato, mozzarella and basil seem simple, but together they make a very harmonious appetizer or side dish.

Ingredients

  • 4 ripe tomatoes
  • 2 balls of mozzarella
  • 1 handful of basil
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp light balsamic vinegar, optional
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut tomatoes and mozzarella into even slices.
  2. Arrange alternately on a platter or on plates.
  3. Scatter basil leaves in between or on top.
  4. Drizzle with olive oil and add some balsamic vinegar if desired.
  5. Season with salt and freshly ground pepper and serve immediately.
