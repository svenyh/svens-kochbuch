Rendang – Indonesia

4 people · Culinary World Tour / World Tour

Indonesian rendang with slowly reduced coconut sauce – intense, dark and with real wow factor. Sven's braise for special occasions.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 25 minutes
  • Braising time: approx. 2–3 hours
  • Total: approx. 150–185 minutes

Ingredients

  • 800 g beef stewing meat
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • onions
  • garlic
  • ginger
  • lemongrass
  • chilli
  • turmeric
  • cumin
  • coriander
  • lime

Instructions

  1. Make the spice paste.
  2. Sear the meat.
  3. Add the paste and pour in the coconut milk.
  4. Braise slowly for 2–3 hours until the sauce is heavily reduced and dark and aromatic.
