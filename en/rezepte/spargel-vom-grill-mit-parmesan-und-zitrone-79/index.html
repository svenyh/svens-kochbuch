Grilled asparagus with parmesan and lemon

2-3 people · Grill & BBQ / asparagus & seasonal cuisine

Parmesan and lemon give grilled asparagus just the mix of spice and freshness that makes it so strong. The recipe is quick to implement and still appears much more refined than a simple standard side dish.

Timing

  • Preparation: 10 minutes
  • Grill time: 6-10 minutes over medium direct heat
  • Total: approx. 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g green or white asparagus
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 30-40g Parmesan
  • 1/2 lemon
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: fresh herbs

Instructions

  1. Only trim the ends of green asparagus and peel white asparagus completely.
  2. Mix the asparagus with olive oil, salt and pepper.
  3. Grill over medium heat for 6-10 minutes, turning regularly.
  4. Immediately after grilling, drizzle with lemon juice and add some lemon zest if you like.
  5. Finally, sprinkle freshly grated Parmesan on top and serve immediately.
