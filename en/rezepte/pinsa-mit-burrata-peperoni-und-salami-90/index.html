Pinsa with burrata, pepperoni and salami

2 people · Oven dishes/snacks

For me, this Pinsa is the perfect mix of quick and still special. Burrata brings creaminess, pepperoni a little spiciness and the salami just the savory depth that makes an uncomplicated evening really good.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 10-12 minutes
  • Total: approx. 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 pinsa bases, pre-baked
  • 150 ml pureed tomatoes
  • 80-100 g sliced ​​salami
  • 1 pepper, cut into strips
  • 1 small red onion, cut into thin rings
  • 1 scoop of burrata
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Oregano or Italian herbs
  • optional: some chili oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 220 °C top/bottom heat.
  2. Spread the pinsa bases with pureed tomatoes and season with salt, pepper and herbs.
  3. Spread the salami, peppers and onion rings on top.
  4. Bake the pinsa for 10-12 minutes until the edges are crispy.
  5. Only after baking put the burrata on top and tear it open slightly.
  6. Serve with olive oil and a little chili oil if desired.
