Chili con carne

4 people · Pan dishes

Hearty minced beef with beans, corn and tomatoes – a Tex-Mex classic that warms you up and keeps well.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 35 minutes
  • Total: approx. 50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600 g minced beef
  • 2 cans kidney beans
  • 1 can sweetcorn
  • 2 onions
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 400 g tomatoes
  • chilli powder
  • cumin

Instructions

  1. Brown the mince.
  2. Add the onions.
  3. Stir in the spices.
  4. Add the tomatoes.
  5. Add beans and corn.
  6. Simmer for 30 minutes.
