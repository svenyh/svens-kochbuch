Pierogi with sauerkraut and mushroom filling

4 people · Dough dishes / Polish cuisine

Pierogi with sauerkraut and mushrooms are one of the classic fillings where everything depends on a well-seasoned, not too wet mass. If the dough and filling are properly prepared, they will be hearty, round and really traditional.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 35 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 10 minutes
  • Total: approx. 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500g flour
  • 300 ml hot water
  • 1 egg
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 300 g sauerkraut
  • 30 g dried porcini mushrooms
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Soak porcini mushrooms in hot water and then chop finely.
  2. Squeeze out the sauerkraut well and chop it roughly.
  3. Finely dice the onions and fry in oil until golden.
  4. Add the mushrooms and sauerkraut and braise the filling for a few minutes, then season with pepper and allow to cool.
  5. Knead the flour, hot water, egg and salt into a smooth dough and let it rest briefly.
  6. Roll out the dough thinly and cut out circles.
  7. Put some filling on each circle, fold it together and press the edges firmly.
  8. Cook the pierogi in batches in gently simmering salted water until they rise to the surface.
  9. If desired, serve straight away or briefly fry in butter with onions.

Frequently asked questions

Can I freeze this?

Very suitable for freezing.
