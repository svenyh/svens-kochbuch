Mustard party with potatoes

2-3 people · Egg dishes / home cooking

Mustard celebrations with potatoes are honest home cooking that works so well because of its simplicity. The creamy mustard sauce, the eggs and the potatoes together create the down-to-earth comfort food that you'll enjoy eating again and again.

Timing

  • Preparation: 10 minutes
  • Cooking time eggs: 10 minutes
  • Sauce: 10 minutes
  • Total: approx. 20-25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 6 eggs
  • 500 ml milk
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 2-3 tbsp medium hot mustard
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: some nutmeg
  • optional: parsley
  • Side dish: potatoes or bread

Instructions

  1. Place eggs in cold water, bring to the boil and cook hard for 9-10 minutes from the boiling point.
  2. Rinse eggs in cold water and peel.
  3. Melt butter in a saucepan, stir in flour and sauté for about 1 minute.
  4. Gradually add the milk while stirring to create a smooth sauce.
  5. Let the sauce simmer briefly until it thickens slightly.
  6. Stir in mustard, sugar, salt, pepper and optionally nutmeg and season to taste.
  7. Halve the eggs or add them whole to the sauce.
  8. Sprinkle with parsley and serve with potatoes or bread.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Sprinkle with parsley and serve with potatoes or bread.

Can I substitute ingredients?

For more depth, replace some of the mustard with coarse mustard.
