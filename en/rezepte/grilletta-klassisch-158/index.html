Grilletta classic

4 people · Classic recipes / snacks & snacks

The Grilletta is the classic snack version of the burger - simple, hearty and without unnecessary frills. That's exactly why it works so well: juicy minced meatballs, rolls, onions, a little sauce and you're done.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Fry meatballs: approx. 10 minutes
  • Toast the rolls: approx. 3-4 minutes
  • Total: approx. 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600g mixed minced meat
  • 1 small onion, very finely diced
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 4 burger or large buns
  • 4 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 medium onion in rings
  • 4 pickled cucumbers in slices
  • optional: 4 leaves of lettuce

Instructions

  1. Thoroughly knead the minced meat with finely diced onion, egg, breadcrumbs, mustard, paprika powder, salt and pepper.
  2. Form four flat, slightly larger meatballs as they will contract slightly when frying.
  3. Heat oil in a skillet and fry the meatballs over medium to medium-high heat for 4 to 5 minutes per side, until well browned and cooked inside.
  4. Halve the rolls and toast them in a dry pan or briefly in the oven at 180 °C top/bottom heat for 3 to 4 minutes.
  5. Spread the bottom halves of the bun with ketchup, place the meatballs on top and cover with onion rings and pickle slices.
  6. Add some salad if desired, put the lid on and serve immediately.
