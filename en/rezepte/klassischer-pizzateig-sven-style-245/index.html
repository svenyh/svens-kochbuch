Classic pizza dough Sven style

For 2 large pizzas or 1 sheet pizza · Pizza & Bread / Oven & Casseroles

A simple, reliable pizza dough for at home. Good for classic round pizzas or a house pizza from a tray.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Walking time: approx. 45-60 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 10-15 minutes at 230-250°C

Ingredients

  • 500 g wheat flour type 550
  • 1 cube of fresh yeast or 1 packet of dry yeast
  • 325 ml lukewarm water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Pour the lukewarm water into a large bowl and dissolve the sugar and yeast in it.
  2. Let the mixture stand for 5-10 minutes until the yeast starts to work easily.
  3. Add flour, salt and olive oil.
  4. Knead everything for 8-10 minutes to form a smooth, elastic dough.
  5. Cover the dough and let it rise in a warm place for 45-60 minutes.
  6. Then knead again briefly and roll out thinly or slightly thicker, depending on the pizza you want.
  7. Top with sauce and toppings and bake in a very hot oven.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

For more flavor, let the dough rise overnight in the refrigerator.
