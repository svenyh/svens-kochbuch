Shrimp wok with pak choi and garlic

4 people · Asian wok / wok dishes

Shrimp wok with pak choi and garlic is a quick, aromatic wok dish with clear freshness and a strong garlic aroma. The shrimp are only cooked briefly and therefore remain juicy and tender.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Wok preparation: approx. 8 minutes
  • Total: approx. 23 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 2 bok choy
  • 1 red pepper
  • 150 g sugar snap peas
  • 4 cloves of garlic
  • 1 piece of ginger, approx. 15 g
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 lime
  • 2 tablespoons chopped coriander, optional
  • For the sauce: 4 tablespoons soy sauce
  • For the sauce: 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • For the sauce: 1 tbsp honey
  • For the sauce: 1 tbsp lime juice
  • For the sauce: 1 teaspoon chili flakes or 1 small red chili

Instructions

  1. Pat shrimp dry. Cut the pak choy into coarse pieces. Cut peppers into strips. Clean sugar snap peas.
  2. Cut the garlic into thin slices and finely chop the ginger.
  3. Mix the sauce from soy sauce, fish sauce, honey, lime juice and chili.
  4. Heat the wok to high heat. Add oil and fry the shrimp for 1 to 2 minutes per side. Take out.
  5. Briefly fry the garlic and ginger in the wok.
  6. Fry peppers and snow peas for 2 minutes.
  7. Add pak choi and let it wilt briefly.
  8. Put the shrimp back in the wok, pour on the sauce and toss everything together for 1 minute.
  9. Serve with lime wedges and optional cilantro.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with jasmine rice or rice noodles.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Serve with lime wedges and optional cilantro.
