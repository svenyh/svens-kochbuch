Ratatouille with polenta

3-4 people · Fast cuisine

Ratatouille with polenta is exactly the kind of vegetable dish that doesn't taste like sacrifice. The braised vegetables add depth, the polenta provides calm on the plate and together it becomes a very harmonious meal.

Timing

  • Preparation: 15 minutes
  • Pan and pot: 20 minutes
  • Total: approx. 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 eggplant
  • 1 zucchini
  • 2 peppers
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 can of diced tomatoes (400 g)
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 250g polenta
  • 750 ml vegetable broth
  • 30g butter
  • 30g grated Parmesan, optional
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 1 tsp Herbs de Provence

Instructions

  1. Cut the eggplant, zucchini, peppers, onion and garlic into pieces.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large pan and briefly sauté onion and garlic.
  3. Add the eggplant, peppers and zucchini and fry for 6-8 minutes.
  4. Stir in the tomato paste, chopped tomatoes and herbs de Provence and let everything simmer for 15 minutes.
  5. For the polenta, bring the broth to the boil, pour in the polenta and cook for 4-5 minutes while stirring.
  6. Stir in butter and optionally Parmesan.
  7. Season the ratatouille with salt and pepper and serve with the polenta.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Season the ratatouille with salt and pepper and serve with the polenta.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Stir in butter and optionally Parmesan.
