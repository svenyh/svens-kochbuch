Bobotie

4 people · Oven dishes

Bobotie is considered South Africa's national dish. Spiced minced meat topped with a golden egg-milk crust blends African, Asian and European influences.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 35 minutes
  • Total: approx. 50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600 g minced beef
  • 2 onions
  • 2 slices sandwich bread
  • 150 ml milk
  • 2 tbsp apricot jam
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 eggs
  • 100 ml milk for the topping
  • salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Soak the bread in milk.
  2. Fry the onions.
  3. Add the mince.
  4. Stir in spices and jam.
  5. Transfer to a baking dish.
  6. Whisk eggs with milk.
  7. Pour over the top.
  8. Bake for about 35 minutes at 180 °C.
