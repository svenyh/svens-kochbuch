Meat pie

6 people · Oven dishes / meat dishes

Meat pie is one of those recipes that tastes good warm, but is almost even better the next day. Baking causes everything to come together, the structure becomes firmer and this means it can be cut cleanly and served later.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 50 minutes
  • Total: approximately 1 hour 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 700g mixed meat or cooked meat for the pie
  • 1 onion
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 stale bread roll
  • 1 carrot
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • marjoram
  • Butter for the mold

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C top/bottom heat.
  2. Soak the rolls in water and squeeze them out.
  3. Finely chop the onion and carrot and sauté briefly.
  4. Mix the meat with rolls, eggs, vegetables and spices or chop finely, depending on the desired consistency.
  5. Butter a loaf tin or small baking dish and pour in the mixture.
  6. Smooth the surface.
  7. Bake in the oven for about 45-50 minutes, until the pie is firm and lightly browned.
  8. Allow to cool slightly before cutting.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Goes well with bread, mustard or pickled vegetables.
