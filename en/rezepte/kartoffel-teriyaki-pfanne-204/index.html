Potato teriyaki pan

4 people · Stir fry

The Potato Teriyaki Skillet is a good example of a subtle fusion twist without exaggeration. Potatoes, vegetables and chicken remain familiar but gain more spice and brightness.

Ingredients

  • 800 g waxy potatoes
  • 300 g chicken breast fillet
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 zucchini
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 4 tbsp teriyaki sauce
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 tsp sesame
  • 1 spring onion
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut the potatoes into small cubes and pre-boil them in salted water for 8 minutes. Drain and allow to evaporate.
  2. Cut the chicken, peppers, zucchini and onion into bite-sized pieces.
  3. Heat oil in a large pan. Fry the potatoes until golden brown, then remove them.
  4. Brown the chicken all over in the same pan. Add onion, peppers and zucchini and fry for 4 minutes.
  5. Mix teriyaki sauce, soy sauce and honey. Add to the pan along with the potatoes and glaze for 2 to 3 minutes.
  6. Serve with sesame seeds and finely chopped spring onions.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with sesame seeds and finely chopped spring onions.
