Grilled halloumi

2-3 people · Grill & BBQ / Vegetarian

Grilled halloumi is quick to make and great when you want something vegetarian with a real bite on the grill. It adds spice and structure and works just as well on its own as it does as part of a larger plate.

Timing

  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Grill time: 4-6 minutes over medium direct heat
  • Total: approx. 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 250g halloumi
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp chopped herbs, e.g. E.g. oregano or parsley
  • pepper
  • optional: some honey

Instructions

  1. Cut the halloumi into thick slices and pat dry.
  2. Brush with a little olive oil and season with oregano, chili or lemon zest if desired.
  3. Preheat the grill or a grill pan to medium direct heat.
  4. Grill the halloumi for 2 to 3 minutes per side until clearly browned.
  5. Just turn carefully so that the slices don't break.
  6. Immediately after grilling, refine with a little lemon juice or herb oil.
  7. Serve immediately with salad, bread or grilled vegetables.
