Greek Lentil Soup – Fakés

4 people · Soups/home cooking

Fakés is the ultimate Greek lentil soup - hearty, uncomplicated and seasoned just right with tomato, bay leaf and oregano. An honest pot for cool days that becomes particularly rounded with a little vinegar or lemon at the end.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 25-30 minutes
  • Total: approx. 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 300 g brown lentils
  • 1 large onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 400 g pureed tomatoes
  • 1 l vegetable broth
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar to taste
  • optional: lemon juice
  • optional: parsley

Instructions

  1. Rinse the lentils briefly and set aside.
  2. Peel the onion and carrot, dice finely and chop the garlic.
  3. Heat olive oil in a large pot and sauté vegetables for 4-5 minutes.
  4. Add tomatoes, lentils, stock, bay leaves and oregano.
  5. Bring the soup to the boil and simmer over medium heat for 25-30 minutes until the lentils are soft.
  6. Remove the bay leaf and press the soup lightly or partially puree it, if desired.
  7. Season with salt and pepper.
  8. Finally, season with 1-2 tablespoons of red wine vinegar or the juice of half a lemon - this makes Fakés typically Greek-fresh. Serve with parsley.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with olive bread or baguette.
