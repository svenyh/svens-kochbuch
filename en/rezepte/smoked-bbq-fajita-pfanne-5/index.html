Smoked BBQ Fajita Skillet

4 people · Grill & BBQ / meat dishes

The Smoked BBQ Fajita Pan brings strong roasted flavors, smoke and lots of spice to a dish that remains uncomplicated at the same time. It gets its own, very hearty character, especially from the smoker or grill.

Timing

  • Preparation: 20 minutes
  • Sauté: 15 minutes
  • Smoking: 45-60 minutes
  • Finish: 10 minutes
  • Total: approximately 1 hour 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 700 g beef in strips
  • 2 peppers
  • 1 zucchini
  • 2 onions
  • 2-3 tbsp BBQ rub
  • 3-4 tbsp BBQ sauce
  • 1 lime
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 4 bratwursts
  • 1 cup of tzatziki
  • 1 pack of flatbread
  • Salt and pepper as needed

Instructions

  1. Season the beef with BBQ rub and let it steep briefly.
  2. Cut peppers, zucchini and onions into strips.
  3. Heat cast iron pan to high heat.
  4. Sear the meat and remove it again.
  5. Roast the vegetables well too.
  6. Return meat to pan.
  7. Place the pan in the smoker at approx. 120-130 °C for 45-60 minutes.
  8. Use hickory pellets.
  9. Then put the pan back on the gas stove.
  10. Add BBQ sauce and let everything simmer briefly.
  11. Finish with fresh lime juice.
  12. Serve with tzatziki, flatbread and bratwurst.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with tzatziki, flatbread and bratwurst.
