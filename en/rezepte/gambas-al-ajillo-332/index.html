Gambas al Ajillo

3 people · Fish & seafood

Prawns in hot garlic oil – an Andalusian classic on the table in minutes. The garlic oil is the best part – serve with bread for dipping.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 8 minutes
  • Total: approx. 18 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g prawns
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 5 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 chilli
  • parsley
  • salt

Instructions

  1. Pat the prawns dry and season lightly with salt.
  2. Heat the olive oil.
  3. Fry the garlic slices and chilli until fragrant.
  4. Cook the prawns for 2–3 minutes per side.
  5. Sprinkle with parsley and serve immediately.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with baguette for dipping.
