Strawberry Horchata with Rice Milk Sekt Royal

1 glass · Summer horchatas & aguas frescas

Light summer aperitif: Strawberry Horchata with Rice Milk Sekt Royal. Dry sparkling wine adds festive bubbles – perfect as a summer aperitif.

Timing

  • Prep time: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 100 ml Strawberry Horchata with Rice Milk
  • 120 ml well-chilled dry sparkling wine
  • Ice cubes
  • strawberry slices and fresh mint

Instructions

  1. Chill the base drink (Strawberry Horchata with Rice Milk) and alcohol well.
  2. Fill a champagne flute with ice cubes.
  3. Pour 100 ml Strawberry Horchata with Rice Milk first.
  4. Top up carefully with 120 ml sparkling wine.
  5. Stir gently, garnish with matching fruit and serve immediately.
