Cold Brew Orange

approx. 2 glasses · Summer drinks, lemonades & mocktails

A layered cold brew with orange juice – caffeinated, fruity, and ideal as a refreshing summer coffee.

Timing

  • Prep time: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 300 ml cold brew
  • 150 ml orange juice
  • ice cubes
  • orange slices

Instructions

  1. Add ice to the glass.
  2. Pour in the orange juice.
  3. Slowly pour in the cold brew.
  4. Serve with an orange slice.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

Cold brew can steep the day before; add orange juice and ice when you are ready to serve.

How long does it keep?

Mixed cold brew keeps in the fridge for 1 day.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Mandarin or blood orange juice works instead of regular orange juice.
