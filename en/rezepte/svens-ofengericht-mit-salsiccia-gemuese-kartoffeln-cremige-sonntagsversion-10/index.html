Sven's oven dish with salsiccia, vegetables and potatoes (creamy Sunday version)

3-4 people · Oven dishes / meat dishes

This is one of those dishes that you make once and then want again and again. The salsiccia really adds flavor, the oven vegetables become soft and aromatic and at the end the creamy component makes everything even more rounded.

Timing

  • Preparation: 20 minutes
  • Oven time: 35-40 minutes
  • Total: approx. 60 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500-600g salsiccia
  • 800g potatoes
  • 2 peppers
  • 1 zucchini
  • 1 large onion
  • 150 g crème fraîche
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Paprika powder
  • optional: garlic

Instructions

  1. Pre-boil the potatoes and cut them into pieces.
  2. Cutting vegetables.
  3. Divide salsiccia into pieces.
  4. Put everything in an oven dish and season.
  5. Mix with a little oil.
  6. Bake at 190-200°C for approx. 35-40 minutes.
  7. Finally, fold in the crème fraîche or add it in dollops.
  8. Continue cooking briefly and serve.
