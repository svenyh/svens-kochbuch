Pizza sauce basic recipe

For 2 large pizzas or 1 sheet pizza · Basic recipes / pizza & bread

A simple, strong pizza sauce for classic pizza, sheet pizza and pizza rolls. Made quickly and much better than when bought ready-made.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Brewing time optional: approx. 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 can chopped tomatoes (400 g)
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 small clove of garlic
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Place the chopped tomatoes in a bowl.
  2. Add tomato paste, finely chopped or pressed garlic, oregano, sugar and olive oil.
  3. Mix everything well and season with salt and pepper.
  4. Use the sauce straight away or let it steep for 10 minutes before topping.
