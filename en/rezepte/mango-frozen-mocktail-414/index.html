Mango Frozen Mocktail

approx. 2–3 glasses · Summer drinks, lemonades & mocktails

A tropical mango frozen mocktail – creamy, fruity, and ready in seconds in the Thermomix.

Timing

  • Prep time: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g frozen mango
  • 300 ml orange juice
  • 1 lime
  • ice cubes

Instructions

  1. Add everything to the mixing bowl and blend 30 Sek. / Stufe 10.
  2. Serve immediately.
