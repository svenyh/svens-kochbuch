Ogórki w zalewie gyros

stock · Side dishes/stock

For me, pickled cucumbers like this are exactly the kind of recipe that makes a collection complete. They are spicy, slightly sweet and sour and bring to the table exactly that special side dish idea that you don't find everywhere.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 30 minutes
  • Brewing time: at least 24 hours
  • Total: from 24 hours

Ingredients

  • 2 kg of cucumbers
  • 30 g gyros seasoning
  • 2 large carrots
  • 2-3 cloves of garlic
  • 2-3 onions
  • 3 peppers
  • 1 glass of vinegar (approx. 250 ml, 10%)
  • 1/2 glass of oil
  • 2 tbsp salt
  • 2 glasses of sugar

Instructions

  1. Wash the cucumbers and cut them into thicker slices.
  2. Mix with salt and let steep briefly.
  3. Cut onions into half rings, chop carrots and peppers, chop garlic.
  4. Add everything to the cucumbers.
  5. Mix vinegar, oil and gyros seasoning.
  6. Add sugar and mix well.
  7. Pour the marinade over the vegetables and mix everything thoroughly.
  8. Pour into clean jars and store in a cool place.
