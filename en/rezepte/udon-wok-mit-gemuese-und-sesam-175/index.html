Udon wok with vegetables and sesame

4 people · Asian wok / wok dishes

Udon wok with vegetables and sesame is a soft, creamy noodle wok with lots of vegetables and a spicy soy teriyaki sauce. The thick udon noodles give the dish a completely different texture than classic mie or rice noodles.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cook pasta: about 5 minutes
  • Wok preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 400g udon noodles
  • 200 g mushrooms
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 bok choy
  • 2 carrots
  • 4 spring onions
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 piece of ginger, approx. 20 g
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • For the sauce: 4 tablespoons soy sauce
  • For the sauce: 2 tablespoons teriyaki sauce
  • For the sauce: 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • For the sauce: 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • For the sauce: 1 teaspoon honey
  • For the sauce: 1 teaspoon chili oil, optional

Instructions

  1. Cook or loosen the udon noodles according to the package instructions, then drain.
  2. Cut the mushrooms into slices, peppers into strips, pak choi into pieces, carrots into thin sticks and spring onions into rings.
  3. Mix together the sauce made from soy sauce, teriyaki sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, honey and optionally chili oil.
  4. Heat the wok to high heat. Add oil and briefly fry the garlic and ginger.
  5. Stir-fry carrots, mushrooms and peppers for 3 minutes.
  6. Add pak choy and spring onions.
  7. Add the udon noodles to the wok, pour on the sauce and mix everything well.
  8. Continue frying for 2 minutes until everything is hot.
  9. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and serve.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

Mix together the sauce made from soy sauce, teriyaki sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, honey and optionally chili oil.
