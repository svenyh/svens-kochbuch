Cheeseburger roll made from pizza dough

4-6 people · Party food / oven dishes / snack

The big party version: a filled cheeseburger roll made from pizza dough that is cut into slices after baking. Ideal for guests, cozy evenings and anything that makes you feel full and happy without any complications.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 25-30 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 rolls of fresh pizza dough
  • 600 g minced beef
  • 1 large onion
  • 180g cheddar
  • 5 gherkins, finely chopped
  • 3 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tbsp mustard
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1 tbsp milk
  • 1-2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • some oil

Instructions

  1. Finely dice the onion and fry.
  2. Add the minced meat and fry vigorously until crumbly.
  3. Season with salt, pepper and paprika powder.
  4. Stir in ketchup, mustard and pickles.
  5. Let the mixture cool briefly.
  6. Lay out the two pizza doughs to form a larger surface or put them together slightly overlapping.
  7. Spread the filling oblong in the middle, leaving space at the edges.
  8. Pour cheddar on top.
  9. Close the dough into a roll and press the ends firmly.
  10. Place on a baking sheet, seam side down.
  11. Brush with egg yolk and milk mixture and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
  12. Bake at 190 °C top/bottom heat for about 25-30 minutes until golden brown.
  13. Allow to rest for 5-10 minutes before slicing.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

It goes well with burger sauce, garlic dip or a coleslaw.
