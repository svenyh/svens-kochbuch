Aperol Spritz

1 glass · Cocktails / summer drinks

Aperol Spritz is a modern classic that thrives precisely on its simple structure. Bitter orange, tanginess and lots of ice make it a drink that is quick to make and still immediately creates the right aperitif mood.

Timing

  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Mix: 2 minutes
  • Serve immediately iced

Ingredients

  • 3 ice cubes or more
  • 90 ml Prosecco
  • 60ml Aperol
  • 30 ml mineral water or soda
  • 1 orange slice

Instructions

  1. Fill a large wine glass with ice.
  2. Pour in Prosecco.
  3. Add Aperol.
  4. Top up with mineral water or soda.
  5. Stir briefly and serve with an orange slice.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

Prosecco, Aperol and mineral water can chill 1–2 hours before – mix when you are ready to serve.

How long does it keep?

Mixed spritz tastes best fresh and keeps in the fridge only briefly.

Can I substitute ingredients?

For a non-alcoholic version, replace Aperol with bitter orange syrup.
