Sven's AirFryer Iskender Kebab

4 people · Air fryer

This is exactly the kind of dish that makes you realize how much the AirFryer can do in everyday life. Spicy meat, good sauce and warm bread - made quickly but with the feeling of real comfort food.

Timing

  • Total time: approx. 45 minutes
  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Freeze: 10-15 minutes
  • AirFryer: approx. 20 minutes
  • Rest time: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 kg ground beef (minced twice)
  • 2 tbsp natural yogurt (10%)
  • 2 pureed onions
  • 2 tbsp ajvar
  • 3 tbsp kebab seasoning
  • 1 tsp Vegeta
  • pepper
  • 50 ml olive oil
  • 2 tbsp parsley (chopped)
  • Flatbread
  • Tomato sauce

Instructions

  1. Knead all the ingredients for the meat thoroughly.
  2. Flatten the mixture between baking paper, about 1-1.5 cm thick.
  3. Freeze the meat plate for 10-15 minutes.
  4. Cook in the AirFryer at 200 °C for about 20 minutes and turn after 10 minutes.
  5. Let the meat rest for 5 minutes and then cut into strips.
  6. Roast the flatbread in the oven at 180 °C for 6-8 minutes.
  7. Serve with tomato sauce and yogurt.

Frequently asked questions

Can I freeze this?

Freeze: 10-15 minutes.

What side dish goes well?

Serve with tomato sauce and yogurt.
