Classic red sangria

4 glasses · Cocktails / summer drinks

Classic red sangria is just right if you want a summer drink to be fruity, uncomplicated and sociable. Well chilled and with a little steeping time, it tastes like a terrace, sun and a relaxed evening with several glasses.

Timing

  • Preparation: 10 minutes
  • Brewing time/cooling: 30 minutes
  • Total: approx. 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 bottle of dry red wine (0.75 l)
  • 200 ml orange lemonade or Fanta
  • 1 orange
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 apple
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 100 ml orange juice
  • Ice cubes

Instructions

  1. Cut the orange, lemon and apple into slices or cubes.
  2. Put the fruit, sugar and orange juice in a carafe and let it steep for 10 minutes.
  3. Pour in red wine and orange lemonade.
  4. Stir well and chill for at least 30 minutes.
  5. Serve with ice cubes.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

It is best to prepare for guests 1-2 hours in advance.
