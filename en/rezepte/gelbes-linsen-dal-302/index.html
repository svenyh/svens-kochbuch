Yellow lentil dal

4 people · Soups & Stews

Creamy dal with yellow or red lentils is mildly spicy, quick to make and perfect for everyday life. No exotic catalog of ingredients - just lentils, spices and a little coconut milk, which makes everything nice and velvety. This goes well with bread, naan or, if you like, rice.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 20-25 minutes
  • Total: approx. 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 300 g red or yellow lentils
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 piece of ginger, approx. 2 cm
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 800 ml water or vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt
  • optional: 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • optional: coriander greens or parsley
  • Bread, naan or rice to serve

Instructions

  1. Rinse the lentils briefly.
  2. Finely chop the onion, garlic and ginger.
  3. Heat oil in a pot and sauté onion for 3-4 minutes.
  4. Briefly roast the garlic, ginger, cumin, turmeric and garam masala.
  5. Add the lentils, water or stock and simmer for 15-20 minutes until the lentils are soft.
  6. Stir in coconut milk and simmer for 5 minutes.
  7. Season with salt and, if desired, puree a little to make the dal creamier.
  8. Serve with fresh coriander – with bread, naan or rice.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Creamy dal with yellow or red lentils is mildly spicy, quick to make and perfect for everyday life. No exotic catalog of ingredients - just lentils, spices and a little coconut milk, which makes everything nice and velvety. This goes well with bread, naan or, if you like, rice.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Rice is optional – the dal is just as good with flatbread or naan.
