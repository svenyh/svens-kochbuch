Mango-passionfruit-rum slush for the Ninja Slushi

2-3 glasses · Cocktails/Ninja Sushi

Mango and passion fruit bring a lot of fruit and, together with rum, exactly the right direction in the glass that quickly tastes like a holiday. The slush is soft, exotic and a really good alternative to heavier cocktails, especially on warm days.

Timing

  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Machine: approx. 15-30 minutes
  • Total: approx. 20-35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 250 ml mango nectar
  • 150 ml passion fruit juice
  • 4 cl white rum
  • 50 ml still water
  • optional: 1 tsp sugar syrup

Instructions

  1. Chill all ingredients.
  2. Mix mango nectar, passion fruit juice, rum and water.
  3. Pour into the Ninja Slushy and let it run until the desired consistency.
  4. Tap straight away and serve immediately.
  5. Optionally fine-tune with a little sugar syrup to taste.
