Potato soup with Mettenden

4-6 people · Soups/home cooking

Potato soup with Mettenden is a down-to-earth classic that is instantly filling and warming. Especially on cool days, it is a reliable family dish with an honest, uncomplicated character.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 25-30 minutes
  • Follow up: 2-3 minutes
  • Total: approx. 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 800g potatoes
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 onion
  • 1 leek or some celery
  • 1 l vegetable broth
  • 2-3 Mettenden or Vienna sausages
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • marjoram

Instructions

  1. Peel the potatoes, onions and soup vegetables and cut them into coarse cubes.
  2. Heat some oil or butter in a large pot and sauté the onions and vegetables for 3 to 4 minutes.
  3. Add potatoes, roast briefly and fill everything with broth.
  4. Cook the soup gently for 20 to 25 minutes until the potatoes are tender.
  5. Cut the meatballs into pieces and cook for the last 8 to 10 minutes so that they release their aroma.
  6. Puree some of the soup or press it lightly with a potato masher to make it thick.
  7. Season with salt, pepper and, if desired, marjoram and let it steep for another 2 minutes.
  8. Serve with parsley or fresh bread.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with parsley or fresh bread.
