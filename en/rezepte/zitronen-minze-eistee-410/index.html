Lemon Mint Iced Tea

approx. 6–8 glasses · Summer drinks, lemonades & mocktails

A refreshing lemon and mint iced tea with green tea – light, aromatic, and perfect for hot days.

Timing

  • Prep time: 10 minutes
  • Chill time: 1 hour

Ingredients

  • 1 litre green tea
  • 2 lemons
  • honey
  • mint
  • ice cubes

Instructions

  1. Brew the green tea.
  2. Stir in lemon juice and honey.
  3. Add mint.
  4. Chill well.
  5. Serve with ice.
