Pizza

Pizza Margherita classic

2 people · Pizza / classics & home cooking

The classic Margherita pizza relies on just a few good ingredients. Less toppings, lots of flavor and a beautifully hot baked base.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 10-12 minutes at 250°C

Ingredients

  • 1 portion of classic pizza dough
  • 200 g pureed tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 small clove of garlic
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 2 balls of mozzarella
  • some fresh basil leaves
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Instructions

  1. Prepare the pizza dough and roll it out thinly.
  2. Mix the pureed tomatoes with tomato paste, garlic, salt, pepper and oregano.
  3. Spread the sauce over the dough and spread the mozzarella on top.
  4. Bake in a hot oven until the edges and cheese are nicely browned.
  5. After baking, garnish with basil and a little olive oil.
