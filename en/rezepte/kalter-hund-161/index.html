Cold dog

1 loaf tin, approx. 12 pieces · Classic Recipes / Baking & Sweets

Cold Dog is one of the most well-known sweet home cooking classics ever - no baking, lots of chocolate and that typical cookie layer look that instantly brings back memories. That's exactly why it fits perfectly into a block of classic recipes.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 25 minutes
  • Cooling time: at least 6 hours, preferably overnight
  • Standing time before cutting: approx. 10-15 minutes
  • Total active: approx. 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 250 g coconut oil
  • 200 g dark chocolate
  • 100g milk chocolate
  • 2 eggs
  • 120g powdered sugar
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 packet of vanilla sugar
  • 250 g butter biscuits
  • 1 loaf pan
  • Baking paper for the mold

Instructions

  1. Line the loaf pan with baking paper.
  2. Carefully melt the coconut oil together with the dark and milk chocolate in a water bath.
  3. Mix eggs with powdered sugar, cocoa powder and vanilla sugar until smooth.
  4. Slowly stir the melted chocolate mixture into the egg mixture until a smooth cream forms.
  5. Put some chocolate mixture on the bottom of the mold, then place a layer of shortbread cookies on top.
  6. Layer the chocolate mixture and cookies alternately until everything is used up. Finish with a chocolate layer.
  7. Let the cold dog solidify in the refrigerator for at least 6 hours, preferably overnight.
  8. Let stand at room temperature for 10 to 15 minutes before slicing, then slice.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Let the cold dog solidify in the refrigerator for at least 6 hours, preferably overnight.
