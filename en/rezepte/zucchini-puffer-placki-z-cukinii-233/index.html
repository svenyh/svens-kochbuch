Zucchini pancakes / Placki z cukinii

3-4 people · Pan-fried dishes / quick cuisine

Crispy zucchini fritters that are quick to make and work well as a main dish or as a side dish.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Roasting time: approx. 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 large zucchini
  • 2 eggs
  • 120g flour
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • some parsley
  • Oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Roughly grate the zucchini, mix with a little salt and let stand for 10 minutes.
  2. Then express well.
  3. Finely dice or grate the onion and finely chop the garlic.
  4. Mix everything with eggs, flour, parsley, salt and pepper.
  5. Heat oil in a pan and form small buffers from the mixture.
  6. Fry until golden brown on both sides and drain briefly on kitchen paper.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with yogurt dip or herb quark.
