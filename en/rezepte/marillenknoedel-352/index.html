Marillenknödel

4 people · Baking & sweets

Sweet potato dumplings filled with apricots, rolled in buttered breadcrumbs – a Wachau landmark and one of Austria's most beloved desserts.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 30 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g potato dough
  • 8 apricots
  • 50 g butter
  • 80 g breadcrumbs
  • sugar

Instructions

  1. Wrap the apricots in dough.
  2. Shape into dumplings.
  3. Boil.
  4. Roll in buttered breadcrumbs.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

Dough and apricot filling can be prepared ahead; shape dumplings when you are ready to cook.

Can I freeze this?

Raw dumplings freeze well and can be cooked straight from frozen.

How long does it keep?

Cooked dumplings keep in the fridge for 1 day – pan-fry in butter or breadcrumbs.

What side dish goes well?

Serve with icing sugar and cinnamon.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Plum or apple purée works instead of apricots for a different filling.
