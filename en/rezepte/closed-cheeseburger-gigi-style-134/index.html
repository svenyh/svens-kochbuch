Closed Cheeseburger Gigi Style

4 people · Oven dishes / finger food / burger special

The special version with creamy burger sauce, red onions and extra spicy cheese. Slightly more intense, juicy and modern in taste.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 20-25 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 rolls of fresh pizza dough
  • 500 g minced beef
  • 1 red onion
  • 100g cheddar
  • 100 g grated Gouda or mozzarella
  • 4 pickled cucumbers, finely diced
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 1 tsp cucumber stock
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1 tbsp milk
  • 1-2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • some oil

Instructions

  1. Finely dice red onion.
  2. Fry the minced meat vigorously in a little oil.
  3. Fry the onion briefly.
  4. Season with salt, pepper and paprika powder.
  5. For the sauce, mix mayonnaise, ketchup, mustard and cucumber stock.
  6. Allow the minced mixture to cool slightly.
  7. Divide pizza dough into 8 pieces.
  8. Place some minced meat, some sauce, pickles and both types of cheese on the pieces of dough.
  9. Seal the dough tightly and place it on the tray with the seam side down.
  10. Brush with egg yolk and milk mixture and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
  11. Bake at 190 °C top/bottom heat for approx. 20-25 minutes until the surface is golden brown.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

This variant goes well with wedges or a fresh coleslaw.
