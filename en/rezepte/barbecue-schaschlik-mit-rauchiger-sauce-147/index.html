Barbecue shashlik with smoky sauce

4 people · Grill / BBQ / Modern kitchen

Barbecue shashlik is a more modern, heartier style for those who like roasted flavors and a darker sauce. Nevertheless, the basic idea remains: meat, onions, peppers and a skewer that simply works outside.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Marinate: 1-2 hours
  • Grilling time: approx. 18 minutes over medium heat
  • Rest time: 3 minutes
  • Total without marinade: approx. 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork or chicken
  • 2 peppers
  • 2-3 onions
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 150 ml BBQ sauce
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • optional: some chili or smoked paprika powder

Instructions

  1. Cut the meat into cubes and mix with oil, spices and about a third of the BBQ sauce.
  2. Cut peppers and onions appropriately.
  3. Marinate meat for 1 to 2 hours.
  4. Thread everything alternately onto skewers.
  5. Fry the skewers all over over medium grill heat and let them simmer until done.
  6. During the last few minutes, brush lightly with the remaining BBQ sauce several times.
  7. Be careful not to burn the sauce too early.
  8. Serve with coleslaw, bread or baked potatoes.
