Coq au Vin

4 people · Braised dishes / Holiday meals

Chicken slowly braised in red wine – a Burgundian classic for special occasions. Sven's family-friendly version with bacon, mushrooms and a rich sauce.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 25 minutes
  • Braising time: approx. 90 minutes
  • Total: approx. 2 hours

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg chicken thighs
  • 200 g bacon
  • 250 g mushrooms
  • 2 onions
  • 2 carrots
  • 500 ml red wine
  • 300 ml chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 bay leaf
  • salt
  • pepper
  • clarified butter

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken and sear well.
  2. Roast the bacon and vegetables.
  3. Add the tomato paste.
  4. Deglaze with wine and stock.
  5. Return the chicken.
  6. Braise for 90 minutes.
  7. Add mushrooms near the end and season.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

Make ahead – tastes even better the next day.

What side dish goes well?

Serve with potatoes or baguette.
