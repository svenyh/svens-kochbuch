Classic tomato and cucumber salad

3-4 people · Salads/side dishes

Tomato and cucumber salad is one of those simple companions that always goes well. Fresh, light and with a clear dressing, it is a typical everyday and grill classic.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 10 minutes
  • No waiting necessary

Ingredients

  • 4 tomatoes
  • 1 cucumber
  • 1 small onion
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp vinegar
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Parsley

Instructions

  1. Cut tomatoes and cucumber.
  2. Chop onion finely.
  3. Mix oil and vinegar and season.
  4. Mix everything and sprinkle with parsley.
