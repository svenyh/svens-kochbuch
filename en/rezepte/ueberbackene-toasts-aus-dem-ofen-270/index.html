Quick cuisine

Baked toast from the oven

For 2-3 people · Quick cuisine/snacks

Quick baked toasts with cheese and savory filling. Good for a simple evening or a small hunger.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 10-12 minutes at 200°C

Ingredients

  • 6 slices of toast bread
  • 150 g cooked ham or salami
  • 150g grated cheese
  • 2 tbsp crème fraîche
  • 1 small tomato
  • 1/2 pepper
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • oregano

Instructions

  1. Cut the ham or salami into small pieces.
  2. Finely dice the tomatoes and peppers.
  3. Mix everything with cheese and crème fraîche.
  4. Season with salt, pepper and oregano.
  5. Spread on the toast slices.
  6. Place on a baking sheet.
  7. Bake in the oven until the cheese is melted and lightly browned.
  8. Serve hot.
