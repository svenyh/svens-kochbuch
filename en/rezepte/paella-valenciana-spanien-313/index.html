Paella Valenciana – Spain

4 people · Culinary World Tour / Europe

Golden paella with chicken, green beans and peppers – Sven's Spanish version for pan or grill. Do not stir again until a light crust forms at the bottom.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Total: approx. 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 300 g paella rice
  • 500 g chicken
  • 200 g green beans
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 900 ml chicken stock
  • saffron or turmeric
  • sweet paprika
  • olive oil
  • lemon

Instructions

  1. Sear the chicken.
  2. Sauté the vegetables.
  3. Add the rice, season and pour in the stock.
  4. Do not stir again.
  5. Cook for 20 minutes until a light crust forms at the bottom.
  6. Serve with lemon.
