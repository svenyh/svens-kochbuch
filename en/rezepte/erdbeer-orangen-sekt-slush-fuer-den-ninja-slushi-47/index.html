Strawberry-orange sparkling wine slush for the Ninja Slushi

2-3 glasses · Cocktails/Ninja Sushi

The strawberry-orange slush is fruity, slightly tangy and just right for warm days or a relaxed evening. The combination of berries, orange and sparkling wine has a summery feel without being too heavy or artificially sweet.

Timing

  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Machine: approx. 15-30 minutes
  • Total: approx. 20-35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 250 ml strawberry nectar
  • 150 ml orange juice
  • 100 ml still water
  • 100 ml dry sparkling wine
  • optional: 1-2 tsp sugar syrup

Instructions

  1. Pre-cool all ingredients well.
  2. Mix strawberry nectar, orange juice and water.
  3. Pour the mixture into the Ninja Slushy and let it run until the desired consistency.
  4. Finally, briefly stir in the dry sparkling wine or pour it directly into the glass.
  5. Optionally season with a little sugar syrup.
