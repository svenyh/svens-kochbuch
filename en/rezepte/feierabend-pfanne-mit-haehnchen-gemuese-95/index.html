Fast cuisine

After-work pan with chicken and vegetables

2-3 people · Fast cuisine

This is exactly the kind of dish you need after a long day at work. Everything goes into a pan, is fried briefly and at the end you have a warm, honest meal without much effort. Fast, uncomplicated and still really good.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Total: approx. 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g boneless chicken breast or chicken thighs
  • 600 g waxy potatoes, pre-cooked or from the day before
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 zucchini
  • 1 large onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika powder
  • Salt, pepper
  • optional: some crème fraîche or herbs to serve

Instructions

  1. Cut the potatoes into cubes, divide the vegetables into bite-sized pieces and cut the chicken into strips.
  2. Heat oil in a large pan and fry the chicken vigorously.
  3. Add the onion and garlic and sweat briefly.
  4. Add the potatoes, peppers and zucchini to the pan and fry everything together until golden brown.
  5. Season with paprika, salt and pepper and cook for a few more minutes.
  6. Serve with some crème fraîche or fresh herbs if desired.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Also goes well with broccoli or carrots.
