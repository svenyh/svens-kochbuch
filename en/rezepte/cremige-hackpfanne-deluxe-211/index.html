Creamy minced pan deluxe

4 people · Stir fry

This creamy minced skillet is a true everyday classic with a little more depth and comfort. It looks familiar, tastes well-rounded and fits well into a modern family kitchen.

Ingredients

  • 500g mixed minced meat
  • 1 onion
  • 1 red pepper
  • 150 g mushrooms
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 200 ml cream
  • 100 ml broth
  • 100 g cream cheese
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 1 tbsp parsley

Instructions

  1. Chop the onion, peppers and mushrooms into small pieces.
  2. Heat oil in a large pan and fry the minced meat vigorously.
  3. Add onion, peppers and mushrooms and fry for 5 minutes.
  4. Stir in tomato paste and roast briefly. Pour in the broth and cream.
  5. Stir in cream cheese, mustard and paprika powder and let the sauce simmer for 4 to 5 minutes until creamy.
  6. Season with salt and pepper and serve with parsley.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

The pan goes well with rice, potatoes or tagliatelle.
