Mediterranean grilled skewers with halloumi, vegetables and herb oil

4 people · Grill &amp; BBQ

Mediterranean grilled skewers with halloumi, peppers, zucchini, onions and mushrooms. A meatless grill variant with herbal oil.

Timing

  • Preparation: 20 minutes | Grilling time: 10-15 minutes | Total time: approx. 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 250g halloumi
  • 1 zucchini
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 yellow pepper
  • 2 red onions
  • 250 g mushrooms
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Wooden or metal skewers

Instructions

  1. Cut the halloumi into large cubes.
  2. Cut vegetables into bite-sized pieces.
  3. Place the wooden skewers in water for about 20 minutes beforehand.
  4. Skewer the halloumi and vegetables alternately.
  5. Mix olive oil with oregano, thyme, garlic, lemon juice, salt and pepper.
  6. Brush skewers with herb oil.
  7. Grill all over on the grill over medium heat.
  8. After about 10-15 minutes, the vegetables and halloumi should be lightly browned.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Goes well with tzatziki, flatbread or a tomato salad.
