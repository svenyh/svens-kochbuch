Massaman Curry – Thailand

4 people · Culinary World Tour / World Tour

Mild Thai massaman curry with potatoes and peanuts – Sven's version for everyone who wants aromatic food without extreme heat.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 45 minutes
  • Total: approx. 60 minutes

Ingredients

  • 700 g chicken or beef
  • 500 g potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • massaman curry paste
  • peanuts
  • fish sauce optional
  • lime
  • sugar
  • stock

Instructions

  1. Sear the meat.
  2. Toast the curry paste in coconut milk.
  3. Add the potatoes, onions, meat and stock.
  4. Braise gently until everything is tender.
  5. Season with lime.
