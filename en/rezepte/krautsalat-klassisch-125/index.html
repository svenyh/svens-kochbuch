Classic coleslaw

4 people · Salads / classics

Coleslaw is exactly the kind of salad that always goes with grilling, currywurst or schnitzel. With vinegar, oil and a little sugar it stays crunchy and gets exactly that typical snack and grill character.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Brewing time: approx. 60 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 75 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1/2 white cabbage
  • 1 small onion
  • 3 tbsp vinegar
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut or slice the white cabbage very finely.
  2. Dice the onion finely.
  3. Mix vinegar, oil, sugar, salt and pepper.
  4. Mix with the cabbage and knead vigorously.
  5. Let it steep for at least 1 hour.
