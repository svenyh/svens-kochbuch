Pizza

Classic pizza dough Sven style

2 large pizzas or 1 sheet pizza · Pizza / Basics & Sauces

A classic yeast dough for pizza that is suitable for everyday use and works very well both on the tray and as a round pizza.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Walking time: approx. 45-60 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 10-15 minutes at 230-250°C

Ingredients

  • 500 g wheat flour type 550
  • 1 cube of fresh yeast or 1 packet of dry yeast
  • 325 ml lukewarm water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Pour the lukewarm water into a bowl and dissolve the sugar and yeast in it.
  2. Let stand for 5-10 minutes until the yeast works slightly.
  3. Add flour, salt and olive oil and knead everything for 8-10 minutes to form a smooth, elastic dough.
  4. Cover the dough and let it rise in a warm place for 45-60 minutes.
  5. Then knead briefly and roll out thinly or slightly thicker depending on the pizza you want.
  6. Top with sauce and toppings of your choice and bake in a very hot oven.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

If you want more flavor, let the dough rise overnight in the refrigerator.
