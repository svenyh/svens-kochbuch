Wild berry sparkling wine slush for the Ninja Slushi

2-3 glasses · Cocktails/Ninja Sushi

A berry sparkling wine slush for the Ninja Slushi with forest fruit nectar, grape juice and rosé sparkling wine – fruity and well balanced. Add rosé sparkling wine at the end. Pairs with strawberry-orange sparkling slush and mango passion fruit rum slush.

Timing

  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Machine: approx. 15-30 minutes
  • Total: approx. 20-35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 250 ml forest fruit or currant nectar
  • 100 ml red grape juice
  • 100 ml still water
  • 100 ml rosé sparkling wine
  • optional: 1 tsp sugar syrup

Instructions

  1. Cool all ingredients well.
  2. Mix nectar, grape juice and water.
  3. Pour into the Ninja Slushy and let run until desired consistency.
  4. Stir in rosé sparkling wine just before serving or pour it directly into the glass.
  5. Optionally top off with a little sugar syrup.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Slush tastes best fresh; leftovers keep in the fridge only briefly.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Optionally top off with a little sugar syrup.
