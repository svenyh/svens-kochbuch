Parmigiana di Melanzane

4 people · Italian inspired cuisine / oven dishes

Parmigiana di Melanzane is an aromatic oven dish that brings together eggplant, tomato and cheese in a very rounded way. A nice recipe for anyone who likes Italian vegetable dishes a little stronger.

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggplants
  • 700 g pureed tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 balls of mozzarella
  • 70g Parmesan
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 handful of basil
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut the eggplant into slices, season lightly with salt and let sit for 20 minutes. Then pat dry.
  2. Fry the slices in batches in a little olive oil until golden brown or roast them in the oven.
  3. Finely dice the onion and garlic. Sauté in a little olive oil, add the pureed tomatoes and simmer with sugar, oregano, salt and pepper for 15 minutes.
  4. Cut mozzarella into slices.
  5. Preheat the oven to 180 °C top/bottom heat.
  6. Line a baking dish with some tomato sauce, then layer the eggplant, sauce, mozzarella, parmesan and basil.
  7. Continue like this until everything is used up. Finish with sauce, cheese and some basil on top.
  8. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes. Let rest for 10 minutes before serving.
