Kaiserschmarrn – Austria

4 people · Culinary World Tour / Europe

Fluffy Kaiserschmarrn with icing sugar and apple compote – Sven's Austrian classic for sweet evenings. Optionally without raisins so everyone enjoys it.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 250 ml milk
  • 150 g flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • pinch of salt
  • butter
  • raisins optional
  • icing sugar
  • apple compote or plum compote

Instructions

  1. Separate the eggs.
  2. Whisk the yolks with milk, flour, sugar and salt.
  3. Beat the egg whites until stiff and fold in.
  4. Fry in butter, tear apart and caramelise.
