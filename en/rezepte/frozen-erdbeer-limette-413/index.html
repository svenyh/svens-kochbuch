Frozen Strawberry Lime

approx. 2–3 glasses · Summer drinks, lemonades & mocktails

A frozen strawberry and lime drink – icy, fruity, and lightning-fast in the Thermomix.

Timing

  • Prep time: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g frozen strawberries
  • 2 limes
  • 250 ml water
  • ice cubes

Instructions

  1. Add everything to the mixing bowl and blend 30 Sek. / Stufe 10.
  2. Serve immediately.
