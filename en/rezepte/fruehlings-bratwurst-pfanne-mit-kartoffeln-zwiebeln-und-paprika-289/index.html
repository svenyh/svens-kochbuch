Spring bratwurst pan with potatoes, onions and peppers

4 people · Pfannengerichte / Grill &amp; BBQ

Hearty bratwurst pan with potatoes, peppers and onions. Good for plancha, grill pan or large pan.

Timing

  • Preparation: 15-20 minutes | Grilling time: 20 minutes | Total time: approx. 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 5-6 coarse sausages
  • 800 g small potatoes
  • 2 peppers
  • 2 red onions
  • 1 bunch of spring onions
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika powder
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: 1 tbsp mustard
  • optional: some parsley

Instructions

  1. Halve the potatoes and pre-cook them for about 10 minutes.
  2. Cut the sausages into coarse pieces.
  3. Cut peppers, onions and spring onions.
  4. Heat grill pan or plancha.
  5. Fry potatoes in oil until they get color.
  6. Add the bratwurst pieces and fry vigorously all over.
  7. Add peppers and onions.
  8. Season with paprika, salt and pepper.
  9. Optionally stir in some mustard.
  10. Finally add spring onions and parsley on top.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

Very good leftover food if there are still potatoes from the day before.

What side dish goes well?

Serve with ajvar or herb quark.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Optionally stir in some mustard.
