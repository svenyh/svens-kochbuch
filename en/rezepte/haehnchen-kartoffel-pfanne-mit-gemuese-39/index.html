Chicken and potato pan with vegetables

2 people · Fast cuisine

This is exactly the kind of everyday dish that's filling without being boring. Everything fits together, nothing is unnecessarily complicated and in the end there is an honest plate on the table.

Timing

  • Total time: 45 minutes
  • Preparation: 10 minutes
  • Oven time: 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 400–450 g shredded chicken
  • 600-700g potatoes
  • 1-2 onions
  • 1 pepper
  • 1 small zucchini
  • 150-200g mushrooms
  • 1.5-2 tbsp oil
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional garlic powder
  • 150 g yogurt as a dip

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 200 °C fan oven.
  2. Cut potatoes into bite-sized cubes. Cut onions into wedges. Roughly chop the peppers, zucchini and mushrooms.
  3. If necessary, cut the shredded chicken a little smaller.
  4. Mix everything in a large bowl with oil, paprika powder, salt, pepper and optionally a little garlic powder.
  5. Pour the mixture into a baking dish and cook covered for 25 minutes.
  6. Remove the cover, turn everything once and bake for another 15-20 minutes until the potatoes are tender and the chicken is cooked through.
  7. Optionally use the grill function for 3-5 minutes at the end to create more roasted aromas.
  8. Serve with yogurt dip.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

The yogurt dip goes well with salt, pepper and a little lemon juice.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Optionally use the grill function for 3-5 minutes at the end to create more roasted aromas.
