Classic shashlik pot with paprika sauce

4 people · Stews / Classics / Hearty cuisine

A classic shish kebab pot with paprika sauce doesn't require any complicated technique, just enough time for the meat, onions and sauce to come together. This is exactly what makes the dish so reliable and popular.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Braising time: approx. 35-45 minutes
  • Total: approx. 60 minutes
  • Also good for preparation

Ingredients

  • 1kg pork
  • 3 onions
  • 2 peppers
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp paprika powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 300 ml paprika sauce
  • 150 ml broth
  • optional: 1 teaspoon mustard or a little sugar to round it off

Instructions

  1. Cut the meat into cubes and fry it in a pot or roaster.
  2. Add the onions and peppers and fry until both are lightly colored.
  3. Stir in the paprika powder and deglaze immediately with the broth and paprika sauce.
  4. Cover and let everything simmer over low heat for 35 to 45 minutes.
  5. Stir occasionally and add a little more liquid if necessary.
  6. At the end, let the sauce simmer slightly, uncovered.
  7. Season with salt, pepper and optionally a little mustard or sugar.
  8. Serve with bread, rice or potatoes.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?
