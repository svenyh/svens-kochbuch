Classic pot roast with dark sauce

6 people · Oven dishes/holiday meals

A good braise doesn't need any tricks, just time, roasted flavors and a well-constructed sauce. When meat, vegetables and liquid come together harmoniously, you get exactly the strong, dark Sunday cuisine you hope for.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 25 minutes
  • Fry: approx. 15 minutes
  • Braising time: approx. 2.5 to 3 hours
  • Total: approx. 3 to 3.5 hours

Ingredients

  • 1.8 kg roast beef
  • 2 tbsp clarified butter
  • 2 large onions
  • 2 carrots
  • 1/4 celeriac
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 500 ml red wine
  • 700 to 1000 ml beef stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp peppercorns
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Pat the roast dry, season with salt and pepper.
  2. Fry vigorously all over in the clarified butter in the roasting pan.
  3. Remove the meat briefly. Roast onions, carrots and celery.
  4. Add tomato paste and roast briefly.
  5. Deglaze with red wine and let it boil down a little.
  6. Put the roast back in, fill with stock, add bay leaf and peppercorns.
  7. Simmer with a lid at 160 to 170 °C for about 2.5 to 3 hours until the meat is tender.
  8. Remove the roast, puree the sauce or strain it through a sieve and reduce as desired.
