Korean grilled bulgogi

3-4 people · Grill & BBQ / meat dishes

Grilled bulgogi brings sweetness, spice and roasted flavors together very directly. If the meat is well marinated and grilled hot, the result is a grilled dish that is ready quickly and still has significantly more depth than standard skewers.

Timing

  • Preparation: 15 minutes
  • Marinate: 30-60 minutes
  • Grill: 5-8 minutes over high direct and medium indirect heat
  • Rest time: 2 minutes
  • Total: approx. 50-80 minutes

Ingredients

  • 700 g beef rump or entrecôte, sliced ​​very thinly
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 small piece of ginger
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar or apple cider vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon chili flakes or gochugaru
  • 1 tsp sesame
  • 2 spring onions

Instructions

  1. Finely grate or chop the garlic, ginger and onion very finely.
  2. Mix with soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil, vinegar and chili to make a marinade.
  3. Mix the meat with the marinade and let it steep for at least 30 minutes.
  4. Preheat the grill or plancha strongly.
  5. Preheat the grill strongly. First grill the meat for 1 to 2 minutes per side over direct high heat and then let it finish over medium indirect heat for 3 to 4 minutes.
  6. Before serving, let it rest for 2 minutes so that the meat juices are distributed.
  7. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and spring onions and serve immediately.
