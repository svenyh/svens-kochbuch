Schaumburg wedding soup

4 people · Wölpinghausen specialties / soups

This wedding soup is a real regional holiday soup with a calm depth. The broth, filling and meat components mesh neatly together, so that in the end you get exactly what you want from such a classic: something celebratory, down-to-earth and very warm for the table.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 30-40 minutes
  • Total: approx. 50-60 minutes
  • For a festive meal, let it steep for a good 30 minutes before serving

Ingredients

  • 1.5 l strong beef or chicken broth
  • 300 g soup meat or boiled beef
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 piece of celery
  • 1 leek stalk
  • 100 g asparagus pieces, optional
  • 100 g egg custard
  • 8-10 small dumplings or marrow dumplings
  • 100 g fine noodles or soup noodles
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: some nutmeg

Instructions

  1. Heat the broth in a large pot and, if necessary, cut the soup meat into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Clean and finely chop the carrots, celery and leek and cook gently in the broth for a few minutes.
  3. Cook noodles separately or directly in the soup until al dente, depending on how clear you want the broth to remain.
  4. Cut the egg custard into cubes and add it to the hot, no longer boiling soup together with the dumplings.
  5. Add the meat and optionally asparagus pieces and let everything sit for a few minutes.
  6. Season with salt, pepper and a little nutmeg.
  7. Before serving, sprinkle with parsley and serve hot in deep plates or cups.
