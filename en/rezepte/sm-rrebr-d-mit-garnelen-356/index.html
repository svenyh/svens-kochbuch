Smørrebrød with prawns

4 people · Snacks & small dishes

Danish open sandwiches with prawns, cream cheese and dill – prettily arranged, light and perfect as a snack or small meal.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 8 slices rye bread
  • 300 g prawns
  • 150 g cream cheese
  • 1 lemon
  • dill
  • lettuce leaves

Instructions

  1. Spread the bread with cream cheese.
  2. Add lettuce.
  3. Arrange the prawns.
  4. Garnish with dill and lemon.
