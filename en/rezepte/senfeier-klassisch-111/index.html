Classic mustard celebration

3-4 people · Classics & home cooking

Mustard celebrations are one of those simple classics that still immediately bring back childhood memories. Creamy mustard sauce, boiled eggs and potatoes - that's all you often need for really good home cooking.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Cook eggs: 8-10 minutes
  • Sauce: approx. 8 minutes over low heat
  • Total time: approx. 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 6 eggs
  • 40g butter
  • 40g flour
  • 500 ml milk
  • 2-3 tbsp medium hot mustard
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Potatoes as a side dish

Instructions

  1. Depending on size, boil eggs hard for 8 to 10 minutes, rinse and peel.
  2. Melt butter, stir in flour and pour in milk while stirring.
  3. Simmer the sauce over low heat for 5 to 8 minutes until creamy.
  4. Season with mustard, sugar, salt, pepper and, if desired, a little nutmeg.
  5. Halve the eggs or place them whole in the sauce and let them simmer for 2 minutes.
  6. Serve with boiled or jacket potatoes.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Stir in the mustard at the end to keep the taste fresh.

What side dish goes well?

Serve with boiled or jacket potatoes.
