Watermelon Lime Mint Agua Fresca

approx. 6 glasses · Summer horchatas & aguas frescas

A light, ice-cold summer drink made with watermelon, lime, and mint. Fruity, fresh, and perfect in hot weather.

Timing

  • Prep time: 10 minutes
  • Chill time: 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 kg watermelon, rind removed and as seedless as possible
  • juice of 2 limes
  • 300 ml cold water
  • 1–2 tbsp agave syrup or honey, optional
  • 8–10 ice cubes
  • 8–10 mint leaves
  • optional: lime slices for serving

Instructions

  1. Dice the watermelon.
  2. Add watermelon, lime juice, water, optional agave syrup or honey, and ice cubes to the mixing bowl.
  3. Blend 30 Sek. / Stufe 8–10.
  4. Add mint leaves and stir in 5 Sek. / Stufe 4.
  5. Taste and add more lime or sweetness if needed.
  6. Chill for 30 minutes or serve immediately over ice.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

Add watermelon, lime juice, water, optional agave syrup or honey, and ice cubes to the mixing bowl.
