Melon and feta salad with mint

2-3 people · Side dishes/salads

Melon, feta, and mint together are just the kind of salad that works right away in the summer. It is fresh, salty, juicy and particularly strong when you want something light and cool to go with the grilled food.

Timing

  • Prep: 10-15 minutes
  • Optional cooling time: 10 minutes
  • Total: approx. 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600g watermelon
  • 150g feta
  • 1 small handful of fresh mint leaves
  • 1 lime
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • freshly ground pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut the watermelon into bite-sized cubes and remove any seeds.
  2. Roughly crumble the feta or cut it into small pieces.
  3. Roughly chop the mint leaves.
  4. Make a quick dressing with lime juice and olive oil.
  5. Mix the melon, feta and mint loosely, drizzle with the dressing and finally refine with a little pepper.
  6. Serve well chilled - the salad tastes best especially on warm days.
