Cabanossi potato pan

For 3 people · Quick cuisine / stir-fry dishes

Quick, hearty pan with potatoes, cabanossi and peppers. Perfect for leftover potatoes.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 15-18 minutes

Ingredients

  • 800 g pre-cooked potatoes
  • 250 g Cabanossi
  • 1 pepper
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Paprika powder
  • 1 tbsp ajvar optional

Instructions

  1. Cut potatoes into slices.
  2. Cut cabanossi into pieces.
  3. Dice peppers and onions.
  4. Heat oil in a large pan.
  5. Fry potatoes until golden brown.
  6. Add cabanossi, peppers and onion.
  7. Continue frying everything together.
  8. Stir in some ajvar if desired.
  9. Season with salt, pepper and paprika powder.
