Bratwurst with sauerkraut from the oven

2 people · Oven dishes

Sometimes the down-to-earth cuisine is the best. Everything comes together nicely in the oven, the sauerkraut becomes rounder and the bratwurst gets exactly that hearty flavor that you like in such dishes.

Timing

  • Total time: 40 minutes
  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Oven time: 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 bratwursts
  • 400-500g sauerkraut
  • 1 red or white onion
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • a small pinch of garlic powder
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optionally 1 teaspoon mustard
  • optionally 1 pinch of sugar

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 190 °C fan oven.
  2. Place the sauerkraut and juice in a baking dish.
  3. Cut the onion into strips and mix it into the sauerkraut with oil, a little garlic powder, salt and pepper.
  4. Optionally incorporate mustard or a small pinch of sugar.
  5. Place the bratwursts raw on the sauerkraut and do not pierce them.
  6. Bake the dish on the middle shelf for 25 minutes.
  7. Carefully turn the sausages over and bake for another 10-15 minutes until golden brown.
  8. Optionally use the grill function for 3-5 minutes at the end.
  9. Let rest for 2-3 minutes before serving.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

It is better to bake fine grilled sausages at 190 °C instead of 200 °C so that they do not burst.
