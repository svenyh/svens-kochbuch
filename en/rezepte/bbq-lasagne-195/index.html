BBQ lasagna

4 people · Oven dishes

This BBQ lasagna combines classic layers with a spicy, slightly smoky note. Hearty, juicy and just right if you want something a little stronger out of the oven.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagne sheets
  • 500 g minced beef
  • 1 large onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 500 g pureed tomatoes
  • 150 ml BBQ sauce
  • 150 ml beef broth
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika powder
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • 50g butter
  • 50g flour
  • 600 ml milk
  • 150g grated cheddar
  • 50 g grated Parmesan
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Finely dice the onion and garlic. Heat the olive oil in a large pot and fry the minced meat vigorously until crumbly.
  2. Add onion and garlic and fry for 3 to 4 minutes. Stir in tomato paste and fry briefly.
  3. Pour in the pureed tomatoes, BBQ sauce and broth. Season with both types of peppers, brown sugar, salt and pepper. Allow the sauce to simmer gently for 20 minutes.
  4. For the light sauce, melt butter in a saucepan, stir in flour and sauté for 1 minute. Gradually pour in the milk while stirring and let it boil until creamy for 5 minutes.
  5. Season the light sauce with salt and pepper and stir in 30 g Parmesan.
  6. Preheat the oven to 180 °C top/bottom heat. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on a baking dish.
  7. Layer the lasagna sheets, BBQ minced sauce and light sauce alternately. Finish with light sauce and sprinkle cheddar cheese and remaining Parmesan on top.
  8. Bake the lasagne for 35 to 40 minutes, until the top is golden brown. Let rest for 10 minutes before cutting.
