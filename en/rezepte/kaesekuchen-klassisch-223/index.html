Classic cheesecake

1 springform pan · Baking & sweets / classics & home cooking

A classic cheesecake with quark that stays juicy and is easy to make. Pure, down-to-earth and always appropriate.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 55-65 minutes at 170 °C top/bottom heat
  • Cooling time: at least 1 hour

Ingredients

  • 750 g quark
  • 200g sugar
  • 1 packet of vanilla sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 200 ml cream
  • 1 packet of vanilla pudding powder
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • optional: 200 g butter biscuits or shortcrust pastry base

Instructions

  1. Grease a springform pan or line it with baking paper and optionally prepare the base.
  2. Mix the quark, sugar, vanilla sugar, eggs, cream, pudding powder, butter and lemon juice until smooth.
  3. Pour the mixture into the mold.
  4. Bake in the preheated oven until the cake is set and lightly golden.
  5. Allow to cool slowly in the switched off oven with the door slightly open.
