Honeydew Mint Agua Fresca

6 glasses · Summer horchatas & aguas frescas

An especially light summer drink with delicate sweetness and a fresh mint note.

Timing

  • Prep time: 10 minutes
  • Chill time: 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 small honeydew melon
  • 500 ml cold water
  • juice of 1 lime
  • 10 mint leaves
  • ice cubes
  • optional agave syrup

Instructions

  1. Dice the honeydew melon.
  2. Blend all ingredients 30 Sek. / Stufe 10.
  3. Chill briefly.
  4. Serve with ice.
