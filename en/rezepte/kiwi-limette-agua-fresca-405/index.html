Kiwi Lime Agua Fresca

6 glasses · Summer horchatas & aguas frescas

Fresh, slightly tangy, and wonderfully green - perfect for hot summer days.

Timing

  • Prep time: 10 minutes
  • Chill time: 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 6 kiwis
  • 500 ml water
  • juice of 2 limes
  • ice cubes
  • optional agave syrup

Instructions

  1. Add everything to the mixing bowl.
  2. Blend 30 Sek. / Stufe 10.
  3. Taste.
  4. Serve chilled.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

The Kiwi-Lime Agua Fresca can chill 1–2 hours before or be prepared as a base the day before.

How long does it keep?

Leftovers of the Kiwi-Lime Agua Fresca keep in the fridge for 1–2 days in an airtight container.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Agave syrup or a little extra fruit works instead of honey for sweetness.
