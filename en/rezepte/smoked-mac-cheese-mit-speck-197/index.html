Smoked Mac & Cheese with Bacon

4 people · Pasta dishes

Smoked Mac & Cheese with bacon is pure comfort food with a strong character. Creamy cheese sauce, crispy bacon and a golden crust make for a well-rounded oven dish.

Ingredients

  • 350g macaroni
  • 150 g bacon cubes
  • 40g butter
  • 40g flour
  • 500 ml milk
  • 100 ml cream
  • 150 g cheddar cheese, grated
  • 100 g Gouda, grated
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika powder
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 30g Parmesan
  • 2 tbsp breadcrumbs
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the macaroni in salted water until just al dente. Drain and set aside.
  2. Leave the bacon cubes crispy in a pan without any additional fat. Take out.
  3. Melt butter in a saucepan, stir in flour and sauté for 1 minute. Gradually pour in the milk and cream and stir until smooth.
  4. Let the sauce simmer gently for 5 minutes. Stir in Cheddar, Gouda, mustard and smoked paprika.
  5. Season with salt and pepper. Add the macaroni and bacon to the sauce and mix well.
  6. Pour everything into a baking dish. Mix Parmesan and breadcrumbs and sprinkle on top.
  7. Bake at 200 °C top/bottom heat for 15 to 20 minutes until the surface is golden brown.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

The smoked paprika powder replaces the smoker effect for everyday use and gives the dish the right depth.
