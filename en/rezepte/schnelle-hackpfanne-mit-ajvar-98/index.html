Quick mince pan with ajvar

2-3 people · Fast cuisine

The quick minced pan with ajvar is a good example of immediate after-work cooking with a strong taste. A few steps, a well-rounded seasoning and very uncomplicated preparation make them particularly practical.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Frying and simmering: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500g minced meat
  • 1 onion
  • 1 pepper
  • 2-3 tbsp ajvar
  • 100 ml cream or cooking cream
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt, pepper
  • optional: parsley

Instructions

  1. Dice the onion and chop the pepper into small pieces.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan and fry the minced meat until crumbly and vigorously.
  3. Add the onion and pepper and fry for 4 to 5 minutes until everything takes on color.
  4. Stir in Ajvar and cream and simmer gently for 5 minutes.
  5. Season with salt, pepper and, if desired, a little paprika powder or chili.
  6. Finally add parsley or spring onions on top.
  7. Serve with bread, rice or potatoes.
