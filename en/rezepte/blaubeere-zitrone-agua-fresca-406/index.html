Blueberry Lemon Agua Fresca

6 glasses · Summer horchatas & aguas frescas

Fruity, slightly tart, and wonderfully refreshing.

Timing

  • Prep time: 10 minutes
  • Chill time: 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 300 g blueberries
  • juice of 2 lemons
  • 500 ml water
  • ice cubes
  • optional honey

Instructions

  1. Blend all ingredients 30 Sek. / Stufe 10.
  2. Taste.
  3. Serve well chilled.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

The Blueberry-Lemon Agua Fresca can chill 1–2 hours before or be prepared as a base the day before.

How long does it keep?

Leftovers of the Blueberry-Lemon Agua Fresca keep in the fridge for 1–2 days in an airtight container.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Agave syrup or a little extra fruit works instead of honey for sweetness.
