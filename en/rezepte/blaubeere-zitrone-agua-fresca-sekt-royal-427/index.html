Blueberry Lemon Agua Fresca Sekt Royal

1 glass · Summer horchatas & aguas frescas

Light summer aperitif: Blueberry Lemon Agua Fresca Sekt Royal. Dry sparkling wine adds festive bubbles – perfect as a summer aperitif.

Timing

  • Prep time: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 100 ml Blueberry Lemon Agua Fresca
  • 120 ml well-chilled dry sparkling wine
  • Ice cubes
  • lemon slice and fresh blueberries

Instructions

  1. Chill the base drink (Blueberry Lemon Agua Fresca) and alcohol well.
  2. Fill a champagne flute with ice cubes.
  3. Pour 100 ml Blueberry Lemon Agua Fresca first.
  4. Top up carefully with 120 ml sparkling wine.
  5. Stir gently, garnish with matching fruit and serve immediately.
