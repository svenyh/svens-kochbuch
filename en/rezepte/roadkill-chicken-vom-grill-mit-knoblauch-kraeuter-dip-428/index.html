Roadkill chicken from the grill with garlic herb dip

2–4 people · Grill & BBQ

Inspired by rustic Romanian grilling techniques: crispy spatchcock chicken from the grill, dipped again and again in a bold garlic-herb baste or brushed generously with it. Smoky and crunchy outside, juicy inside, with garlic, paprika and fresh herbs.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Indirect grilling: approx. 35–45 minutes
  • Direct finish: approx. 5–10 minutes
  • Resting: 5–10 minutes
  • Total: approx. 70–80 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, approx. 1.3–1.6 kg
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • optional a little chili
  • Garlic herb baste:
  • 250 ml warm water
  • 4–6 garlic cloves, finely grated or pressed
  • 1 small onion, very finely grated or minced
  • 1 bunch parsley, finely chopped
  • optional a little coriander
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • a splash of lemon juice or vinegar

Instructions

  1. Place the chicken breast-side down on a board and cut out the backbone with poultry shears.
  2. Turn the chicken over and press firmly on the breastbone with both hands until it lies flat.
  3. Mix oil with paprika, salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder and optional chili. Rub the chicken all over.
  4. Prepare the grill for indirect heat, approx. 180–200 °C.
  5. Grill indirectly, skin side up, for approx. 35–45 minutes.
  6. Mix the garlic herb baste.
  7. Once the chicken colours, remove from the grill every 10–15 minutes, dip in the baste or brush generously, and return to the grill.
  8. Finish with the skin side over direct heat until crisp.
  9. Done when the thickest part reaches approx. 74–78 °C core temperature.
  10. Rest 5–10 minutes before carving.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

Mix oil with paprika, salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder and optional chili. Rub the chicken all over.
