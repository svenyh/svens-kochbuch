Elote – Mexican grilled corn

4 people · Grill & BBQ

Grilled corn on the cob with mayo, cheese and chilli – popular Mexican street food that delights at any barbecue.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Grilling: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 corn cobs
  • 100 g mayonnaise
  • 50 g cheese
  • chilli powder
  • lime

Instructions

  1. Grill the corn.
  2. Spread with mayo.
  3. Top with cheese and chilli.
  4. Serve with lime.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

Cotija is authentic – feta works as a substitute.
