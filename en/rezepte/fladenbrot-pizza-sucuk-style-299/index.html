Flatbread pizza Sucuk style

2-3 people · Pizza / Fast food

Crispy flatbread with spicy sucuk, peppers and cheese - a quick pizza variant with a Turkish-oriental character. Prepared in just a few minutes and ideal if you want something different to classic pizza.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 8 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 8-10 minutes
  • Total: approx. 18 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 large flatbreads
  • 150 g sucuk in slices
  • 150 ml pureed tomatoes
  • 1 pepper
  • 1 red onion
  • 150 g mozzarella or Gouda, grated
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: parsley
  • optional: pepperoni

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 220 °C top/bottom heat.
  2. Cut the peppers and onions into fine strips.
  3. Spread the flatbreads with pureed tomatoes and season.
  4. Top with cheese, peppers and onion.
  5. Arrange the sucuk slices on top.
  6. Bake for 8-10 minutes until the edges are crispy.
  7. Drizzle with olive oil and serve with parsley.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

Tomato sauce and vegetables can be prepared ahead; top the flatbread when you are ready to bake.

What side dish goes well?

Drizzle with olive oil and serve with parsley.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Spicy salami or smoked paprika sausage works instead of sucuk.
