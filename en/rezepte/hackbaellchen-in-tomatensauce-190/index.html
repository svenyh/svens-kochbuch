Meatballs in tomato sauce

4 people · Italian inspired cuisine/meat dishes

Meatballs in tomato sauce are uncomplicated, juicy and versatile. Whether with pasta, rice or bread – this dish fits well into a down-to-earth kitchen with a modern touch.

Ingredients

  • 500g mixed minced meat
  • 1 small bread roll or 40 g breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 40g Parmesan
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 700 g pureed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Soak and squeeze the rolls or provide breadcrumbs. Finely dice the onion and garlic.
  2. Mix the minced meat with the egg, bread rolls or breadcrumbs, Parmesan, a little onion, parsley, salt and pepper.
  3. Form small meatballs.
  4. Heat olive oil in a large pan and fry the meatballs all over. Take out.
  5. Sauté remaining onion and garlic in the same pan.
  6. Add the pureed tomatoes, sugar, oregano, salt and pepper and simmer for 10 minutes.
  7. Return the meatballs to the sauce and cook gently for another 10 minutes.
  8. Serve with pasta, bread or rice.
