Classic banana bread

1 loaf pan · Baking & Sweets / Quick Cuisine

An uncomplicated banana bread for very ripe bananas. Juicy, simple and good for breakfast or coffee.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 50-55 minutes at 175°C

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 250g flour
  • 120g sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g butter, melted
  • 1 packet of baking powder
  • 1 packet of vanilla sugar
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • optionally 80 g chopped chocolate or nuts

Instructions

  1. Mash the bananas with a fork.
  2. Mix eggs, sugar, vanilla sugar and melted butter.
  3. Add bananas.
  4. Mix and fold in the flour, baking powder and salt.
  5. Optionally stir in chocolate or nuts.
  6. Pour the dough into a greased loaf pan and bake in the oven until the banana bread is golden brown.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

Optionally stir in chocolate or nuts.
