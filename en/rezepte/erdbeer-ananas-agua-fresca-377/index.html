Strawberry Pineapple Agua Fresca

6 glasses · Summer horchatas & aguas frescas

Fruity, slightly exotic, and wonderfully refreshing. The combination of sweet strawberries and juicy pineapple is the perfect summer drink.

Timing

  • Prep time: 10 minutes
  • Chill time: 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 400 g strawberries
  • 500 g fresh pineapple
  • 400 ml cold water
  • juice of 1 lime
  • 6–8 ice cubes
  • optional 1 tbsp agave syrup
  • mint for serving

Instructions

  1. Roughly chop the fruit.
  2. Blend with water, lime juice, and ice 30 Sek. / Stufe 10.
  3. Taste.
  4. Serve well chilled.
