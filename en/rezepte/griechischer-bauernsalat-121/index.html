Greek farmer's salad

3-4 people · Salads / summer dishes

A Greek farmer's salad does not rely on complicated tricks, but rather on good ingredients. Tomatoes, cucumber, onions, feta and olives together create that fresh, spicy combination that always works.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 15 minutes
  • No waiting necessary

Ingredients

  • 4 tomatoes
  • 1 cucumber
  • 1 red onion
  • 200g feta
  • 100g black olives
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • oregano
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Roughly chop tomatoes, cucumber and onion.
  2. Break feta into pieces.
  3. Put everything in a bowl with the olives and oregano.
  4. Pour olive oil and vinegar over it.
  5. Season with salt and pepper.
