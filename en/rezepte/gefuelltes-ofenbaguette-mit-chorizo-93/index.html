Filled oven baguette with chorizo

3-4 people · Oven dishes/snacks

This is exactly the kind of oven snack that's on the table faster than you think. The chorizo ​​adds spice, the cheese connects everything and in the end you have an uncomplicated recipe that is perfect for guests, a barbecue or simply a cozy evening.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 15-18 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 small baguettes
  • 120g chorizo
  • 1 red pepper
  • 2 spring onions
  • 120g grated cheese
  • 2 tbsp crème fraîche
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C top/bottom heat.
  2. Cut the baguettes lengthways.
  3. Chop the chorizo, peppers and spring onions into small pieces.
  4. Mix with crème fraîche and cheese, season with pepper.
  5. Divide the mixture onto the baguettes.
  6. Bake until the cheese is melted and the baguette is crispy, about 15 to 18 minutes.
