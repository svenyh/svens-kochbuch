Pineapple rum slush for the Ninja Slushi

2-3 glasses · Cocktails/Ninja Sushi

Pineapple and rum go together just as you would want in an uncomplicated summer drink. The slush tastes tropical, cool and pleasantly light - perfect for the terrace, garden or spontaneous visit.

Timing

  • Prep: 3-5 minutes
  • Machine: approx. 15-30 minutes
  • Total: approx. 20-35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 300 ml pineapple juice
  • 100 ml still water
  • 4 cl white rum
  • optional: 1 tsp sugar syrup

Instructions

  1. Keep the pineapple juice, white rum and coconut syrup well chilled.
  2. Add all ingredients to the Ninja Slushy to taste.
  3. Start the appropriate slush program and let the mixture freeze until creamy.
  4. Check briefly from time to time whether the consistency is uniform.
  5. Pour into chilled glasses and serve immediately.

Frequently asked questions

Can I freeze this?

Start the appropriate slush program and let the mixture freeze until creamy.

How long does it keep?

Keep the pineapple juice, white rum and coconut syrup well chilled.
