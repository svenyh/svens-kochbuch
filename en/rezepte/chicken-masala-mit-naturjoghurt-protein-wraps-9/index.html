Chicken Masala with natural yogurt & protein wraps

2-3 people · Stir fry/chicken dishes

Chicken Masala with natural yogurt and protein wraps combines spicy pan-fried cuisine with an everyday serving idea. The result is aromatic, filling and yet uncomplicated enough for a normal weekday.

Timing

  • Preparation: 10 minutes
  • Cooking time: 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken breast
  • 1 onion
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala or masala spice
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 150 g natural yogurt
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 100 ml water or broth
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 4 protein wraps

Instructions

  1. Cut chicken into pieces.
  2. Fry onion.
  3. Add chicken and fry briefly.
  4. Stir in spices and tomato paste.
  5. Deglaze with a little water or broth.
  6. Let it boil down briefly.
  7. Stir in natural yogurt.
  8. No more boiling.
  9. Serve with warm protein wraps.
