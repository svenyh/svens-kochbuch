Chicken Masala with natural yogurt & protein wraps

2-3 people · Stir fry/chicken dishes

Spicy chicken in a creamy yogurt sauce – Chicken Masala combines Indian aromas with an uncomplicated pan version. The protein wraps turn it into a quick, filling weekday meal without long time at the stove.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Frying and simmering: approx. 15 minutes
  • Warming wraps: approx. 2 minutes
  • Total: approx. 25 to 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken breast
  • 1 onion
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala or masala spice
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 150 g natural yogurt
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 100 ml water or broth
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 4 protein wraps

Instructions

  1. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces and season with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat oil in a large pan and sauté the onion for 3 to 4 minutes until golden.
  3. Add the chicken and fry for 4 to 5 minutes until lightly colored on the outside.
  4. Stir in garam masala, paprika powder and tomato paste and toast for 1 minute.
  5. Deglaze with water or broth, bring to a boil and simmer over medium heat for 8 to 10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
  6. Reduce heat, stir in natural yogurt and heat through briefly – do not boil vigorously.
  7. Warm the protein wraps briefly in a dry pan or microwave.
  8. Serve the chicken masala in deep plates or directly in the warm wraps.
