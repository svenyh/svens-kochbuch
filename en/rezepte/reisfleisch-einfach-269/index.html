Quick Cooking

Rice meat easy

For 4 people · Quick Cooking / One Pot

A simple one-pot rice meat with meat, peppers and tomato paste. Warming and uncomplicated.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 25-30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 400 g pork goulash or chicken
  • 250g rice
  • 1 onion
  • 1 pepper
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 500 ml broth
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Paprika powder

Instructions

  1. Cut meat into small pieces.
  2. Chop the onion and pepper into small pieces.
  3. Heat oil in a large pot.
  4. Fry the meat vigorously.
  5. Add onion and peppers.
  6. Briefly roast the tomato paste.
  7. Sprinkle in the rice and stir briefly.
  8. Pour in the broth and season.
  9. Cook everything over a low heat until the rice and meat are soft.
  10. Season to taste and serve.
