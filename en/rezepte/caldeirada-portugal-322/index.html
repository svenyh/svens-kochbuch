Caldeirada – Portugal

4 people · Culinary World Tour / Mediterranean & North Africa

Portuguese stew with potatoes, peppers and seafood – rustic, filling and ideal for guests. Sven's coastal one-pot version.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 30 minutes
  • Total: approx. 50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600 g prawns and mussels
  • 600 g potatoes
  • 2 bell peppers
  • 2 onions
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 400 g tomatoes
  • white wine
  • olive oil
  • bay leaf
  • paprika
  • parsley

Instructions

  1. Layer the potatoes, vegetables and tomatoes.
  2. Pour over wine and a little stock.
  3. Cook for 30 minutes.
  4. Add the seafood towards the end.
