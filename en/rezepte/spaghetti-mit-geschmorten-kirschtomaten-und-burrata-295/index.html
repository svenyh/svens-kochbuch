Spaghetti with braised cherry tomatoes and burrata

2 people · Pasta dishes

No pinsa and no spicy garlic spaghetti – the focus here is on braised cherry tomatoes and creamy burrata. The pasta soaks up the sweet and sour tomato sauce, the burrata comes fresh to the plate and makes everything nice and velvety.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 5 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 250g spaghetti
  • 400 g cherry tomatoes
  • 1 scoop of burrata
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 handful of basil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: chili flakes
  • optional: grated Parmesan

Instructions

  1. Cook spaghetti in plenty of salted water until al dente.
  2. Halve the cherry tomatoes and cut the garlic into thin slices.
  3. Heat olive oil in a large pan and briefly sweat the garlic.
  4. Add tomatoes and simmer over medium heat for 8-10 minutes until soft.
  5. Season with salt, pepper and basil.
  6. Drain the spaghetti, reserving some of the pasta water.
  7. Put the pasta in the pan and mix with a little pasta water.
  8. Arrange on plates, place burrata on top, tear open slightly and serve.
