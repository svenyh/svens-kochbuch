Chicken liver with apples

3-4 people · Stir-fries/poultry dishes

Poultry liver with apples is savory, sweet and slightly sour thanks to exactly this combination. It is important to only fry the liver briefly and to allow the apples to soften without completely disintegrating.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Roast: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g of chicken liver
  • 2 apples
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 2 tbsp oil or butter
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • marjoram

Instructions

  1. Clean the liver and halve it if desired.
  2. Peel, core and cut apples into wedges.
  3. Cut onions into strips.
  4. Slowly fry the onions in a little fat until translucent and slightly golden, then remove.
  5. Fry the apples briefly so that they become soft but still retain their shape and remove them too.
  6. Dredge the liver lightly in flour and fry quickly in the pan.
  7. Add onions and apples again.
  8. Toss everything together briefly and only at the end season with salt, pepper and marjoram.
