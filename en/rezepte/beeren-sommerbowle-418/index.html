Berry Summer Punch

approx. 8–10 glasses · Summer drinks, lemonades & mocktails

A fruity berry summer punch with white grape juice – refreshing, colourful, and perfect for summer gatherings.

Timing

  • Prep time: 15 minutes
  • Chill time: 2 hours

Ingredients

  • strawberries
  • raspberries
  • blueberries
  • white grape juice
  • sparkling mineral water
  • lime
  • ice cubes
  • mint

Instructions

  1. Prepare the fruit.
  2. Mix with the grape juice.
  3. Chill for at least two hours.
  4. Add sparkling mineral water and ice just before serving.
