Danish potato bake

4 people · Oven dishes

Creamy potato bake with cream and cheese from Danish home cooking – hearty, warming and ideal as a side or main dish.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 50 minutes
  • Total: approx. 70 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg potatoes
  • 250 ml cream
  • 150 g cheese
  • 2 onions
  • salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Slice the potatoes thinly.
  2. Slice the onions.
  3. Layer in a dish.
  4. Pour over the cream.
  5. Top with cheese.
  6. Bake for 50 minutes at 180 °C.
