Winter pear and nut cake

For 1 springform pan (26 cm) · Baking & Sweets / Cakes

Juicy cake with pears, walnuts or hazelnuts and winter spices such as cinnamon and vanilla - just right for autumn and winter. Not a classic marble cake or a plum cake, but an independent nut and pear cake with a clear season.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 50-55 minutes
  • Total: approx. 75 minutes

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe pears
  • 150 g walnuts or hazelnuts, roughly chopped
  • 200 g soft butter
  • 150g sugar
  • 1 packet of vanilla sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 250g flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • optional: powdered sugar for dusting

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 175 °C top/bottom heat.
  2. Grease the springform pan and line it with baking paper.
  3. Mix butter, sugar and vanilla sugar until creamy.
  4. Stir in eggs one at a time.
  5. Mix flour, baking powder, cinnamon and salt and fold in milk.
  6. Cut the nuts and pears into cubes and fold them into the dough.
  7. Pour into the mold and smooth it out.
  8. Bake for 50-55 minutes until a stick test comes out clean.
  9. Let cool and dust with powdered sugar if desired.
