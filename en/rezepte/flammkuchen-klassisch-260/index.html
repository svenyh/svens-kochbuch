Classic tarte flambée

For 2 people · Pizza & Bread / Oven & Casseroles

Thin, crispy tarte flambée with crème fraîche, bacon and onions. A simple classic for the oven.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 12 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 10-12 minutes at 230-250°C

Ingredients

  • 250g flour
  • 125ml water
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 150 g crème fraîche
  • 100 g bacon cubes
  • 1 small onion
  • pepper
  • optionally some chives

Instructions

  1. Knead the flour, water, oil and salt into a smooth dough.
  2. Roll out thinly.
  3. Place on baking paper or tray.
  4. Spread with crème fraîche.
  5. Spread the bacon cubes on top.
  6. Cut the onion into fine rings and add them on top.
  7. Season with a little pepper.
  8. Bake until crispy in a very hot oven.
  9. Optionally serve with chives.
