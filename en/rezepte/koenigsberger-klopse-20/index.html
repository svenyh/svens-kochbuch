Königsberger Klopse

4 people · Classics & home cooking

Königsberger Klopse are a real classic that thrives on a juicy meat mixture and a calm, well-seasoned caper sauce. If the broth doesn't boil and the sauce is neatly thickened, you get exactly this familiar Sunday dish.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Total: approx. 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g minced meat (mixed)
  • 1 onion
  • 1 egg
  • 1 stale bread roll
  • 1 tbsp mustard
  • 1 liter of broth
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • 200 ml cream
  • 1 tbsp flour or cornstarch
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Soak the rolls in water and squeeze them out well.
  2. Dice the onion finely.
  3. Thoroughly mix the minced meat with the egg, rolls, onion, mustard, salt and pepper.
  4. Form even balls out of the mixture.
  5. Heat the broth in a large pot, but do not let it boil until bubbly.
  6. Carefully add the meatballs to the broth and let them simmer for about 15 minutes.
  7. Remove the dumplings and keep them warm.
  8. Add cream and capers to the broth.
  9. Thicken the sauce lightly with flour or starch and season with salt and pepper.
  10. Put the dumplings back in the sauce and serve together.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Remove the dumplings and keep them warm.
