Virgin Passion Fruit Spritz

approx. 2 glasses · Summer drinks, lemonades & mocktails

A fruity virgin passion fruit spritz – sparkling, exotic, and refreshingly non-alcoholic.

Timing

  • Prep time: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • passion fruit juice
  • lime juice
  • sparkling mineral water
  • ice cubes
  • mint

Instructions

  1. Add all ingredients to the glass.
  2. Stir gently.
  3. Serve with mint.
