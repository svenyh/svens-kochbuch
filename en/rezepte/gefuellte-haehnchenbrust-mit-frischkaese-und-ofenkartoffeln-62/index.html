Stuffed chicken breast with cream cheese and baked potatoes

2-3 people · Oven dishes / meat dishes

Stuffed chicken breast quickly seems a little more special, but is still suitable for everyday use if the filling is clean and the cooking time is right. Combined with baked potatoes, you get a well-rounded oven dish that's filling without being complicated.

Timing

  • Preparation: 15 minutes
  • Baking time: 30-35 minutes
  • Total: approx. 45-50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 large chicken breast fillets
  • 150 g natural cream cheese
  • 1 small garlic clove, finely grated
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley or chives
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 800g potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon rosemary or Italian herbs
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: 50 g grated cheese for gratinating

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 200 °C fan oven.
  2. Wash the potatoes, cut into wedges, mix with olive oil, rosemary, salt and pepper and place on a tray.
  3. Make deep cuts in the side of the chicken breast to create a pocket.
  4. Mix the cream cheese with garlic, herbs, a little salt and pepper and stuff into the chicken breasts.
  5. Rub the outside of the chicken with oil and paprika powder and place it on the tray next to the potatoes.
  6. Bake for 30-35 minutes until the potatoes are golden brown and the chicken is cooked through.
  7. Optionally add some cheese over the chicken in the last 5 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

A small salad or herb quark goes well with this.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Optionally add some cheese over the chicken in the last 5 minutes.
