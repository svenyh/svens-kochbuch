Grilled vegetable salad with feta

4 people · Grill & BBQ / Salads & Side Dishes

A lukewarm salad of roasted vegetables with feta, balsamic vinegar and lemon. Very good as a grill side dish or light dinner.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Grilling/baking time: approx. 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 zucchini
  • 1 eggplant
  • 2 peppers
  • 1 red onion
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 150g feta
  • 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • some parsley

Instructions

  1. Cut vegetables into coarse pieces.
  2. Mix with a little olive oil, salt and pepper.
  3. Roast on the grill or in the oven until it gets nice color.
  4. Let cool slightly and marinate with balsamic vinegar and lemon juice.
  5. Crumble feta over it and sprinkle with parsley.
