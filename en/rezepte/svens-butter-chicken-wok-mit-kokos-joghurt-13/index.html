Sven's butter chicken wok with coconut & yoghurt

3-4 people · Stir fry/chicken dishes

The butter chicken wok with coconut and yoghurt combines creamy seasoning with a quick, everyday preparation. A good example of how a well-known comfort dish can be implemented in a modern and uncomplicated way.

Timing

  • Preparation: 10 minutes
  • Cooking time: 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600g chicken
  • 1 can of diced tomatoes
  • 1 can of coconut milk
  • 500 g frozen wok vegetables
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 2-3 tbsp natural yogurt
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Pide or naan as a side dish

Instructions

  1. Cut the chicken into pieces and fry briefly.
  2. Do not fry completely dry.
  3. Add chopped tomatoes and coconut milk.
  4. Stir in spices.
  5. Add frozen wok vegetables.
  6. Let everything steep gently for 5-7 minutes.
  7. Add the natural yogurt at the very end.
  8. After that, don't cook hard anymore.
  9. Serve with pide or naan.

Frequently asked questions

Can I freeze this?

Add frozen wok vegetables.

What side dish goes well?

Serve with pide or naan.
