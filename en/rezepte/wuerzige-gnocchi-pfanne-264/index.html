Spicy gnocchi pan

For 2-3 people · Quick cuisine / stir-fry dishes

Quick gnocchi pan with vegetables, cabanossi and ajvar cream. Spicy, creamy and ready in a short time.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 12 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500g gnocchi
  • 1 pepper
  • 1 zucchini
  • 1 onion
  • 150 g cabanossi or diced ham
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 150 ml cream
  • 1 tbsp ajvar
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Chop the peppers, zucchini and onion into small pieces.
  2. Heat oil in a pan.
  3. Fry cabanossi briefly.
  4. Add vegetables and sauté.
  5. Add gnocchi and fry until golden.
  6. Stir in cream and ajvar.
  7. Season with salt and pepper.
  8. Let it simmer briefly and serve.
