AirFryer chicken (crispy & juicy)

2-3 people · Airfryer / Quick & easy

Crispy AirFryer Chicken delivers lots of texture and juicy meat with surprisingly little effort. That's exactly why the recipe fits so well into a modern, everyday kitchen that needs to work quickly.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Airfryer: approx. 16-22 minutes at 190°C
  • Rest time: 2-3 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600-700g boneless chicken thighs or chicken breast
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt, pepper
  • optional: some BBQ rub

Instructions

  1. Pat the chicken dry and mix evenly with the oil and spices.
  2. Preheat the AirFryer to 190°C.
  3. Distribute the meat in the basket without stacking it too much.
  4. Cook for 16 to 22 minutes, depending on thickness, turning or shaking halfway through.
  5. Check the core temperature for larger pieces; the meat should be completely cooked.
  6. Let rest for 2 to 3 minutes before serving.
  7. Serve with dip, salad, chips or wraps.
