Yoghurt cake with fruits

1 springform pan · Baking & Sweets / Quick Cuisine

A light and quick sponge cake with yogurt and fruit. Very versatile and ideal for spontaneous guests.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 35-40 minutes at 180°C

Ingredients

  • 250 g natural yogurt
  • 3 eggs
  • 180g sugar
  • 100 ml oil
  • 250g flour
  • 1 packet of baking powder
  • 1 packet of vanilla sugar
  • 250 g fruit of your choice, e.g. E.g. raspberries, blueberries or peaches

Instructions

  1. Beat eggs with sugar and vanilla sugar until fluffy.
  2. Stir in yogurt and oil.
  3. Mix the flour and baking powder and fold in.
  4. Put the dough in a greased form and spread the fruit on top.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven until the cake is golden.
  6. Let cool and sprinkle with powdered sugar if desired.

Frequently asked questions

Can I freeze this?

Do not allow frozen fruit to thaw completely beforehand.
