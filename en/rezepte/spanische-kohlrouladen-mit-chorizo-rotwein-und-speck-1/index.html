Spanish cabbage rolls with chorizo, red wine and bacon

4 people · Oven dishes/holiday meals

These Spanish cabbage rolls combine hearty filling, spicy chorizo ​​and a strong braising sauce to create a special oven dish. A recipe with a lot of depth that remains down-to-earth and still brings something festive to the table.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 30 minutes
  • Frying and preparing the sauce: approx. 20 minutes
  • Braising time in the oven: approx. 2 to 2.5 hours at 160 °C top/bottom heat
  • Resting time: approx. 10 minutes
  • Total: approx. 3 hours

Ingredients

  • 1 white cabbage
  • 500 g minced meat (mixed or beef)
  • 1 stale bread roll
  • 1 egg
  • 2 onions
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 100g chorizo
  • 150 g sliced ​​bacon
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 200 ml red wine
  • 400 ml beef stock
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Sweet paprika powder
  • Marjoram or thyme
  • oil for frying
  • Side dishes: mashed potatoes
  • Side dishes: baguette

Instructions

  1. Blanch cabbage leaves in boiling salted water until soft. Cut thick leaf veins flat.
  2. Soak the rolls in water and squeeze them out well.
  3. Cut the onions, garlic and chorizo ​​into small pieces and fry them briefly in a pan.
  4. Mix the minced meat with the soaked bread roll, egg, the fried onion-chorizo ​​mixture and the spices.
  5. Fill the cabbage leaves with the minced mixture and shape into roulades.
  6. Wrap the outside of each cabbage roll with bacon.
  7. Fry the roulades on all sides in a roasting pan.
  8. Briefly roast the tomato paste, then deglaze with red wine and top up with beef stock.
  9. Place the roulades in the sauce and braise, covered, in a preheated oven at 160 °C top/bottom heat for about 2 to 2.5 hours. Turn once in between or baste with sauce.
  10. Let the roulades rest in the sauce for 10 minutes before serving so that they cut better and stay juicy.
  11. At the end, remove the lid and let the sauce boil down. Season with salt, pepper and, if necessary, a little sugar.
