Grilled green asparagus with lemon butter

2-3 people · Grill & BBQ / Side Dishes

Grilled green asparagus doesn't need much if the heat, cooking time and lemon butter are right. Then it becomes crunchy, aromatic and exactly the elegant side dish that goes well with meat, fish or simply good bread.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Grill time: 5-8 minutes over medium to medium-high direct heat
  • Total: approx. 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g green asparagus
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 40g butter
  • 1/2 lemon
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: some garlic or freshly grated lemon peel

Instructions

  1. Break off or cut off the woody ends of the asparagus.
  2. Mix the asparagus with olive oil, a little salt and pepper.
  3. Preheat the grill to medium to medium-high direct heat and grill the asparagus for 5 to 8 minutes, turning regularly.
  4. At the same time, melt the butter and mix it with lemon juice and, if desired, a little lemon zest.
  5. Drizzle the lemon butter over the hot asparagus and serve immediately.
