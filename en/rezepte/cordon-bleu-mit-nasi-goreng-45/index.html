Cordon Bleu with Nasi Goreng

1 person · Air fryer

Here, crispy cordon bleu meets spicy nasi goreng and it is exactly this mix that makes the dish so special. It's easy, filling and ideal when you want to serve something hearty with a little more character as a standard side dish.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Airfryer: 12-15 minutes at 190°C
  • Pan for Nasi Goreng: approx. 10-12 minutes
  • Cordon Bleu resting time: 2 minutes
  • Total: approx. 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 Cordon Bleu
  • 150g cooked rice
  • 1 egg
  • Vegetables of your choice
  • Sweet chilli sauce
  • some oil
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Prepare the Cordon Bleu according to the package instructions or prepare it and loosen the rice from the day before.
  2. Meanwhile, heat some oil in a pan.
  3. Add the cooked rice, egg and vegetables to the pan and fry vigorously.
  4. Preheat the Airfryer to 190 °C and cook the Cordon Bleu for 12 to 15 minutes, depending on the size, turn once and then let it rest briefly.
  5. At the same time, heat oil in a pan, fry the onion, vegetables and rice and season with soy sauce, a little garlic and spices.
  6. Only add the egg or additional sauce to the nasi goreng at the very end so that the rice remains fluffy.
  7. Serve with sweet chili sauce.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

Prepare the Cordon Bleu according to the package instructions or prepare it and loosen the rice from the day before.

What side dish goes well?

Serve with sweet chili sauce.
