Grilled potatoes in aluminum foil with rosemary

3-4 people · Grill & BBQ / Side Dishes

Grilled potatoes in aluminum foil are a reliable classic because they are easy to prepare and almost always go well with everything. Rosemary gives them just the savory direction that turns a simple side dish into something real.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Grilling time: approx. 25-35 minutes over medium indirect heat
  • Total: approx. 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 800 g small potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon chopped rosemary
  • 1/2 tsp paprika powder
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Aluminum foil

Instructions

  1. Wash the potatoes, prick them several times and rub them with oil, salt and rosemary.
  2. Wrap tightly in aluminum foil individually or in pairs.
  3. Prepare the grill for medium indirect heat or place the potatoes on the edge of the embers.
  4. Depending on size, grill for 25 to 35 minutes, turning once in between.
  5. To check, pierce with a knife – they should be soft.
  6. Unpack and serve with butter, herb quark or sour cream.
