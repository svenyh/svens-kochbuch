Carnitas tacos

4 people · Meat dishes

Slow-braised pork, crisped in a pan and served in warm tortillas – authentic Mexican street food to make at home.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 180 minutes
  • Total: approx. 3.5 hours

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork shoulder
  • 8 tortillas
  • 2 onions
  • 1 lime
  • coriander
  • salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Braise the pork.
  2. Shred with forks.
  3. Crisp briefly in a hot pan.
  4. Serve in tortillas.
