Coleslaw creamy

4 people · Salads/BBQ

Coleslaw is the creamier and slightly rounder version of coleslaw. It goes particularly well with burgers, BBQ or oven dishes because it brings together freshness and creaminess.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Brewing time: approx. 30 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1/2 white cabbage
  • 2 carrots
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp yogurt
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Finely chop the cabbage and grate the carrots.
  2. Mix mayonnaise, yogurt, vinegar and sugar.
  3. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Mix with the vegetables.
  5. Chill before serving.
