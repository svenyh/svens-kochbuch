Mango Passion Fruit Agua Fresca

approx. 6 glasses · Summer horchatas & aguas frescas

A tropical Agua Fresca with mango and passion fruit. Sweet, tangy, and wonderfully summery.

Timing

  • Prep time: 10 minutes
  • Chill time: 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe mangoes, peeled and diced
  • 200 ml passion fruit juice or passion fruit puree
  • juice of 1 lime
  • 500 ml cold water
  • 6–8 ice cubes
  • optional: 1 tbsp agave syrup or honey
  • optional: mint for serving

Instructions

  1. Peel and dice the mango.
  2. Add mango, passion fruit juice or passion fruit puree, lime juice, water, ice cubes, and optional sweetness to the mixing bowl.
  3. Blend 30 Sek. / Stufe 8–10.
  4. Taste and add more lime or sweetness if needed.
  5. Chill for 30 minutes or serve immediately over ice.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

Add mango, passion fruit juice or passion fruit puree, lime juice, water, ice cubes, and optional sweetness to the mixing bowl.
