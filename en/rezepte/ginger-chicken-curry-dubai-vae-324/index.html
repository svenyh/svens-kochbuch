Ginger Chicken Curry – Dubai/UAE

4 people · Culinary World Tour / World Tour

Spicy ginger chicken curry with plenty of fresh ginger – Sven's Middle Eastern inspired version for everyday cooking. Excellent with naan or pide.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 35 minutes
  • Total: approx. 50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 700 g chicken
  • 2 onions
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • plenty of fresh ginger
  • 400 g tomatoes
  • 200 ml coconut milk or yoghurt
  • garam masala
  • cumin
  • turmeric
  • chilli
  • coriander

Instructions

  1. Sear the chicken.
  2. Sauté the onions, garlic and ginger.
  3. Toast the spices and add the tomatoes.
  4. Cook the chicken in the sauce.
  5. Finish with coconut milk or yoghurt.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with naan or pide.
