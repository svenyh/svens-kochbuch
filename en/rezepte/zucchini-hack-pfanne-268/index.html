Quick cuisine

Zucchini mince pan

For 3 people · Quick cuisine / stir-fry dishes

Tomato pan with minced meat and zucchini. Cooked quickly and versatile with rice, pasta or bread.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 12 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 18 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500g minced meat
  • 2 zucchini
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 can chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Paprika powder
  • oregano

Instructions

  1. Finely chop the onion and garlic.
  2. Cut zucchini into small cubes.
  3. Heat the oil and fry the minced meat vigorously.
  4. Add onion and garlic.
  5. Add the zucchini to the pan and fry briefly.
  6. Pour in chopped tomatoes.
  7. Season with salt, pepper, paprika powder and oregano.
  8. Let it simmer briefly and serve.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with rice, pasta or bread.
