Bratwurst wok with vegetables

4 people · Fusion cuisine

Bratwurst wok with vegetables is an unusual but surprisingly everyday mixture. Hearty, quick and with enough originality to stand out from classic pan-fried dishes.

Ingredients

  • 4 coarse bratwursts
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 yellow pepper
  • 1 zucchini
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp sweet chili sauce
  • 100 ml broth
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1 spring onion
  • Sesame seeds, optional
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut the sausages into slices. Cut the peppers, zucchini and onion into coarse pieces.
  2. Heat oil in a large pan or wok. Fry the sausage slices vigorously all over.
  3. Add the onion, bell pepper and zucchini and fry over high heat for 5 to 6 minutes.
  4. Mix the soy sauce, sweet chili sauce, broth and paprika powder and pour in.
  5. Let everything glaze for 2 to 3 minutes until the sauce sticks slightly.
  6. Serve with spring onions and optionally some sesame seeds.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with rice, potato wedges or simply crusty bread.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Serve with spring onions and optionally some sesame seeds.
