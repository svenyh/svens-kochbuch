Tarte Tatin – France

6 people · Culinary World Tour / Europe

Upside-down apple tart with glossy caramel – Sven's version of a French classic. Sweet, buttery and especially balanced with tart apples.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 25–30 minutes
  • Total: approx. 50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 6 apples
  • 100 g sugar
  • 60 g butter
  • 1 roll puff pastry
  • pinch of salt
  • optional vanilla
  • crème fraîche for serving

Instructions

  1. Caramelise the sugar and stir in the butter.
  2. Arrange apple wedges in the pan and cook briefly.
  3. Cover with puff pastry and tuck in the edges.
  4. Bake at 200 °C for about 25–30 minutes.
  5. Rest briefly and turn out.
