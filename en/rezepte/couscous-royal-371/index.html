Couscous royal

6 people · Holiday dishes

A grand Moroccan couscous with chicken, merguez and vegetables – a festive braise ideal for guests and larger gatherings.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 45 minutes
  • Total: approx. 60 minutes

Ingredients

  • 400 g couscous
  • 500 g chicken
  • 300 g merguez
  • 3 carrots
  • 2 courgettes
  • 1 can chickpeas
  • 1 litre stock
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Sear the meat.
  2. Add the vegetables.
  3. Pour in the stock.
  4. Braise for 45 minutes.
  5. Prepare the couscous according to package instructions.
  6. Serve everything together.
