Strawberry Watermelon Horchata with Rice Milk

approx. 6 glasses · Summer horchatas & aguas frescas

A quick, European-inspired Thermomix version of a Horchata: creamy from rice milk, fruity from strawberries and watermelon, fresh from lime. Perfect for hot summer days, barbecues, or the terrace.

Timing

  • Prep time: 10 minutes
  • Chill time: 1 hour

Ingredients

  • 500 g strawberries
  • 600 g watermelon, rind removed and as seedless as possible
  • 500 ml rice milk
  • 80–120 ml sweetened condensed milk
  • juice of 1 lime
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 pinch cinnamon
  • 6–8 ice cubes
  • optional: fresh mint for serving
  • optional: strawberry slices and small watermelon cubes for garnish

Instructions

  1. Wash the strawberries and remove the greens. Dice the watermelon.
  2. Add strawberries and watermelon to the mixing bowl and blend 20 Sek. / Stufe 8.
  3. Add rice milk, condensed milk, lime juice, vanilla, cinnamon, and ice cubes.
  4. Blend until creamy 30 Sek. / Stufe 8–10.
  5. Taste: if the watermelon is very sweet, 80 ml condensed milk is enough. For more sweetness, use up to 120 ml.
  6. Chill for at least 1 hour.
  7. Serve with ice, fresh strawberries, watermelon cubes, and optional mint.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

Serve with ice, fresh strawberries, watermelon cubes, and optional mint.
