Irish beef rolled roast from the oven

4 people · Oven dishes

A classic oven roast doesn't have to be complicated if the meat, temperature and cooking time are right. That's exactly what makes this rolled roast so strong: honest taste, juicy results and a dish that you really look forward to.

Timing

  • Preparation: 15 minutes
  • Sauté: 10 minutes
  • Oven time: approx. 2 to 2.5 hours
  • Rest time: 10 to 15 minutes
  • Total time: approximately 2 hours 35 minutes to 3 hours 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg Irish beef rolled roast
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 2 tsp medium hot mustard (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder (optional)
  • 2 onions
  • 2 carrots
  • 500 ml beef broth

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 160 °C top/bottom heat or 140 °C fan oven.
  2. Pat the rolled roast dry and rub with salt, pepper and optionally mustard and paprika powder.
  3. Heat the oil in a roasting pan and fry the roast vigorously all over.
  4. Roughly chop the onions and carrots and add them to the roasting pan.
  5. Pour in beef broth and close the roasting pan with the lid.
  6. Cook the roast in the oven until the desired core temperature is reached.
  7. For a juicy result, take the roast out of the oven at a core temperature of 60 to 65 °C.
  8. Allow to rest for 10 to 15 minutes before slicing.
