Chicken and spinach pasta

4 people · Italian inspired cuisine/pasta

Chicken and spinach pasta brings together creamy sauce, some freshness and a pleasant satiety. The recipe appears modern, but remains clearly anchored in an easy-to-understand everyday kitchen.

Ingredients

  • 350g penne or fusilli
  • 500 g chicken breast fillet
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 250 g leaf spinach, fresh or frozen, thawed
  • 200 ml cream
  • 100 ml broth
  • 40g Parmesan
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • nutmeg

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta in salted water until al dente.
  2. Cut the chicken into strips and fry in olive oil until golden brown. Take out.
  3. Finely dice the onion and garlic and sauté in the same pan.
  4. Add spinach and let it wilt or, if using frozen spinach, heat well.
  5. Pour in the cream and broth, stir in the Parmesan and season with salt, pepper and a little nutmeg.
  6. Put the chicken back in the sauce and let it simmer gently for 3 minutes.
  7. Drain the pasta and mix with the sauce.

Frequently asked questions

Can I freeze this?

Fresh spinach brings more lightness, frozen spinach makes the dish less complicated.
