Pimientos de Padrón

4 people · Side dishes / Tapas

Small green peppers from Galicia, quickly fried in hot oil – a Spanish tapas classic with a surprising twist.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 5 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 5–7 minutes
  • Total: approx. 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g Padrón peppers
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • sea salt

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a pan until very hot.
  2. Fry the peppers for 5–7 minutes.
  3. Until they blister.
  4. Sprinkle with coarse sea salt.
  5. Serve immediately.
