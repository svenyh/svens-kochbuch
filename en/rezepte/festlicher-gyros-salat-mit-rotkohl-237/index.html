Festive gyros salad with red cabbage

6 people · Salads & side dishes / classics & home cooking

A more festive version of gyros salad with red cabbage, corn and tomatoes. Visually strong and very good for buffets or guests.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Roasting time: approx. 10 minutes
  • Brewing time optional: approx. 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken or pork slices
  • 2 tbsp gyro seasoning
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1/2 head iceberg lettuce
  • 200 g red cabbage from a jar or finely chopped fresh red cabbage
  • 1 can of corn
  • 1 red onion
  • 150g grated cheese
  • 200g tzatziki
  • 150 g cocktail tomatoes

Instructions

  1. Marinate the meat with gyros seasoning and oil and fry vigorously in the pan.
  2. Allow to cool.
  3. Prepare iceberg lettuce, red cabbage, corn, tomatoes and onion.
  4. Put everything in layers in a large mold.
  5. Spread the meat over it, sprinkle with cheese and serve with tzatziki or spread thinly on top.
