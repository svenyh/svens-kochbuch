Shish kebab pan for indoor use

4 people · Stir-fried dishes / Fast cuisine / Eastern Europe

This shish kebab pan brings the typical taste indoors without really missing the skewers or grill. It's quick to make, spicy and just right for a hearty evening meal.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Roasting and stewing: approx. 15-20 minutes
  • Let it rest briefly: 2 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30-35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 800g pork or chicken
  • 2 peppers
  • 2 onions
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 150 ml paprika sauce
  • optional: some garlic

Instructions

  1. Cut the meat into strips or cubes and season.
  2. Roughly chop the peppers and onions.
  3. Heat the oil in a large pan and fry the meat in batches.
  4. Add peppers and onions and fry.
  5. Pour in the paprika sauce and let everything simmer gently for a few minutes.
  6. Season with salt, pepper and, if necessary, garlic.
  7. Serve directly with bread, rice or potato side dish.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

It is better to fry the meat in batches so that it gets color instead of cooking juice.
