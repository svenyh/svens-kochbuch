Jägerschnitzel with tomato sauce and spirelli

4 people · Classic recipes / classics & home cooking

The Jägerschnitzel is not the West German mushroom schnitzel, but a breaded hunting sausage classic with tomato sauce and noodles. It is precisely this combination that makes the dish so distinctive and immediately recognizable.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Tomato sauce: about 20 minutes
  • Pasta: approx. 8-10 minutes
  • Bake the Jägerschnitzel: approx. 10-12 minutes
  • Total: approx. 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 8 slices of hunting sausage, each approx. 1 cm thick
  • 2 eggs
  • 4 tbsp flour
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Oil for frying
  • 400g spirelli
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 500 ml pureed tomatoes
  • 150 ml vegetable broth
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. For the sauce, finely dice the onion and sauté in butter until translucent.
  2. Stir in tomato paste and roast briefly, then pour in the pureed tomatoes and stock.
  3. Season the sauce with sugar, paprika powder, salt and pepper and simmer gently for 15 to 20 minutes until it is round and slightly creamy.
  4. In the meantime, cook the spirelli in plenty of salted water until al dente and drain.
  5. Pat the hunting sausage slices lightly dry, first in flour, then in beaten egg and then in breadcrumbs.
  6. Heat enough oil in a pan and fry the breaded hunting sausage slices on both sides until golden brown. 2 to 3 minutes per side on medium to medium-high heat is usually enough.
  7. Place the schnitzels briefly on kitchen paper.
  8. Serve the Spirelli with tomato sauce and serve the Jägerschnitzel fresh on the side.
