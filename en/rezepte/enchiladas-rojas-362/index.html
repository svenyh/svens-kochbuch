Enchiladas rojas

4 people · Oven dishes

Rolled tortillas with chicken, baked in red tomato sauce and cheese – a Mexican oven classic for the whole family.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 25 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 25 minutes
  • Total: approx. 50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 8 tortillas
  • 500 g chicken
  • 300 g cheese
  • 400 g tomato sauce

Instructions

  1. Fill the tortillas.
  2. Roll up.
  3. Pour over the sauce.
  4. Top with cheese.
  5. Bake for 25 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with sour cream and salad.
