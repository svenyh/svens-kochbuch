Shrimp in creamy paprika cream sauce

2-3 people · Fish & Seafood

Shrimp in creamy paprika cream sauce combines fine seafood with a very accessible, well-rounded sauce. The dish seems a little more special, but remains light enough to work well in everyday life.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Pan: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 400g prawns, raw and peeled
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 50 ml white wine
  • 200 ml cream
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: some lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Dice the peppers into small pieces and finely chop the onion and garlic.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté onion, garlic and peppers for 4 to 5 minutes.
  3. Deglaze with white wine and let it boil down briefly.
  4. Add the cream and paprika powder and simmer the sauce until slightly creamy.
  5. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Add the shrimp to the sauce and let it simmer for 4 to 5 minutes, depending on their size.
  7. If desired, top off with a few drops of lemon juice and serve.
