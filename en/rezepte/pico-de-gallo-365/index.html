Pico de gallo

4 people · Side dishes

Fresh Mexican salsa of tomatoes, onion, lime and coriander – the perfect dip and side for tacos and tortilla chips.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Resting: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 5 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 1 lime
  • coriander
  • salt

Instructions

  1. Dice everything finely.
  2. Mix together.
  3. Rest for 15 minutes.
