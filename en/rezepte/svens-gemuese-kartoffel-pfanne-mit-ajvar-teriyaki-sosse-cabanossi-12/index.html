Sven's vegetable and potato pan with ajvar teriyaki sauce & cabanossi

3-4 people · Stir-fries/meat dishes

This vegetable and potato stir-fry with ajvar teriyaki sauce and cabanossi brings together familiar ingredients with a little modern twist. Hearty, spicy and suitable for everyday use – just right when you want familiar cuisine to appear a little more exciting.

Timing

  • Prep: 15-20 minutes
  • Pan time: 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 800g potatoes
  • 2 peppers
  • 2 onions
  • 250-300g Cabanossi
  • 2-3 tbsp ajvar
  • 2 tbsp teriyaki sauce
  • 100 ml cream or cooking cream
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Paprika powder

Instructions

  1. Pre-boil the potatoes and cut them into slices or cubes.
  2. Fry peppers and onions.
  3. Add sliced ​​cabanossi.
  4. Add the potatoes to the pan.
  5. Mix together ajvar, teriyaki and cream.
  6. Pour sauce into the pan.
  7. Let everything boil down briefly until it becomes creamy.
  8. Season to taste and serve.
