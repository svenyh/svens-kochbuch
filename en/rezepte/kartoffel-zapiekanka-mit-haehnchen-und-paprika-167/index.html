Potato zapiekanka with chicken and peppers

4 people · Polish regional cuisine / oven & casseroles

This zapiekanka is based on the same potato idea, but with chicken and peppers it takes a more everyday and family-friendly approach to the main course. Creamy sauce, pre-cooked potatoes and golden cheese make this a very well-rounded casserole.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 25 minutes
  • Pre-cook potatoes: approx. 20 minutes
  • Pan preparation: approx. 12 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 22-28 minutes at 200 °C top/bottom heat
  • Resting time: approx. 5 minutes
  • Total: approximately 1 hour 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg waxy potatoes
  • 500 g boneless chicken breast or chicken thighs
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 yellow pepper
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 200 ml cream
  • 100 ml milk
  • 150 g grated cheese, e.g. B. Mozzarella or Gouda
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • some butter or oil for the mold

Instructions

  1. Boil the potatoes in their skins just until cooked through, drain, let cool slightly, peel and cut into slices.
  2. Preheat the oven to 200 °C top/bottom heat and grease a baking dish.
  3. Cut chicken into small pieces. Dice peppers and onions.
  4. Heat the oil in a pan, fry the chicken all over and then add the onion and pepper.
  5. Fry everything for another 5 to 6 minutes, then season with paprika powder, salt and pepper.
  6. Pour in the cream and milk and heat gently for 2 to 3 minutes so that everything combines.
  7. Layer the potato slices in the dish, pour the chicken and pepper mixture over them and sprinkle with cheese.
  8. Bake in the oven for 22 to 28 minutes, until the cheese is golden and everything is hot through.
  9. Let rest for 5 minutes before serving.
