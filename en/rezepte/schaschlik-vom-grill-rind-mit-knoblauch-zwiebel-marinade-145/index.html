Grilled shish kebab - beef with garlic-onion marinade

4 people · Grill / BBQ / Eastern Europe

Beef on the shish kebab skewer may taste a little more distinctive. Garlic and onion lead the way here, but what's most important is that the meat is chosen appropriately and doesn't stay too hot on the grill for too long.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 25 minutes
  • Marinate: at least 2 hours
  • Grilling time: approx. 12-16 minutes over medium direct to indirect heat
  • Rest time: 3 minutes
  • Total without marinade: approx. 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 kg of beef from rump or roast beef
  • 3 onions
  • 2 peppers
  • 4 tbsp oil
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1 teaspoon mustard, optional
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce, optional

Instructions

  1. Cut beef into even cubes and cleanly remove tendons.
  2. Finely chop the garlic and mix with oil, paprika powder, salt, pepper and optionally mustard and Worcestershire sauce.
  3. Marinate the meat in it for at least 2 hours.
  4. Cut the onions and peppers appropriately and put them on skewers with the meat.
  5. Preheat the grill well and grill the skewers over medium heat.
  6. Then, depending on the piece of meat, cook more slowly and turn regularly.
  7. Do not grill for an unnecessarily long time so that the beef remains juicy.
  8. Serve with bread, herb butter or paprika vegetables.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with bread, herb butter or paprika vegetables.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Finely chop the garlic and mix with oil, paprika powder, salt, pepper and optionally mustard and Worcestershire sauce.
