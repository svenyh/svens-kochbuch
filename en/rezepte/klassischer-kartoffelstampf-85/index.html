Classic mashed potatoes

3-4 people · Side dishes / potatoes

A good mashed potato is much more than just a side dish. When it's nice and light, warm and creamy, it turns many dishes into a truly well-rounded meal.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 kg floury potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 120-150 ml warm milk
  • Salt
  • freshly grated nutmeg to taste

Instructions

  1. Peel the potatoes, cut them into pieces and boil them in salted water until soft.
  2. Drain the potatoes and let them evaporate briefly.
  3. Add butter and mash the potatoes roughly.
  4. Gradually incorporate the warm milk until the desired consistency is reached.
  5. Season with salt and a little nutmeg and serve immediately.
