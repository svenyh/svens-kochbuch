Roast pork with dumplings

4 people · Holiday dishes

Juicy shoulder roast pork, slowly braised in the oven – the Austrian Sunday classic, perfect with dumplings and coleslaw.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 30 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 150 minutes
  • Total: approx. 3 hours

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg pork shoulder
  • 2 onions
  • 500 ml broth
  • caraway
  • garlic
  • salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Season the meat.
  2. Sear on all sides.
  3. Add broth and transfer to the oven.
  4. Braise for 2.5 hours.
  5. Serve with dumplings.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with dumplings.
