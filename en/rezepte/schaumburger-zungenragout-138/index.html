Schaumburg tongue ragout

4 people · Wölpinghausen specialties / classics

Tongue ragout is not a loud recipe, but that is exactly where its appeal lies. When it is cooked properly and served with a well-rounded sauce, it appears classic, special and much more delicate than many people first expect.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cooking and brewing time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Total: approx. 40 minutes
  • Can be combined well with a potato side dish as a Sunday dish

Ingredients

  • 800 g cooked beef tongue or veal tongue
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 small piece of celery
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 200 ml cooking broth or beef stock
  • 100 ml cream
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp capers, optional
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: some Worcestershire sauce or nutmeg

Instructions

  1. Cut the tongue into bite-sized slices or cubes and remove any remaining skin if necessary.
  2. Finely dice the onion, carrot and celery and slowly sauté in butter.
  3. Sprinkle flour over it and sauté briefly without letting it get dark.
  4. Deglaze with broth or stock and bring to the boil while stirring to form a smooth sauce.
  5. Stir in the cream and let the sauce simmer gently for a few minutes.
  6. Add tongue and optional capers and let everything simmer over low heat.
  7. Season with lemon juice, salt, pepper and, if necessary, a little Worcestershire sauce.
  8. Serve with potatoes, rice or buttered spaetzle.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with potatoes, rice or buttered spaetzle.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Add tongue and optional capers and let everything simmer over low heat.
