Meatballs in soy-ginger sauce

4 people · Fusion cuisine

Meatballs in soy-ginger sauce bring a spicy fusion touch, but remain clearly understandable and down-to-earth. Just right when you want familiar ingredients to have a slightly different effect.

Ingredients

  • 500 g minced beef
  • 1 egg
  • 40 g breadcrumbs
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 150 ml broth
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp water
  • 1 spring onion
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Mix the minced meat with egg, breadcrumbs, finely diced onion, garlic and a little pepper. Form small balls.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan and fry the meatballs vigorously all over.
  3. Sauté the ginger briefly in the pan. Add soy sauce, broth and honey.
  4. Let the balls simmer gently in the sauce for 8 minutes.
  5. Mix cornstarch with water, stir into the sauce and let it thicken briefly.
  6. Serve with finely chopped spring onions.
