Grill & BBQ

Baked mushrooms from the grill or oven

4 people · Grill & BBQ / Salads & Side Dishes

Large mushrooms with creamy filling and cheese - ideal as a grill side dish or as a small warm snack from the oven.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 15-20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 12 large mushrooms
  • 150 g cream cheese
  • 100g grated cheese
  • 1 small red onion
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • 2 tbsp parsley
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • some oil

Instructions

  1. Clean the mushrooms and carefully twist out the stems.
  2. Chop the stems finely. Also chop the onion and garlic finely.
  3. Mix everything with cream cheese, parsley, spices and some grated cheese.
  4. Fill the mushroom caps with the mixture and sprinkle with the remaining cheese.
  5. Cook indirectly on the grill or bake in the oven until the mushrooms are soft and golden brown.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

Also very tasty with feta instead of cream cheese.
