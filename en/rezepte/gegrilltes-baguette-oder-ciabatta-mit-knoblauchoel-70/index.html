Grilled baguette or ciabatta with garlic oil

3-4 people · Grill & BBQ / Side Dishes

Grilled garlic bread is often the first thing to go when it's done well. A little roasted aroma, good oil and fresh garlic are enough to turn simple bread into a very strong side dish.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 5 minutes
  • Grill time: 2-4 minutes over medium direct heat
  • Total: approx. 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 baguette or 1 ciabatta
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • 1 teaspoon chopped parsley or herbs
  • 1 pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Cut the baguette or ciabatta.
  2. Mix olive oil with finely chopped garlic and spread on the bread.
  3. Preheat the grill to medium direct heat.
  4. Grill the bread slices for 1 to 2 minutes per side, until golden brown and crispy.
  5. Serve with herbs, sea salt or a little butter if desired.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Use garlic oil sparingly to keep the bread crispy.
