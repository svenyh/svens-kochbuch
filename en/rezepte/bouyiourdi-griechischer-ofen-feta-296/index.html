Bouyiourdi – Greek Baked Feta

2-3 people · Oven dishes/side dishes

Bouyiourdi is a classic Greek oven feta: the cheese is baked in the oven with tomatoes, peppers, onions, olive oil and a little chili - spicy, creamy and ideal for dipping with bread or as a warm side dish. Simply put it in a small dish and bake it briefly - that's all you need.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 400g feta in one block
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 pepper
  • 1 small red onion
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder
  • Chili flakes to taste
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: 1 tbsp capers
  • Bread for serving

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C top/bottom heat.
  2. Dice tomatoes and peppers, cut onion into fine strips.
  3. Place the feta in a small oven dish or divide it into large pieces.
  4. Distribute the vegetables around the feta.
  5. Drizzle with olive oil and season with oregano, paprika, chili, salt and pepper.
  6. Bake for 20 minutes until the feta is lightly browned on the outside and soft on the inside.
  7. Serve hot with fresh bread.
