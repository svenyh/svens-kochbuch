Chorizo in red wine

4 people · Tapas / Pan dishes

A Spanish tapas classic. Spicy chorizo braises in robust red wine and develops an intense aroma that pairs perfectly with fresh bread.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 400 g chorizo
  • 250 ml dry red wine
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Instructions

  1. Slice the chorizo.
  2. Finely dice the onion.
  3. Heat the oil and sauté the onion.
  4. Briefly fry the chorizo.
  5. Deglaze with red wine.
  6. Add the bay leaf.
  7. Simmer gently for 15–20 minutes.
  8. Serve with baguette.
