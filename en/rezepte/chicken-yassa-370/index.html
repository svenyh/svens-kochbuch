Chicken yassa

4 people · Poultry dishes

Senegalese chicken in a lemon-mustard marinade – tangy, spiced and one of West Africa's best-known dishes.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 40 minutes
  • Total: approx. 55 minutes

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken thighs
  • 4 large onions
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tbsp mustard
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Whisk lemon juice, mustard and seasonings.
  2. Marinate the chicken.
  3. Sear in a pan.
  4. Add the onions.
  5. Braise for 40 minutes.
  6. Serve with rice.
