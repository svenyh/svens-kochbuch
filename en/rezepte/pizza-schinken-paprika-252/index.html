Pizza & Bread

Pizza ham and peppers

For 2 people · Pizza & Bread / Oven & Casseroles

A mild, family-friendly pizza with ham, peppers and onions. Simply filled and very suitable for everyday use.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 12 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 10-12 minutes at 250°C

Ingredients

  • 1 portion of classic pizza dough
  • 200 g pizza sauce
  • 150g grated cheese
  • 100 g cooked ham
  • 1/2 red pepper
  • 1/2 yellow pepper
  • 1 small red onion
  • oregano
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Roll out and prepare the pizza dough.
  2. Spread the sauce on top.
  3. Pour the cheese on top.
  4. Cut ham into pieces.
  5. Cut the pepper into fine strips and the onion into thin rings.
  6. Distribute everything evenly on the pizza.
  7. Season with a little oregano.
  8. Bake in a hot oven until golden brown.
