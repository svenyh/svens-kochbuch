Gazpacho Andaluz

4 people · Soups

The cold tomato soup from Andalusia is unbeatable in summer. Sven's version with ripe tomatoes, cucumber and pepper – puréed, chilled and ready.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Chilling time: at least 2 hours
  • Total: approx. 2 hours 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 800 g ripe tomatoes
  • 1 cucumber
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Roughly chop the vegetables.
  2. Purée until smooth.
  3. Stir in the oil and vinegar.
  4. Chill for at least 2 hours.
  5. Serve well chilled.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with croutons.
