French onion soup

4 people · Soups

Slowly caramelised onions in rich beef stock – the Parisian icon, topped with cheese and baguette at Sven's table. Warming, deep in flavour and ideal for cold days.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 45 minutes
  • Total: approx. 60 minutes

Ingredients

  • 800 g onions
  • 50 g butter
  • 1 litre beef stock
  • 150 ml white wine
  • baguette
  • cheese
  • salt
  • pepper
  • thyme

Instructions

  1. Slice the onions into fine strips.
  2. Caramelise slowly in butter.
  3. Deglaze with wine.
  4. Add stock and simmer for 30 minutes.
  5. Top with cheese bread and grill until golden.
