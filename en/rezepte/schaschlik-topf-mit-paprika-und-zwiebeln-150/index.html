Shish kebab pot with peppers and onions

4 people · Stews / Winter cuisine / Eastern Europe

The shashlik pot is the more comfortable version of the grill classic. Everything ends up together in the pot, slowly steeps and develops exactly that round, warm pepper-onion note that goes particularly well on cool days.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Braising time: approx. 35-45 minutes
  • Total: approx. 60 minutes
  • Very good for autumn and winter

Ingredients

  • 1kg pork
  • 3 peppers
  • 3 onions
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp paprika powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 250 ml broth
  • 200 ml paprika sauce or chopped tomatoes
  • optional: 1 tsp marjoram

Instructions

  1. Dice the meat and fry it vigorously in oil in a large pot or roasting pan.
  2. Add onions and peppers and fry for a few minutes.
  3. Season with paprika powder and toss briefly.
  4. Pour in the broth and paprika sauce and bring everything to the boil.
  5. Cover and simmer gently over a low heat for 35 to 45 minutes.
  6. Finally, cook uncovered until the consistency is right.
  7. Season with salt, pepper and optionally marjoram and serve hot.
