Closed bacon cheeseburger

4 people · Oven dishes / party food / finger food

The hearty version with crispy bacon, spicy ground beef and melted cheddar. Perfect as a hearty oven dish or party food.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 20-25 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 rolls of fresh pizza dough
  • 500 g minced beef
  • 150g bacon
  • 1 large onion
  • 150g cheddar
  • 2 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1 tbsp milk
  • 1-2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • some oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Cut the bacon into small pieces and fry in a pan until crispy.
  2. Remove the bacon and fry the onion in the bacon fat or a little oil.
  3. Add the minced meat and fry vigorously.
  4. Season with salt, pepper and paprika powder.
  5. Stir in ketchup and mustard, add bacon again.
  6. Let the filling cool slightly.
  7. Cut pizza dough into 8 pieces.
  8. Fill with minced mixture and cheddar.
  9. Seal well and place on the tray with the seam side down.
  10. Brush with egg yolk and milk mixture and sprinkle sesame seeds on top.
  11. Bake at 190 °C top/bottom heat for approx. 20-25 minutes.
