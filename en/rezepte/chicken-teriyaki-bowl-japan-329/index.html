Chicken Teriyaki Bowl – Japan

4 people · Culinary World Tour / World Tour

Quick chicken teriyaki bowl with rice and vegetables – Sven's weeknight version, meal-prep friendly and clear in flavour.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Total: approx. 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 700 g chicken
  • rice
  • broccoli
  • carrots
  • spring onions
  • sesame
  • soy sauce
  • honey
  • mirin or rice vinegar
  • garlic
  • ginger

Instructions

  1. Cook the rice.
  2. Cook the vegetables.
  3. Sear the chicken.
  4. Reduce a sauce from soy sauce, honey, mirin/rice vinegar, garlic and ginger.
  5. Glaze the chicken and serve as a bowl.
