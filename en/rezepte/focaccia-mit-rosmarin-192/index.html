Focaccia with rosemary

1 sheet · Italian inspired cuisine / bread & side dishes

Focaccia with rosemary is a classic bread with a crispy edge, a soft center and lots of fragrance. It goes well as a side dish, for sharing or simply as an uncomplicated treat straight out of the oven.

Ingredients

  • 500 g wheat flour type 550
  • 7 g dry yeast
  • 320 ml lukewarm water
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 5 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 sprigs of rosemary
  • coarse sea salt

Instructions

  1. Mix flour, dry yeast, sugar and 2 teaspoons salt in a bowl. Add water and 3 tablespoons olive oil.
  2. Knead everything into a soft dough and work for 8 to 10 minutes until the dough is elastic.
  3. Cover and let rise in a warm place for 60 to 75 minutes.
  4. Grease a tray or mold with olive oil. Add the dough and gently press it into shape.
  5. Let it rest for another 20 minutes.
  6. Press typical depressions into the dough with your fingers. Brush with remaining olive oil, sprinkle rosemary needles and a little sea salt on top.
  7. Bake at 220 °C top/bottom heat for 20 to 25 minutes until golden brown.
  8. Let cool slightly and cut into pieces.
