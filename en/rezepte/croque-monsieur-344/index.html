Croque Monsieur

2–3 people · Quick weeknight cooking / Oven dishes

The croque monsieur is the quintessential French bistro snack. Crisp toast, ham and melted cheese make a simple but incredibly good dish.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 8 slices sandwich bread
  • 4 slices cooked ham
  • 200 g Gruyère or Emmental
  • 40 g butter
  • 20 g flour
  • 250 ml milk
  • salt
  • pepper
  • nutmeg

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter.
  2. Stir in the flour.
  3. Add the milk and make a béchamel.
  4. Season with salt, pepper and nutmeg.
  5. Top the toast with ham and cheese.
  6. Fold together.
  7. Spread with béchamel.
  8. Sprinkle with cheese.
  9. Gratin for about 15 minutes at 200 °C.

What side dish goes well?
