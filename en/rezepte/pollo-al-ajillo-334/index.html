Pollo al Ajillo

4 people · Pan dishes / Poultry

Chicken in golden garlic oil with white wine – a Spanish pan classic. Hearty, aromatic and perfect with bread for dipping.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Braising time: approx. 35 minutes
  • Total: approx. 50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken thighs
  • 8 garlic cloves
  • 150 ml white wine
  • olive oil
  • salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut the chicken into pieces, season and sear well in olive oil.
  2. Add garlic slices and fry until golden.
  3. Deglaze with white wine.
  4. Braise over medium heat for approx. 35 minutes, turning occasionally.
  5. Serve with bread.
