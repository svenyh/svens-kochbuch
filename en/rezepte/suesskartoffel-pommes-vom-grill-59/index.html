Grilled sweet potato fries

2-3 people · Grill & BBQ / Side Dishes

Grilled sweet potato fries are a nice alternative to classic potato side dishes when you want something a little smokier and more summery. Lightly roasted on the outside and soft on the inside, they bring variety to the grill plate.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Grilling: approx. 18-25 minutes over medium indirect heat
  • Finish: 2-3 minutes on higher heat
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 large sweet potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder optional
  • Sea salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut the sweet potatoes into even sticks and pat dry.
  2. Mix with oil, salt, paprika powder and, if desired, a little starch so that they stick better on the outside.
  3. Prepare the grill for medium indirect heat and preheat a plancha, grill tray or griddle.
  4. Spread the sweet potato sticks on top and cook for 18 to 25 minutes, turning several times.
  5. Finally, roast over a slightly higher heat for 2 to 3 minutes to create more color.
  6. Serve immediately with dip or herb quark.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

Sweet potato sticks can be cut 2–3 hours before and kept dry – mix with oil and spices when grilling starts.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Smoked paprika works instead of regular paprika for a stronger BBQ note.
