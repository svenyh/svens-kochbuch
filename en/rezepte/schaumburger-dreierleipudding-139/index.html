Schaumburger Dreierlei pudding

6 people · Wölpinghausen specialties / dessert

Dreierlei pudding thrives on the fact that several simple components come together to create something special. That's exactly what makes it so suitable for traditional recipe pages: down-to-earth, sweet, a little nostalgic and still really nice on the table.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 10 minutes
  • Cooling time: at least 2 hours
  • It's best to prepare a few hours in advance or the day before

Ingredients

  • 500 ml milk
  • 1 packet of vanilla pudding powder
  • 1 packet of chocolate pudding powder
  • 2-3 tbsp sugar
  • 200 ml cream
  • 1 tbsp sugar for the cream
  • optional: some cinnamon
  • optional: red jelly or fruit compote for serving

Instructions

  1. Cook the vanilla and chocolate pudding with milk and sugar according to the package instructions.
  2. Prepare a mold or several glasses and first pour in a layer of vanilla pudding.
  3. Then add the chocolate pudding on top and let both layers cool slightly.
  4. Beat the cream with the sugar until stiff and place on top of the pudding as a third layer or as a topping.
  5. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours so that the layers set properly.
  6. Before serving, add a little cinnamon or fruit compote if desired.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

It's best to prepare a few hours in advance or the day before.
