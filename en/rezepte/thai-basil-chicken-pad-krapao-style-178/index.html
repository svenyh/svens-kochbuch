Thai Basil Chicken (Pad Krapao Style)

4 people · Asian wok / wok dishes

Thai Basil Chicken in Pad Krapao style is a quick, intense wok dish with minced meat, chili, garlic and basil. It thrives on high heat, clear seasoning and a slightly salty-spicy sauce with a clear depth.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Wok preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Total: approx. 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600 g minced chicken or very finely chopped chicken breast
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 onion
  • 4 cloves of garlic
  • 2 red chilies
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 bunch of Thai basil or basil
  • 1 lime
  • For the sauce: 4 tablespoons soy sauce
  • For the sauce: 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • For the sauce: 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • For the sauce: 1 teaspoon brown sugar
  • For the sauce: 3 tablespoons water

Instructions

  1. Dice the peppers and onions small. Finely chop the garlic and chili.
  2. Mix the sauce from soy sauce, fish sauce, oyster sauce, sugar and water.
  3. Heat the wok to high heat. Add oil and briefly fry the garlic and chili.
  4. Add the minced chicken and fry until crumbly but vigorous.
  5. Add onion and bell pepper and continue frying for 3 minutes.
  6. Pour in the sauce and let everything simmer briefly over high heat.
  7. Roughly tear the basil and fold it in briefly.
  8. Serve with a little lime juice.
