Pea soup with potatoes

4 people · Soups/home cooking

Pea soup with potatoes is a classic, filling pot for cool days. It can be rustic, but still has to appear balanced - that's exactly what happens with the potatoes, the peas and a careful cooking time.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 45 minutes
  • Total: approx. 1 hour

Ingredients

  • 300 g dried peas
  • 500g potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 liter broth
  • 1 tbsp oil or a little bacon
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • marjoram

Instructions

  1. Soak dried peas beforehand if necessary.
  2. Clean the onion, carrot and potatoes and cut them into pieces.
  3. Sauté the onion in a little oil or with a little bacon.
  4. Add peas, carrot and broth and cook until soft.
  5. Add potatoes and continue cooking until cooked through.
  6. If desired, puree a small portion to make the soup thicker.
  7. Season with salt, pepper and marjoram and serve hot.
