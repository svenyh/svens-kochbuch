Lemon vodka slush for the Ninja Slushi

2 glasses · Cocktails/Ninja Sushi

This lemon vodka slush is clear, fresh and deliberately has a more direct taste. That's exactly what makes it strong if you want something cool with a decent citrus note that doesn't look playful, but rather clean and straightforward.

Timing

  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Machine: approx. 15-30 minutes
  • Total: approx. 20-35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 120 ml lemon juice
  • 220 ml still water
  • 30 ml sugar syrup
  • 4 cl vodka

Instructions

  1. Chill the lemon juice, water, sugar syrup and vodka well.
  2. Pour all ingredients into the Ninja Slushi.
  3. Start the slush program and let the mixture turn slushy.
  4. If necessary, taste briefly and adjust the sweet-sour balance.
  5. Pour immediately into glasses and serve ice cold.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

Lemon juice, water, syrup and vodka can be prepared and chilled 1–2 hours ahead in the fridge.

How long does it keep?

Best served fresh; leftovers keep in the fridge only briefly before turning liquid again.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Optionally use more sugar syrup for sweetness or a little more lemon juice for acidity.
