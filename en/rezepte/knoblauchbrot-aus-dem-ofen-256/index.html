Garlic bread from the oven

For 4 people · Basic recipes/side dishes

Crispy bread with lots of garlic, butter and herbs. Perfect with pizza, soup, salad or from the grill.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 10-12 minutes at 200°C

Ingredients

  • 1 baguette or ciabatta
  • 80g soft butter
  • 2-3 cloves of garlic
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optionally some grated Parmesan

Instructions

  1. Mix the butter with finely chopped garlic and parsley.
  2. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Cut the baguette or cut it in half lengthways.
  4. Spread the garlic butter generously on top.
  5. Optionally sprinkle some Parmesan on top.
  6. Bake in the oven until crispy and lightly golden brown.
  7. Serve warm.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

Optionally sprinkle some Parmesan on top.
