Grilled GDR beer mustard neck steaks

4 people · Grill &amp; BBQ

Marinated pork neck steaks with mustard, beer and lots of onions. A hearty grill recipe with a strong GDR influence.

Timing

  • Marinate: at least 6 hours, preferably overnight | Preparation on barbecue day: 15 minutes | Grilling time: approx. 12-18 minutes | Total active: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4-6 pork neck steaks
  • 3-4 large onions
  • 4 tbsp medium hot mustard
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder
  • 1 tsp marjoram
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 bottle of beer, approx. 0.5 l
  • optional: 1-2 cloves of garlic
  • optional: 1 teaspoon caraway

Instructions

  1. Peel onions and cut into rings.
  2. Mix mustard, oil, paprika, marjoram, pepper and salt to form a seasoning paste.
  3. Spread the neck steaks generously with the mustard mixture.
  4. Layer the steaks with the onion rings in a bowl or baking dish.
  5. Pour beer over the top until the meat is well covered or at least well surrounded.
  6. Cover and let sit in the fridge for at least 6 hours, preferably overnight.
  7. Before grilling, remove the meat from the marinade and let it drain a little.
  8. Remove the onions from the marinade and fry them separately in a grill pan.
  9. Grill the neck steaks over medium to high heat until they are nicely browned on the outside and juicy on the inside.
  10. Serve with the braised beer onions.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Cover and let sit in the fridge for at least 6 hours, preferably overnight.

What side dish goes well?

Serve with the braised beer onions.
