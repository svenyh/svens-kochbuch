Oven cheese spaetzle

4 people · Oven dishes/side dishes

Creamy spaetzle with melted baked cheese is exactly the side dish that quickly turns a simple evening into something really hearty. Everything goes into a mold and goes into the oven for a moment - and a hearty dish is on the table that will fill you up even without meat.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 20-25 minutes
  • Total: approx. 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g fresh spaetzle
  • 250 g Camembert or oven cheese
  • 200 ml cream or sour cream
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 100 g grated Emmental or Gouda
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • nutmeg
  • optional: 1 tbsp chives

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C top/bottom heat.
  2. Finely dice the onion and sauté in butter until translucent.
  3. Mix the spaetzle, cream, grated cheese, salt, pepper and nutmeg.
  4. Pour the mixture into a greased baking dish.
  5. Cut the Camembert into pieces and place on the spaetzle.
  6. Bake for 20-25 minutes until the cheese is golden brown.
  7. Sprinkle with chives and serve hot.
