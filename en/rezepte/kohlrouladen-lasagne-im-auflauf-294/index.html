Cabbage roll lasagna in casserole

4 people · Oven dishes / fusion

This is where cabbage roll filling and lasagna idea come together: leaves instead of pasta sheets, minced meat, tomato sauce and cheese in the casserole. No classic cabbage rolls and no normal lasagna - but a hearty fusion casserole from the oven.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 25 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 40-45 minutes
  • Total: approx. 70 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 small white cabbage
  • 500 g minced meat mixed
  • 1 egg
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp breadcrumbs
  • 400 ml pureed tomatoes
  • 200 ml cream or sour cream
  • 150g grated cheese
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Paprika powder
  • 2 tbsp oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C top/bottom heat.
  2. Remove the cabbage leaves from the stalk, blanch in boiling salted water for 3-5 minutes until they are pliable, drain and allow to cool slightly.
  3. Finely dice the onion and fry in oil.
  4. Mix the minced meat with egg, breadcrumbs, onion, salt, pepper and paprika.
  5. Briefly roast the tomato paste and mix it with the pureed tomatoes and cream to make a sauce.
  6. Spread a baking dish with some sauce.
  7. Layer cabbage leaves with minced meat and sauce, like for lasagna - each layer should be well covered with sauce.
  8. Sprinkle with cheese and bake for 40-45 minutes until the top is golden brown.
