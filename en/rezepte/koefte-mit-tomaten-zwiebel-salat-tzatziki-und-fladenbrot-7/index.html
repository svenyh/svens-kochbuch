Köfte with tomato and onion salad, tzatziki and flatbread

3-4 people · Stir-fries/meat dishes

Köfte with tomato and onion salad, tzatziki and flatbread are a nice example of simple cuisine with a lot of taste. The dish looks sociable, filling and fits perfectly on an uncomplicated table with several components.

Timing

  • Preparation: 20 minutes
  • Roast: 10-12 minutes
  • Total: approx. 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 600 g minced meat
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp breadcrumbs
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 250 g yogurt
  • 1/2 cucumber
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • 1 flatbread
  • some parsley
  • oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Mix the minced meat with grated onion, egg, breadcrumbs and spices.
  2. Form small elongated kofta.
  3. Fry or grill all over in a little oil.
  4. Chop tomatoes and red onion for the salad.
  5. Dress with a little salt, pepper and parsley.
  6. For tzatziki, grate cucumber and mix with yogurt and garlic.
  7. Warm up the flatbread and serve everything together.
