Mojito

1 glass · Cocktails / summer drinks

A good mojito doesn't depend on show, but on freshness, balance and clean work in the glass. When lime, mint and rum are right, it becomes a drink that seems light but still has real character.

Timing

  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Mix: 3 minutes
  • Serve immediately with lots of ice cream

Ingredients

  • 1 lime
  • 2 tsp brown sugar
  • 8-10 mint leaves
  • 50 ml white rum
  • 100 ml carbonated mineral water
  • Ice cubes or crushed ice

Instructions

  1. Cut the lime into pieces and put it in a glass with the sugar.
  2. Press the mint leaves lightly and add them.
  3. Crush briefly with a pestle, but do not grind the mint too finely.
  4. Add rum and ice.
  5. Top up with mineral water and stir briefly.
  6. Serve immediately.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

For a non-alcoholic version, simply omit the rum.
