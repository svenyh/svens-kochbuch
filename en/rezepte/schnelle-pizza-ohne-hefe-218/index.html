Pizza

Quick pizza without yeast

2-3 people · Pizza / Fast food

A quick pizza version without yeast for days when you want things to be quick and uncomplicated.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 15-18 minutes at 220°C

Ingredients

  • 250g flour
  • 1 packet of baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 125ml water
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 150 g pureed tomatoes
  • 150g grated cheese
  • Topping as desired, e.g. E.g. salami, peppers, onions or mushrooms

Instructions

  1. Mix flour, baking powder and salt.
  2. Add water and olive oil and knead into a smooth dough.
  3. Roll out the dough directly and place it on a tray or baking paper.
  4. Spread with pureed tomatoes and top with cheese and desired toppings.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven until the edges are golden brown.
