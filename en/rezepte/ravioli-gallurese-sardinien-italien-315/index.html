Ravioli Gallurese – Sardinia/Italy

4 people · Culinary World Tour / Europe

Sardinian ravioli in tomato sauce with Pecorino – Sven's simplified version using good ready-made ravioli. Quick, Mediterranean and still authentic in character.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 30 minutes
  • Total: approx. 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g cheese-filled ravioli
  • 400 g tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • basil
  • Pecorino
  • olive oil
  • salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Sauté the onion and garlic.
  2. Add the tomatoes and simmer for 25 minutes.
  3. Cook the ravioli, toss with the sauce and serve with Pecorino.
