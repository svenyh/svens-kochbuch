Baked potatoes with herb quark

For 2-3 people · Quick kitchen / oven dishes

Simple baked potatoes with fresh herb quark. An uncomplicated dinner with little effort.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 35-40 minutes at 200°C

Ingredients

  • 800g potatoes
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Paprika powder
  • 250 g quark
  • 2 tbsp yogurt
  • 1 small clove of garlic
  • 2 tbsp herbs, e.g. E.g. chives and parsley

Instructions

  1. Wash the potatoes thoroughly and halve or quarter them.
  2. Mix with oil, salt, pepper and paprika powder.
  3. Place on a baking sheet and bake in the oven until golden brown.
  4. In the meantime, mix the quark with the yogurt.
  5. Finely chop the garlic and herbs and stir in.
  6. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. Serve the hot potatoes with herb quark.
