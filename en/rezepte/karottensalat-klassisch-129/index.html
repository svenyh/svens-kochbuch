Classic carrot salad

3-4 people · Salads/side dishes

Carrot salad is simple, inexpensive and often just the thing when you want something fresh on the table. With a mild dressing and some apple it gets a nice balance of freshness and sweetness.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Brewing time: approx. 10 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 5 carrots
  • 1 apple
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp honey
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Peel and roughly grate the carrots; only grate the apple just before mixing.
  2. Mix lemon juice, oil, honey, salt and pepper to make a dressing.
  3. Mix everything thoroughly and let it steep for 10 minutes.
  4. Season again before serving.
  5. Add parsley or nuts if desired.
