Winter shish kebab from the oven

4 people · Oven dishes / winter cuisine / Eastern Europe

Winter shish kebab from the oven is exactly the solution when it's not grilling weather outside, but you still want to bring this hearty skewer idea to the table. The oven makes everything a little calmer, juicier and very suitable for everyday use.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Marinate: optional 1-2 hours
  • Oven time: approx. 30-40 minutes at 190 °C top/bottom heat
  • Gratinate briefly: 3-5 minutes with top heat or grill function
  • Total: approx. 55-60 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1kg pork
  • 3 peppers
  • 3 onions
  • 4 tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp paprika powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 150 ml broth or paprika sauce
  • optional: some marjoram or caraway

Instructions

  1. Prepare the meat, peppers and onions and marinate the meat with oil and spices.
  2. Place everything on skewers or, alternatively, layer them tightly in an oven dish.
  3. Pour in a little broth or paprika sauce to keep the meat juicy.
  4. Cook in a preheated oven at around 190 °C for around 30 to 40 minutes.
  5. Turn once in between or cover with liquid.
  6. Finally, briefly switch on the top heat or grill function to create some color.
  7. Serve with bread, potatoes or winter vegetables.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Allow to rest for 3 to 5 minutes before serving to keep the skewers juicy.

What side dish goes well?

Serve with bread, potatoes or winter vegetables.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Marinate: optional 1-2 hours.
