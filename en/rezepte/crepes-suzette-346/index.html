Crêpes Suzette

4 people · Baking & sweets

Thin crêpes in a buttery orange-lemon sauce – a classic French dessert that impresses served warm at the table.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 250 ml milk
  • 125 g flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 30 g butter
  • For the sauce: 2 oranges
  • For the sauce: 1 lemon
  • For the sauce: 50 g sugar
  • For the sauce: 40 g butter

Instructions

  1. Make the crêpe batter.
  2. Cook thin crêpes.
  3. Reduce the orange and lemon juice.
  4. Caramelise the sugar.
  5. Work in the butter.
  6. Toss the crêpes in the sauce.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Keep the crêpes thin – they soften further in the sauce.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Optionally flambé with Grand Marnier.
