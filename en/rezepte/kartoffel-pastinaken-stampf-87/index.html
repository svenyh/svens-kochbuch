Potato and parsnip mash

3-4 people · Side dishes / potatoes

The parsnip mash is the finer, more aromatic sister of the classic potato mash. The slight sweetness of the parsnip makes it particularly suitable for hearty oven and stewed dishes.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 12 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Total: approx. 32 minutes

Ingredients

  • 700 g floury potatoes
  • 300 g parsnips
  • 50g butter
  • 120-150 ml warm milk
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • some nutmeg to taste

Instructions

  1. Peel the potatoes and parsnips and cut them into equal pieces.
  2. Cook both in salted water until soft.
  3. Drain and allow to evaporate briefly.
  4. Add butter and mash everything coarsely.
  5. Smooth with warm milk and season with salt, pepper and a little nutmeg.
  6. Serve hot.
