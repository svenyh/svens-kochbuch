Pizza rolls with ham and cheese

For 4 people · Pizza & bread / snacks

Hearty, quick-made pizza rolls with ham, cheese and peppers. Ideal for guests or as an uncomplicated snack.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 12-15 minutes at 200°C

Ingredients

  • 4 baked rolls or half rolls
  • 150 g cooked ham
  • 150g grated cheese
  • 1/2 pepper
  • 2 tbsp crème fraîche
  • 2 tbsp sour cream
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • oregano

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven.
  2. Dice the ham and peppers small.
  3. Mix with cheese, crème fraîche and sour cream.
  4. Season with salt, pepper and oregano.
  5. Cover the bun halves with it.
  6. Place on a baking sheet.
  7. Bake in the oven until the cheese and top are golden brown.
  8. Serve hot.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

Also delicious with salami instead of ham.
