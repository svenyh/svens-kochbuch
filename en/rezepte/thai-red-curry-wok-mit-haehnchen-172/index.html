Thai Red Curry Wok with Chicken

4 people · Asian wok / wok dishes

Thai Red Curry Wok with Chicken combines wok vegetables with a creamy, spicy coconut sauce. The red curry paste, lime and fresh basil ensure a clearly Thai character and a very well-rounded seasoning.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Wok and curry preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total: approx. 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast fillet
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 zucchini
  • 150 g sugar snap peas
  • 1 small eggplant
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 2 to 3 tablespoons red curry paste
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • 1 lime
  • 1 handful of Thai basil or regular basil

Instructions

  1. Cut chicken into strips. Cut the peppers, zucchini and eggplant into bite-sized pieces. Cut onion into strips.
  2. Heat the wok and add oil. Fry the curry paste for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add chicken and sauté for 2 to 3 minutes.
  4. Add onion, eggplant, peppers and zucchini and continue frying for 3 minutes.
  5. Pour in coconut milk and mix everything well.
  6. Stir in fish sauce and sugar.
  7. Add the snow peas and simmer the curry gently for 6 to 8 minutes.
  8. Season with lime juice and fold in the basil.
  9. Serve immediately.
