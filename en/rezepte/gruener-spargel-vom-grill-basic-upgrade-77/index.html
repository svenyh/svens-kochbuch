Green asparagus from the grill – Basic & Upgrade

2-3 people · Grill & BBQ / asparagus & seasonal cuisine

Green asparagus from the grill works very well on its own, but wins even further with small upgrades. That's exactly why this variant is practical: a clean base that can quickly be made a little larger or more special depending on the occasion.

Timing

  • Preparation: 10 minutes
  • Basic grilling time: 5-8 minutes over medium direct heat
  • Upgrade with bacon: approx. 8-10 minutes
  • Total: approx. 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g green asparagus
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 lemon
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: 30 g Parmesan
  • optional: 4-6 slices of bacon
  • optional: 1 small clove of garlic

Instructions

  1. Snap or cut off the woody ends of the asparagus; Green asparagus usually does not need to be peeled.
  2. Mix the asparagus with olive oil, salt, pepper and a little garlic if desired.
  3. Bring the grill to medium direct heat and place the asparagus directly on the grate or in a grill pan.
  4. Grill the asparagus for 5 to 8 minutes, turning regularly, until it has light grill marks and is still firm to the bite.
  5. For the basic version, serve immediately after grilling with just lemon juice and a little salt.
  6. For the upgrade version, grate Parmesan over it or wrap the asparagus in bacon and then grill for 2 to 3 minutes longer.
  7. Serve hot as a side dish or with bread.
