Backhendl

4 people · Meat dishes

Viennese classic: breaded chicken fried golden brown – crisp outside, juicy inside. Great with potato salad or a fresh green salad.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 25 minutes
  • Total: approx. 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg chicken pieces
  • 2 eggs
  • flour
  • breadcrumbs
  • salt
  • pepper
  • oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken.
  2. Coat in flour.
  3. Dip in egg.
  4. Coat in breadcrumbs.
  5. Fry until golden brown.
