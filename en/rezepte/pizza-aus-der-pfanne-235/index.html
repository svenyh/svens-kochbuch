Pizza

Pizza from the pan

2 people · Pizza / Fast food

A practical pizza variant for pan and lid if the oven is not available or you want it to be particularly uncomplicated.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Walking time: approx. 30-45 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 8-10 minutes per pizza

Ingredients

  • 250g flour
  • 1/2 cube fresh yeast
  • 150 ml lukewarm water
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 150 g pureed tomatoes
  • 125g mozzarella
  • Topping as desired, e.g. E.g. salami, peppers, onions or mushrooms

Instructions

  1. Dissolve yeast and sugar in lukewarm water.
  2. Add flour, salt and olive oil and knead into a smooth dough.
  3. Let the dough rise for 30-45 minutes and divide into two portions.
  4. Roll out to fit the pan.
  5. Lightly oil a non-stick pan and briefly fry the dough over medium heat.
  6. Turn the base, top with tomatoes, cheese and toppings and cook with the lid on until the cheese is melted and the base is crispy.
