Classic pasta salad with mayo

4-6 people · Salads / classics

A classic pasta salad with mayonnaise is one of the recipes that is almost always the first to be empty at parties and barbecues. It's creamy, uncomplicated and that's exactly why it's so popular.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Brewing time: approx. 30 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 400 g short noodles
  • 150g mayonnaise
  • 100g peas
  • 1 red pepper
  • 4 pickles
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta and let it cool.
  2. Finely dice peppers, cucumbers and onions.
  3. Mix mayonnaise with mustard.
  4. Mix everything and season with salt and pepper.
  5. Allow to steep well before serving.
