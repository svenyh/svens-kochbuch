Spaghetti with garlic and sambal oelek

1 person · Pasta dishes

This spaghetti relies on just a few ingredients that together bring a surprising amount of character to the plate. Garlic, oil and sambal oelek provide speed, heat and just the easy spice you want on stressful days.

Timing

  • Total time: 15-20 minutes
  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Cooking time: 10-12 minutes

Ingredients

  • 150g spaghetti
  • 4-6 cloves of garlic
  • 4-5 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 to 1 tsp Sambal Oelek
  • 2-4 tbsp pasta water
  • 20-30g Parmesan, optional
  • 1 egg yolk, optional

Instructions

  1. Cook the spaghetti in plenty of salted water until al dente and reserve some of the pasta water.
  2. Chop garlic finely.
  3. Heat the olive oil over a low heat and let the garlic simmer until translucent without browning it.
  4. Stir in sambal oelek.
  5. Add 2-4 tablespoons of pasta water and allow the sauce to emulsify slightly.
  6. Add the drained spaghetti to the pan and toss well.
  7. Optionally, stir in the parmesan and egg yolk over a very low heat to make the sauce even creamier.
  8. Serve immediately.
