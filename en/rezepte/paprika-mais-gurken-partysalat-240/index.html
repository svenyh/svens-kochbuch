Pepper, corn and cucumber party salad

4-6 people · Salads & Side Dishes / Fast Food

A quick, fresh party salad with a creamy dressing. Perfect as a side dish for grilled dishes or for buffets.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Brewing time: approx. 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 peppers
  • 1 cucumber
  • 1 can of corn
  • 1 small red onion
  • 150 g sour cream
  • 100g yogurt
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • some lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley

Instructions

  1. Chop the peppers, cucumber and onion into small pieces and drain the corn.
  2. Mix the sour cream, yogurt, mustard, salt, pepper and a little lemon juice into a dressing.
  3. Mix everything in a bowl.
  4. Season with parsley.
  5. Let it steep briefly and serve.
