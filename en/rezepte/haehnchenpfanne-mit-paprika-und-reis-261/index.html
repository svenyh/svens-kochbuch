Chicken stir-fry with peppers and rice

For 3-4 people · Quick cuisine / stir-fry dishes

A quick, creamy chicken pan with peppers and rice. Good for an uncomplicated evening after work.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken breast
  • 2 peppers
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 200g rice
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 200 ml cream or cooking cream
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Sweet paprika powder

Instructions

  1. Cook the rice according to the package instructions.
  2. Cut chicken breast into strips.
  3. Chop the peppers and onions into small pieces.
  4. Heat oil in a large pan.
  5. Fry the chicken vigorously and remove it.
  6. Sauté onion, garlic and peppers in the same pan.
  7. Briefly roast the tomato paste.
  8. Pour in the cream and season.
  9. Add the chicken again and let it simmer briefly.
  10. Serve with the rice.
