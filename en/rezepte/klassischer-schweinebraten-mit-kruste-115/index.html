Classic roast pork with crust

4-6 people · Oven dishes / classics

A classic crusted pork roast is just the kind of Sunday meal that instantly tastes like family and the holiday. Crispy on the outside, juicy on the inside and with a strong sauce, this is a real classic, grandma's style.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Roasting: approx. 90 minutes at 180 °C top/bottom heat
  • Finalize the crust: 5-10 minutes at 220°C or grill function
  • Rest time: 10 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 2 hours

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg roast pork with rind
  • 2 onions
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 piece of celery
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp cumin
  • 500 ml broth
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut the rind into a diamond shape and season generously with salt.
  2. Roughly chop vegetables and place in a roasting pan. Place the roast on top.
  3. Season with caraway and pepper and add a little oil over it.
  4. Roast in the preheated oven at 180 °C top/bottom heat for about 90 minutes, adding broth every now and then.
  5. Finally, increase the temperature to 220 °C or switch on the grill for 5 to 10 minutes so that the crust pops up.
  6. Let the roast rest for 10 minutes before cutting and season the sauce separately.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

This goes well with dumplings, red cabbage or sauerkraut.
