Burrito bowl

4 people · Bowls

Everything in one bowl: rice, chicken, beans, corn and avocado – the popular Tex-Mex trend to build yourself.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 30 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • rice
  • chicken
  • sweetcorn
  • beans
  • tomatoes
  • avocado

Instructions

  1. Prepare the ingredients.
  2. Arrange in bowls.
  3. Serve with lime.
