Plum cake with sprinkles

For 1 tray or springform pan · Baking & Sweets / Cakes

A classic plum cake with yeast dough and cinnamon sprinkles. Particularly good in late summer and autumn.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 25 minutes
  • Walking time: approx. 45-60 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 30-35 minutes at 180°C

Ingredients

  • 500g flour
  • 1 cube of yeast
  • 250 ml milk
  • 80g sugar
  • 80g butter
  • 1 egg
  • 800 g plums
  • 200 g flour for the crumbles
  • 120 g sugar for the sprinkles
  • 120 g butter for the crumbles
  • 1 tsp cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Prepare a yeast dough and let it rise.
  2. Wash, stone and halve the plums.
  3. For the streusel, knead the flour, sugar, butter and cinnamon.
  4. Press the yeast dough onto the tray.
  5. Distribute the plums tightly on top.
  6. Pour the sprinkles on top.
  7. Bake in the oven until golden brown.
  8. Let cool and serve.
