Butter cake from the tray

For 1 tray · Baking & Sweets / Sheet Cakes

A simple yeast cake from a tray with butter and sugar. Classic, uncomplicated and very popular with coffee.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Walking time: approx. 45-60 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 20-25 minutes at 180°C

Ingredients

  • 500g flour
  • 1 cube of yeast
  • 250 ml milk
  • 70g sugar
  • 70g butter
  • 1 egg
  • 100 g butter for the topping
  • 80 g sugar for the topping
  • optionally flaked almonds

Instructions

  1. Prepare a yeast dough from the basic ingredients.
  2. Let the dough rise.
  3. Press onto a tray.
  4. Make small depressions with your fingers.
  5. Spread butter on top in small pieces.
  6. Sprinkle sugar and optionally flaked almonds on top.
  7. Bake in the oven until golden brown.
  8. Let cool and serve.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?

Sprinkle sugar and optionally flaked almonds on top.
