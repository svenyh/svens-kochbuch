Königsberger Klopse classic

4 people · Classics & home cooking

Königsberger Klopse are exactly the kind of classics that seem refined and down-to-earth at the same time. The light caper sauce makes the dish special and ensures that it immediately tastes like traditional Sunday cuisine.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Allow the meatballs to steep: approx. 10 minutes
  • Sauce and finish: about 10 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500g mixed minced meat
  • 1 bun
  • 1 egg
  • 1 onion
  • 1 liter broth
  • 40g butter
  • 40g flour
  • 200 ml cream
  • 2-3 tbsp capers
  • lemon juice
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Soak the rolls. Mix the minced meat with the bread roll, egg, onion, salt and pepper and form into balls.
  2. Heat the broth, but do not let it boil, and let the dumplings simmer in it for about 10 minutes.
  3. For the sauce, melt butter, stir in flour and pour in some cooking stock.
  4. Add the cream and capers and season with lemon juice, salt and pepper.
  5. Add the meatballs to the sauce and let them steep gently for 5 minutes.
  6. Serve with potatoes or puree.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?

Serve with boiled potatoes or mashed potatoes.
