Ajvar soup with pide

3-4 people · Soups/after-work meals

This soup is just the right mix of spicy, creamy and uncomplicated. The ajvar immediately brings in depth and warmth, and when paired with soft pide, it becomes a really comforting meal.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 15 minutes
  • Total time: approx. 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 large onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 red pepper
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3-4 tbsp ajvar (mild or spicy)
  • 700 ml vegetable broth
  • 200 ml cream or cooking cream
  • 100 g cream cheese or sour cream
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika powder
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • optional: chili flakes
  • fresh parsley or chives
  • 2 pide breads or pieces of pide for serving

Instructions

  1. Finely dice onion, garlic and peppers.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot and sauté the onion and peppers for 4-5 minutes until soft.
  3. Sauté the garlic briefly, then stir in the tomato paste and ajvar.
  4. Deglaze with vegetable broth and let everything simmer gently for 10 minutes.
  5. Stir in the cream and cream cheese, then puree the soup finely or leave it slightly chunky if desired.
  6. Season with paprika powder, salt, pepper and optionally some chili.
  7. Heat the pide briefly in the oven or in the pan and serve the soup with herbs.

Frequently asked questions

Can I substitute ingredients?
