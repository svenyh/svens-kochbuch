Sarma – Balkans/Croatia

4–6 people · Culinary World Tour / Europe

Stuffed sauerkraut rolls with minced meat – Sven's Balkan take on a classic. Often tastes even better the next day.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 40 minutes
  • Braising time: approx. 90–120 minutes
  • Total: approx. 130–160 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 head sauerkraut leaves
  • 700 g minced meat
  • 100 g rice
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • paprika
  • salt
  • pepper
  • 500 ml stock
  • 200 g bacon
  • tomato paste

Instructions

  1. Mix the minced meat with rice, onion, garlic and spices.
  2. Wrap in cabbage leaves.
  3. Braise with bacon, stock and tomato paste for 90–120 minutes.
