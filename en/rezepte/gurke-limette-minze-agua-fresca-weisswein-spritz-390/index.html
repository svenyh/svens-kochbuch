Cucumber Lime Mint Agua Fresca White Wine Spritz

1 glass · Summer horchatas & aguas frescas

Light summer aperitif: Cucumber Lime Mint Agua Fresca White Wine Spritz. Well-chilled dry white wine and mineral water make a fresh summer spritz.

Timing

  • Prep time: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 120 ml Cucumber Lime Mint Agua Fresca
  • 120 ml well-chilled dry white wine
  • 30–40 ml mineral water
  • Ice cubes
  • cucumber slices, lime slice and mint

Instructions

  1. Chill the base drink (Cucumber Lime Mint Agua Fresca) and alcohol well.
  2. Fill a large wine glass with ice cubes.
  3. Pour 120 ml Cucumber Lime Mint Agua Fresca first.
  4. Add 120 ml white wine and 30–40 ml mineral water.
  5. Stir gently, garnish with matching fruit and serve immediately.
