Grilled vegetable skewers

3-4 people · Grill & BBQ / Side Dishes

Vegetable skewers are at their best from the grill when they don't just come along, but are carefully seasoned and cooked thoroughly. This makes them a real side dish that brings color, bite and freshness to the plate.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Marinate: 10-15 minutes
  • Grilling time: approx. 10-14 minutes over medium heat
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 peppers
  • 1 large zucchini
  • 250 g mushrooms
  • 1 red onion
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Wooden or metal skewers

Instructions

  1. Cut vegetables into similarly sized pieces so that everything cooks evenly.
  2. Mix a marinade with olive oil, salt, pepper, paprika powder and herbs.
  3. Mix the vegetables with it and let it steep for 10 to 15 minutes.
  4. Preheat the grill to medium heat and thread the vegetables onto skewers or spread them in a grill tray.
  5. Grill the skewers for 10 to 14 minutes, turning several times, until the vegetables are soft and lightly browned.
  6. Finally, refine with a little lemon juice or herb butter if desired.
  7. Serve hot as a side dish or with dip and bread.
