Loaded potato casserole

4 people · Potato dishes

Loaded potato casserole stands for creamy potatoes, bacon and melted cheese in a particularly hearty combination. Just the thing if you want it to be down-to-earth and filling.

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg waxy potatoes
  • 150 g bacon cubes
  • 1 onion
  • 200 g sour cream
  • 150 ml cream
  • 150g grated Cheddar or Gouda
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1 tbsp butter for the mold
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Peel the potatoes, cut them into slices and pre-boil them in salted water for 8 minutes. Pour off.
  2. Leave the bacon cubes in a pan until crispy. Sauté the finely diced onion briefly.
  3. Mix the sour cream, cream, paprika powder, salt and pepper.
  4. Grease a baking dish with butter. Layer potatoes, bacon-onion mixture and sour cream mixture.
  5. Sprinkle with grated cheese and bake at 190 °C top/bottom heat for 30 to 35 minutes.
  6. Sprinkle with finely chopped spring onions before serving.
