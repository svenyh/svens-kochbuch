Mashed potatoes with dill

3-4 people · Side dishes / potatoes

This variant is a little fresher than the classic pound and that's exactly why it's so practical. The dill adds lightness without the dish losing its down-to-earth character.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 10 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 kg floury potatoes
  • 50g butter
  • 120-150 ml warm milk
  • 2-3 tbsp fresh dill, finely chopped
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Peel the potatoes, cut them into pieces and boil them in salted water until soft.
  2. Drain and allow to evaporate briefly.
  3. Add butter and mash the potatoes roughly.
  4. Mix with warm milk until creamy.
  5. Finally, fold in the dill and season with salt and a little pepper.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it keep?

Add the dill at the end to keep it fresh.
