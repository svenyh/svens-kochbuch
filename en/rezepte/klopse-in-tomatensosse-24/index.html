Dumplings in tomato sauce

4 people · Classics & home cooking

Dumplings in tomato sauce are an uncomplicated everyday dish that cooks quickly and still looks like real food. It is crucial that the dumplings remain loose and that the tomato sauce is given enough time so that it tastes more rounded.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 20 minutes
  • Total: approx. 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500g minced meat
  • 1 egg
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 tbsp breadcrumbs
  • 1 can of diced tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Paprika powder

Instructions

  1. Mix the minced meat with egg, finely diced onion, breadcrumbs, salt, pepper and a little paprika powder.
  2. Form small balls.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and briefly fry the dumplings all over.
  4. Finely chop the garlic and sauté briefly.
  5. Add tomato paste and chopped tomatoes.
  6. Season the sauce with salt, pepper and paprika powder.
  7. Let the meatballs simmer in the tomato sauce over medium heat for 15-20 minutes.
  8. Season again before serving.
