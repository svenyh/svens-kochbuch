Hearty tortellini salad

4-5 people · Salads & Side Dishes / Fast Food

A spicy tortellini salad for barbecues or take-away parties. Quick to make, filling and easy to prepare.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 20 minutes
  • Cooking time: approx. 5-8 minutes
  • Brewing time: approx. 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g fresh tortellini
  • 150 g cooked ham
  • 1 pepper
  • 1 small cucumber
  • 1 small red onion
  • 150 g cocktail tomatoes
  • 150g yogurt
  • 100g mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • some parsley

Instructions

  1. Cook tortellini according to package instructions and allow to cool.
  2. Chop the ham, bell pepper, cucumber, onion and tomatoes into small pieces.
  3. For the dressing, mix yogurt, mayonnaise, mustard, salt and pepper.
  4. Mix everything together in a large bowl.
  5. Sprinkle with parsley and let it steep briefly.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this ahead?

Good to prepare the day before.
