Patatas Bravas

4 people · Side dishes / Spain

Crispy oven potatoes with a spicy bravas sauce – a Spanish tapas classic simplified by Sven for everyday cooking. Punchy, hearty and popular with young and old.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Roasting time: approx. 35 minutes
  • Total: approx. 50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 kg potatoes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • salt
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 400 g passata
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • a pinch of cayenne

Instructions

  1. Peel, dice and toss the potatoes with oil and salt.
  2. Roast at 200 °C for approx. 35 minutes until golden.
  3. For the sauce, sauté the onion and garlic.
  4. Add passata, sugar, paprika and cayenne.
  5. Simmer the sauce for 10 minutes and serve with the potatoes.
