spaghetti bolognese

4 people · Italian inspired cuisine/pasta

Spaghetti Bolognese is one of those dishes that almost always works and is enjoyed by many people. A rich meat sauce, cleanly cooked pasta and little effort make this recipe a sure-fire everyday favorite.

Ingredients

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 500 g minced beef
  • 1 onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 stick of celery
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 800 g diced tomatoes
  • 150 ml red wine or 100 ml stock
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • 50g Parmesan

Instructions

  1. Dice the onion, carrots, celery and garlic very finely.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pot and fry the minced meat vigorously.
  3. Add vegetables and fry for 5 minutes. Stir in tomato paste and roast briefly.
  4. Deglaze with red wine or broth. Add chopped tomatoes.
  5. Season with sugar, oregano, salt and pepper and simmer gently for 30 minutes.
  6. Cook the spaghetti in salted water until al dente, drain and serve with the sauce.
  7. Sprinkle with Parmesan.

Frequently asked questions

What side dish goes well?
