White asparagus in butter and lemon foil

2-3 people · Grill & BBQ / asparagus & seasonal cuisine

White asparagus in foil stays juicy and absorbs butter and lemon particularly well. This creates an elegant, but still uncomplicated side dish from the grill that goes very well with meat or fish.

Timing

  • Prep: 10-15 minutes
  • Grilling time in foil: 15-20 minutes over medium indirect heat
  • Total: approx. 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500-800g white asparagus
  • 40g butter
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 lemon
  • Salt
  • optional: some freshly chopped parsley

Instructions

  1. Peel the white asparagus thoroughly and cut off the ends. This is important so that it doesn't remain fibrous later.
  2. Prepare a large piece of aluminum foil and place the asparagus in the middle.
  3. Spread butter on top in small pieces, season with sugar, a little salt and lemon juice.
  4. Close the foil tightly so that a well-closed package is created.
  5. Grill the package over medium indirect heat for about 15 to 20 minutes until the asparagus is soft but still holds its shape.
  6. Open carefully, sprinkle with a little parsley if necessary and serve immediately.
