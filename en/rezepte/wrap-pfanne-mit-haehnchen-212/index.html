Stir fry

Wrap pan with chicken

4 people · Stir fry

The chicken wrap is quick, juicy and pleasantly uncomplicated. It combines familiar ingredients with a modern idea without appearing artificially trendy.

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast fillet
  • 4 wheat wraps
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 onion
  • 150g corn
  • 150 ml cream
  • 100 ml pureed tomatoes
  • 80g grated cheese
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 teaspoon paprika powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin, optional
  • Salt
  • pepper

Instructions

  1. Chop the chicken, peppers and onion into small pieces. Cut wraps into wide strips.
  2. Heat oil in a large pan and brown the chicken all over.
  3. Add the peppers and onion and fry for 4 minutes. Fold in the corn.
  4. Pour in the cream and pureed tomatoes, season with paprika powder, optionally cumin, salt and pepper.
  5. Allow the sauce to simmer slightly for 3 minutes. Fold in the wrap strips and cheese.
  6. Let simmer over low heat for another 2 minutes until the wraps are soft and juicy.

Frequently asked questions
