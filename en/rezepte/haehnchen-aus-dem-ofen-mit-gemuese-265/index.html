Oven chicken with vegetables

For 3 people · Quick kitchen / oven dishes

An uncomplicated oven dish with chicken, potatoes and vegetables. Everything on one sheet and suitable for everyday use.

Timing

  • Preparation: approx. 15 minutes
  • Baking time: approx. 45-50 minutes at 200°C

Ingredients

  • 3 chicken legs or 500 g chicken thighs
  • 800g potatoes
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 pepper
  • 1 large onion
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • pepper
  • Paprika powder
  • Thyme or marjoram

Instructions

  1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into pieces.
  2. Also chop vegetables roughly.
  3. Mix everything with oil and spices.
  4. Place on a baking sheet or in a large mold.
  5. Season the chicken and place on top.
  6. Cook in the oven until the chicken and vegetables are nicely browned.
  7. Let it rest briefly and serve.
