Strawberry Horchata with Rice Milk Rosé Spritz

1 glass · Summer horchatas & aguas frescas

Light summer aperitif: Strawberry Horchata with Rice Milk Rosé Spritz. Dry rosé and sparkling mineral water turn the base drink into a light terrace aperitif.

Timing

  • Prep time: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 120 ml Strawberry Horchata with Rice Milk
  • 120 ml well-chilled dry rosé
  • 30–40 ml mineral water
  • Ice cubes
  • strawberry slices and fresh mint

Instructions

  1. Chill the base drink (Strawberry Horchata with Rice Milk) and alcohol well.
  2. Fill a large wine glass with ice cubes.
  3. Pour 120 ml Strawberry Horchata with Rice Milk first.
  4. Add 120 ml rosé and 30–40 ml mineral water.
  5. Stir gently, garnish with matching fruit and serve immediately.
